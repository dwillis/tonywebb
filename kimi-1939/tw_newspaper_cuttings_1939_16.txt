OUT IN FOURTEEN
OVERS

Queen's Meet a Bowler
of Merit

The Bedford Town second eleven scored
a runaway victory on Saturday when they
defeated Queen's Works by 156 runs.
Thanks to a fine bowling feat by Humphreys, who captured eight wickets for
25 runs, the Works were dismissed for the
extremely low total of 49 runs. Thirty-two
of these were contributed by two batsmen, King and Cork, who were the only
two to reach double figures. Queen's
innings lasted only fourteen overs. They
had only four regular first team players.
Scores:--
Bedford Town 2nd XI: V. J. Harris c
