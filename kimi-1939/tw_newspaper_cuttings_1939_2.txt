BEDFORDSHIRE STANDARD: FRIDAY 14 JULY 1939-2

MODERN SCHOOL'S BIG
SCORE

NORTHANTS. AMATEURS FORCE A
DRAW

The Modern School piled up a big score
against Northants. Amateurs on the School
field on Wednesday, declaring with 215 for
5 wickets. The main contributors were K.
C. C. Campin 50 not out, D. R. Goodwin
(40), G. Harrison (38), and D. E. Farr (37).
The Amateurs failed, with the exception of
C. J. Longland, who opened the innings and
scored 104 not out. When stumps were
drawn they had scored 154 for 7, and the
game was left drawn. Scores:--

CLOPHILL AVENGE A DEFEAT

MARGIN OF 25 OVER AMPTHILL

Clophill avenged their previous defeat by
Ampthill by winning the return game in
Ampthill Park on Saturday. J. Taylor
figured largely for the visitors, and credit for
his side's victory was chiefly due to him for
his score of 26 runs and also for taking 7 wickets for 32 runs. For Ampthill, Pickering
batted well for 13, but with the exception of
the last pair, Sharpe and Penwright, who
made 11 each, none of the other batsmen
could contribute double figures, and the innings closed for 69. When Clophill batted,
J. Taylor scored steadily, and was not dismissed until nearly the end of the game for
his 26. Scores:--
