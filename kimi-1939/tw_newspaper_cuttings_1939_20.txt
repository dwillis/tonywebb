The Chronicle: Friday 4 August 1939

SUTTON'S WIN BY FOUR
WICKETS
----
ASPRO'S SPORTING ACTION
----
SUTTON'S v. ASPRO

Played at Cintra on Saturday, and, after an interesting game, ended in a win for Sutton's by 4 wickets, the winning hit being made in the last over of the match. Sutton's won the toss, and decided to put the Aspro in first. This policy was soon justified, as 3 wickets were down for 30, but then Keable and Blackman made a stand, and, with the tail wagging vigorously, they had made 171 when the last wicket fell. Keable played a valuable innings of 61. Anderson bowled 24 overs for 58 runs and 7 wickets--a very creditable performance. Against good bowling by Lund and Butler, Sutton's found runs difficult to obtain, but Cottrell came along with one of his best innings, and with Sayles (33), H. Bradfield (34 not out) and Croom (22) also playing good knocks, they were able to get the necessary runs for victory. This, however, was only possible by the sporting action of the Aspro side, who fielded during the last quarter of an hour in hard rain. Lund bowled excellently to take 4 wickets for 45, while Cottrell's innings of 63 was a splendid effort.

READING WIN BY FIVE
WICKETS
----
USEFUL HALF-CENTURY BY
NEILAN
----
READING SUNDAY v. CHISWICK PRIORY

Played at Earley on Sunday, and won by Reading by 5 wickets. Chiswick Priory won the toss, and, batting first, lost 2 wickets for 16. H. Sharp (51) and F. Ahl (35) then batted well, adding 78 runs before A. V. Percival bowled Sharp. Fifteen runs later the same bowler had Ahl well caught at cover by Bennett, these 2 wickets being obtained at a personal cost of 6 runs in 5 overs. The visitors' total was 159, and Reading did well on a wicket that was taking spin to pass that total with 5 wickets in hand, K. C. Bennett (30) and H. W. Neilan (56 not out) both batting very well.

FACTORY WIN LOW-SCORING
GAME AT NEWBURY
----
HUNTLEY AND PALMERS v. OLD NEWBURIANS

Apart from Fisher and Pateman, Huntley and Palmers' batsmen put up a very poor show at New-bury on Saturday against the Old Newburians. Huntley and Palmers batted first, and were all out for the poor score of 101 (Fisher 41 not out and Pateman 32), no one else reaching double figures. Thurkill was the most successful bowler with 4 for 28. The home side found runs hard to get against the bowling of Butler and Carver, and were dismissed for 78, H. and P.s winning by 23 runs. Only two of their batsmen managed to reach double figures. Butler took 6 for 37 and Carver 4 for 33.
