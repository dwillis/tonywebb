A. W. BRIERS—R. CROWNSHAW AXIS AGAIN!

WELWYN GARDEN CITY 32.................................ABBOTS LANGLEY 90

ABBOTS LANGLEY'S victory at Welwyn by 58 runs on Sunday was chiefly due to the remarkable bowling of A. W. Briers, who, in the course of seven overs, captured six wickets for nine runs. He was ably supported by R. Crownshaw, who in eight overs secured four wickets for 21 runs.

Abbots Langley batted first, and were soon in difficulties with O'Brien, who bowled magnificently in capturing six wickets for 19 runs. A. W. Briers and C. Sharp saved the situation with scores of 25 and 27 respectively, but even so, a total of 90 appeared insufficient. However, the home batsmen were unable to cope with the Briers—Crownshaw combination, and were dismissed in 54 minutes for a meagre 32. Score:—
