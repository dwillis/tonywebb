CONSISTENT BATTING BY VISITORS

Thanks to a display of consistent batting, in which Simons, Briers, Glenister, and Carlisle particularly shone, Abbots Langley were able to declare at 160 for eight wickets in their match with the Old Albanians at St. Albans. The home team, however, was never in danger of defeat, Griffiths and George, the opening pair, batting confidently, especially the former, whose 61 not out was an enterprising effort. Fenton, of the Old Albanians, was the only outstanding bowler, capturing five wickets for 36 runs in 12 overs. Score:
