FIVE WICKETS FOR FIVE RUNS!

Twyning gave an excellent display on
Saturday by beating the visiting side,
Corse Lawn, by three wickets and 58 runs.
Twyning batted first and knocked up 102
for seven, of which S. Healey contributed
51 not out He was given good support by
S. Schofield (13), A. Clements (19), and G.
Hone (10). In spite of the considerable
total, however, F. Cole (Corse Lawn)
managed to take four wickets for only 20
runs, while L. Dudfield, W. Spry and O.
Johnson captured one each for 15, 29, and
54 respectively. Corse Lawn had a chance
of victory when O. Hartland scored 13, and
F. Cole 16. But they failed to keep it up,
and gave way under the pressure of S.
Healey's excellent bowling. Corse Lawn was
eventually dismissed for 44. S. Healey
claimed five wickets for one run each, and
C. Merrick two wickets for two runs. A.
Schofield had two for seven, and A. Sowden
one for 11.

MATCHES CANCELLED

The game arranged between Tewkesbury
"A" and Evesham "A" was cancelled
owing to Tewkesbury's ground being unfit
due to Friday's heavy rain.
The match between Longdon and Moore
Cricket Club was cancelled owing to the
latter being unable to raise a team.

CAPTAIN'S TEAM BEATEN

Bushley commenced its season on Saturday with a match between the captain's
(E. E. Bisley) eleven and the sub-captain's
(C. Walker) team, and after a pleasant and
exciting game resulted in the latter team
(although a man short) winning by one
wicket and five runs. The captain's team
went in first, and half the side was out for
33. S. Hunt was in good form, however,
and contributing 22 (including a 6 and two
4's). The last man (J. W. Day) scored a
valuable 14, and the team was dismissed for
85. C. Fowler was in good form with the
ball, and captured the first four wickets
for 15 runs; F. Mayo had three for 12; and
P. Townsend three for eight. P. Hart and
W. Barnes opened the account for the subcaptain's team, and thanks to the latter,
32 was on the board before a separation
was effected. Thirty additional runs were
on the score before the next wicket fell,
and the fielding side looked like being
beaten very badly. However, a slump took
place and the following six wickets added
28 only. But this was sufficient to gain victory. Barnes contributed 40 not out, including five 4's: and J. F. Fowler scored 17. E.
E. Bisley, for the captain's eleven, captured
four wickets for 23, and J. W. Day, three
for seven.

OVERBURY BEATEN AT HOME

Overbury and Buckland and Laverton
opened their season on Saturday when they
met at Overbury, the visitors winning by
12 runs. Batting first, Buckland knocked
up 73, G. Hacklett hitting a useful 38.
Overbury could only muster 61 in reply
despite a stand of 22 by W. Powell. For
Overbury, A. Perks captured four for nine
and E. Stanley four for 32, the Buckland
bowlers being R. Bryant (4 for 21), H.
Green (4 for 26), and J. Collis (2 for 12).

RIPPLE WIN BY NINE RUNS.

Ripple entertained Worcester Nomads on
Saturday, and in a low-scoring match, won
by nine runs. It was definitely a bowler's
match, as is revealed by the analyses.

Ripple gathered 44 runs, to which J. Duncan contributed 14. Of the other players,
only one failed to score, and thus the runs
were fairly evenly divided.

For the Nomads, R. Short and R. Griffiths
took four wickets each for 17 and 25 respectively.

W. Willshan made a determined effort to
give his team victory, and had scored 17
when he was bowled by E. Butler. Apart
from this the batting of the visitors was
poor, and the account was closed with 36
only on the board.

F. Devereaux was the chief cause of this
debacle, as he captured four wickets for
only five runs. Butler took four for 15, and
F. Page two for 13.

HASFIELD AND TIRLEY LOSE FIRST
GAME

Hasfield and Tirley opened their season
on Saturday by being defeated at Norton by
one wicket and 19 runs.

The visitors, who took first knock, began
moderately well, T. Dudfield and D. Barnes
scoring 19 between them. The scoring rather
sagged after this, and five wickets were down
for only 27 runs. L. Bond contributed
another ten, and on the remaining batsmen
being out, the visitors closed their innings
with a total of 44 only.

Norton's victory was in no small measure
due to the 23 runs contributed by A. Bradshaw, because no other player with the exception of A. Maycott (12 not out) reached
double figures. The issue was put beyond
all doubt with three wickets in hand, and
the Norton score closed at 63 for nine.

For Norton, F. Wheeler had the splendid
analysis of six wickets for 10, L. Gardiner
capturing the remaining four for 14 runs.

No fewer than seven bowlers were tried by
the visitors, of whom G. Didcote was most
successful with four wickets for nine; J. W.
Bartlett had three for 12, and W. Steward
and T. Dudfield one each for eight and nine
respectively.

FORTHAMPTON C.C.

As it was found impossible, owing to lack
of players, to carry on Forthampton Cricket
Club this season, the fixtures have been cancelled. At a meeting of members on Wednesday evening, an inventory of the available cricketing materials was made, with a
view to their being disposed of, but it was
stated the club was only in abeyance, and
it was hoped that it would be re-started
in the future. Meanwhile, it was decided
that the assets of the club should be invested in the hands of one or more trustees, so that, if revived, it would have a
sound financial basis.
