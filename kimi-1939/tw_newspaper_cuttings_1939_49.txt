DRAWN GAME AT HARPENDEN.

Harpenden second team's match
with Markyate was left drawn.
F. C. Prescott (53) and A. R.
Haswell (37) gave Harpenden a
good start. A. Howell (29) also
played well and Harpenden declared
with 155 for 5. Markyate, left
with 105 minutes in which to get
the runs, relied on defence, with
the result that the game became
very slow and ended in a draw,
Betts (25) and Nelsey (22) being

Leavesden Hospital put up a
capital performance to beat Three
Counties Hospital, at Aldersy, by 87
runs. They dismissed Three Counties
for 89 (T. Short 20). E. Mar-
tin and Wells 5 for 37. Leavesden won
the match with six wickets in hand
and totalled 176 (J. Hook 42, G.
Wells 27), eight members of the
side reaching double figures.
