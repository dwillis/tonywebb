Hatfield Hyde were well beaten at Offley, on Saturday, when the home team succeeded by 28 runs. Offley declared with 162 for 6. Wilcox, who retired after making 57. Harper (42 not out) and Nergus (36) all batting well. Odell (2-- 29), Hulks (1--23), Ivory (1--38) and Williams (1--54) were the Hat- field Hyde bowlers to capture wickets. Williams batted splendidly to take 61 for the Hyde, who replied with 134. Picken (33) and Ivory (15), being the only other players to reach double figures against the bowling of Harper (5-- 34), Halliwell (3--42) and Wilcox (2--22).

All clean bowled, Hatfield Hyde lost to Graveley by ten runs. They made 83 (A. Charnock 27, T. Jonns 25, M. Nuttock 11) and Graveley replied with 93 (H. Davies 20), D. Turnbull (4--51), R. Gib- son 4--49 and G. Hulks (1--12) taking the wickets.

Hitchin Wednesday 202 (E. K. Norgan 70, G. Briars 42) beat Welwyn Stores 176 (A. Robson 38, L. Owens 25, H. Oughton 19, H. Hall 19, S. Silverthorne 15) by 26 runs. G. Smith (5--40) bowling steadily for Hitchin.

London Colney (55) beat Ber- nard's Heath (41) by fourteen runs in a low-scoring game, at London Colney, on Sunday. O. Eaton (14) was the only player to reach double figures for London Colney and W. Sheraton (9 not out) was top scorer for the Heath. H. Eames (3--26), R. Prior (3--9), G. Eames (3--26) and S. Scales (1--3) took the wickets for the winners, while G. Prior (6--20) and G.
