Weston-super-Mare v. Whitchurch
Heath (Cardiff)

Entertaining Whitchurch Heath, a Car-
diff team. yesterday. Weston-super-Mare
just failed to force a win. B. O. Allen.
the Gloucestershire amateur. played a
brilliant innings for Weston. hitting 72 in
37 minutes including three sixes and
eight fours. Scores:—
