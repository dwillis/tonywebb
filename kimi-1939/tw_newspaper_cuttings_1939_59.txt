hamstedians a good start, but after
they had been separated wickets fell
quickly, the ninth falling with the
Old Boys were 42 in arrears. Filoot

ST. ALBANS' FINE VICTORY AT
LUTON.

St. Albans brought off a capital
performance on Saturday when they
beat Luton by twenty-one runs at
Wardown Park.
St. Albans batted first, but A. S.
Hacker (21?) and S. H. Rabone
(46) were the only players to reach
double figures against good bowl-
ing, particularly by R. B. Webb
(7—29). St. Albans had eight men
out for 64, but J. Holt (3 not out)
played very steadily to keep his end
up while S. H. Rabone added 23 for
the ninth wicket.
Luton lost both their opening bats-
men for nine. With F. Minsbull (3
for 9) and J. Holt (2—27) and S. H.
Rabone (1—10) all bowling effec-
tively five wickets were down for 36
and seven men were out for 51.
A. J. Warboys aided by C. J. Parr-

ridge tried hard to pull the game
round. When they had raised the
score to 70, Warboys, who had made
36, was caught by Weatherhead off
Lismore (3—10), who finished off
the innings for the addition of three
runs by taking a return from Webb
and getting Emery lbw. Scores:—

TWO "HAT TRICKS" AT
BEECH BOTTOM.

second team by seven wickets at

Wright (5—1) and F. Iles (5—13)
