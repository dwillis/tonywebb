HARPENDEN WEDNESDAY'S
ESCAPE.

Harpenden Wednesday's home

WEDNESDAYS' WIN ON
SUNDAY!

Sandridge again disappointed their
supporters on Sunday by losing their
home game with Radlett Wednesday
by 44 runs.

Dismissed in ten overs for 32, no
member of the Sandridge team
reached double figures, Chaplin (9)
and Boon (8) being top scorers.
Radlett scored 26 of the necessary
runs before the first wicket fell,
Sampson going on first change then
secured 3 wickets for 6 runs, finishing up with 4 for 12. W. Lamb
subsequently hit out, and he and
Hemsey added 21 for the last
wicket. The fact that two Sandridge players—one their principal
run-getter and the other who was
holding up his end nicely were
run out had disastrous effect on the
side, but the bowling of Brennan
was exceptionally good, as his
figures, 5—13, show. Scores:—

A SETBACK FOR ELSTREE.

Elstree lost unexpectedly to the
Old Johnian's on Saturday, F. Evans
(6—32) was in great form with the
ball and the Old Boys, who batted
first, had a disastrous time. The
first wicket fell at 11 and seven
men were out for 14. R. Lumley
(10) helped P. Keston (36) to stop
the rot, but the eighth wicket fell
at 24. J. Stich (15) and Keston
took the total to 48 for 9, and J.
Leach, who made one not out, kept
his end up while Keston lifted the
total to 74 before being bowled by
J. Milner.
W. H. Ridout (22) and J. Milner
(10) put on 30 in an opening partnership for Elstree, but things went
badly after that and five men were
out for 39. A. Wilshire (15) lifted
the score to 54 for 6, but D. Kerrane (6—37) and C. Leigh (4—17)
got on top again and the innings
closed for 62. Scores:—
