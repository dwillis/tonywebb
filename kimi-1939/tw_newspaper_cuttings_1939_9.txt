BEDFORDSHIRE TIMES: FRIDAY 11 AUGUST 1939-6.

BOWLING PARKERS

Help Pulloxhill Along to Victory

Pulloxhill played Mr. H. Westrope's XI at Pulloxhill on August Monday, the match resulting in a win for Pulloxhill by 52 runs. Mr. H. Westrope's eleven batted first, and after losing a wicket at three the captain and K. Marshall took the score to 15. From then on wickets fell fast against some good bowling by R. F. Parker (seven for 12) and H. F. Parker (three for 15), and they were all out for 29.

Pulloxhill suffered an early shock, for W. S. Dollimore was caught at the wicket off the second ball of the innings. E. Ashton and E. H. Parker took the score to 12 before the former was bowled, and then B. Jellis helped the latter to add 4 and was caught. E. H. Parker, after scoring 17, left one run later. A fifth-wicket partnership between R. F. Parker and Ashton saw their opponents' total passed and contributions from the "tail" batsmen helped to make a total of 81.

A VALUABLE STAND

Bedford Seconds Lively against Royston

The Bedford Town second eleven scored an easy victory at Royston on Monday, defeating the home team by 130 runs to 36. P. H. Humphreys was mainly instrumental in dismissing the home side so cheaply, his six wickets costing him only seven runs. Sleightholm captured the remaining four wickets for 24 runs.

Bedford batted first and lost two quick wickets, Ferriman and Humphreys being out with only two runs on the board. Gompertz followed soon afterwards, but a stand by Howard and Ward added 41 runs for the fourth wicket. Howard hit five fours and two sixes, and Ward and Sleightholm also hit a six each. However, the last four batsmen added only 27 runs, and the innings closed at 180.

Sleightholm took a wicket in his second over, and after that wickets fell in quick succession and the Royston innings ended at 36. They fared better in a second innings, scoring 78 for four wickets. The scores were:
