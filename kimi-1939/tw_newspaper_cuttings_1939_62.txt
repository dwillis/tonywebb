LEAVESDEN WHACK.
WEMBLEY WANDERERS.

Leavesden beat Wembley Wanderers by 119 runs at Leavesden on Saturday. Ward (5—20), Wells (4—13) and Watson (1—1) dismissed the Wanderers for 44. Leavesden passed that total with seven wickets in hand. R. Burke, who batted splendidly, appeared to be well on the way to a century, but at 89 fell to a brilliant catch by H. Gee in the long field. A. PALMER'S FINE BOWLING
FOR MARKYATE.

Owing to holidays, Markyate
fielded a weakened team on Satur-
day when they visited Westbourne
at Luton and lost by 21 runs.
H. Norris (27 not out) and Archer
(19) were the only Markyate players
to reach double figures. After their
partnership had been ended with the
score at 44 for 2, a collapse set in
and the innings closed for 69.
Thanks to a fine innings by A.
Vaas (46), Westbourne passed the
Markyate total with eight wickets in
hand, but bowlers got on top after
that. A. Palmer having two inspired
overs in which he claimed five vic-
tims, all clean bowled, without
conceding a run. He had the final
analysis of 5 for 8, and it seemed
that had he been brought back
earlier, Markyate might have won.
