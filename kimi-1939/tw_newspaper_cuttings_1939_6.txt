Thurleigh v. Cople

Thurleigh won a very pleasant game
at home on Saturday by 26 runs. J. C.
Thorne (6 for 19) and R. G. Thorne (3 for
4), for the home team, and E. Hillyard (5
for 21) for the visitors, bowled well. In
the second innings Thurleigh made 41,
three men being run out. Cople replied
with 43 for five made in twenty minutes.
