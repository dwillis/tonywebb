SANDRIDGE BEAT REFEREES.

Two veterans figured prominently
in a low-scoring game at Sandridge,
where the Mid Herts Referees lost by
24 runs on Saturday.
Batting first the Referees were
dismissed for 35. A. C. Sampson
obtaining 4 for 8. Winch 2 for 6.
Atkins 1 for 0 and Caustin 1 for 9.
This score should have been easy
for Sandridge, but they found
Eames bowling his breaks with his
old-time accuracy and seven wickets were down for 21. Caustin (37
not out) and Sampson, however,
added 19 for the eighth wicket and
Caustin afterward hit out, obtaining 16 in one over, and the innings
closed for 59. Clay (17) played a
sound defensive innings for the
Referees. Scores:--

TOWNLEY AND SCOTT TO THE
RESCUE.

King's Langley beat Berkhamsted
by 25 runs at King's Langley on
Saturday, which was a very creditable performance after an indifferent start. Eight wickets were down
for 39, but then J. F. Scott (33)
joined W. J. Townley, who carried
his bat for 49, and the total
reached 120. So far this season,
Townley has made 584 runs, including two centuries, for King's Langley. B. W. Atkins (4--20), J. F.
Scott (3--35) and Edmonds (2--
29) dismissed Berkhamsted for 87.
