WEST HERTS AND WATFORD OBSERVER: FRIDAY 11 AUGUST 1939-2.

HEMPSTEAD GET OPPONENTS OUT TWICE

Wimbledon Crusaders Hemel Hempstead 45 & 64...........(3 wkts.) 150

Although rain delayed the start until nearly 3.30 p.m., Hemel Hempstead found plenty of time in which to comfortably defeat Wimbledon Crusaders on the Heath Park ground on Monday. The visitors, batting first, could only muster 45, Brandon taking six for 15 and Capel four for 26.

Hemel Hempstead started strongly, and 81 runs were on the board before the first wicket fell. The total was taken to 150 for the loss of only two more wickets, Grange contributing a sound 54 before being run out and Wallers hitting 56, including six fours and two sixes, before being caught on the boundary.

Batting a second time, Wimbledon Crusaders fared only slightly better than on the first occasion and were dismissed for 64 runs.

SCRATCHED BUT PLAYED LATER

Rickmansworth 74..............Old Spicers 91

No cricket was possible at Rickmansworth over the Bank Holiday until 4 o'clock on Monday. The match had been scratched at lunch time, but the weather improved, and the captains decided to make a start. The Rickmansworth team was made up at the last minute (as several people had left the ground at lunch time), and they could only find nine batsmen. Old Spicers batted first and made 91, the last wicket putting on 27. Murray hit 2 for 37 and Edmunds bowled well, taking seven for 21. When Rickmansworth batted Marriott played well for 29, and with Chicken had the best stand of the day, but Rickmansworth were all out for 74, losing by 17 runs. Score:
