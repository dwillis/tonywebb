BEDFORDSHIRE TIMES : FRIDAY 25 AUGUST 1939.5.

BEDFORD LEAGUE

Caldecote Make Sure of
Premier Division

Caldecote first eleven, by their big victory over Ickwell on Saturday at Caldecote, became champions of the Premier Division of the Bedford and District League.

Ickwell could score only 24 and Caldecote replied with 132, C. Odell scoring over half a century. A fine display of hitting was given by A. Clark, who got three fours in succession. He scored 39. For Caldecote C. Odell took six for 8 and A. Clark four for 12. Caldecote's record in the League to date is: played 13, won 11, drawn 2, points 24. Scores:—

CENTURY BY SCRIVENER FOR
SHIRE HALL

At Luton on Saturday the Shire Hall won an exciting game against Commer Cars, Luton. Commer Cars batted first and compiled 201 runs, Prigmore taking four wickets for 48 runs. Scrivener and Fowler opened the Shire Hall's innings and at once runs came at a good pace. At 73 Fowler was bowled, but Scrivener continued in excellent style and completed his century without giving a chance.
Scores:—
Commer Cars: 201.
Shire Hall: Scrivener not out 105, Fowler b Rees 27, Davis b Bandy 2, Prigmore c Hodges b Fuller 29, Tookey lbw b Fuller 5, Chissell not out 12, extras 27, total for four wickets 207.
