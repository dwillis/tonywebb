Herts Advertiser: Friday 4 August 1939-5

REDBOURN'S COLLAPSE.
Redbourn suffered another defeat
on Saturday when they lost at
Woodside Park by 62 runs,
because their batting did not come
up to the standard of their bowling
and fielding. J. Claxton (80) hit
hard for Woodside Park, who
declared with 161 for 9. Redbourn soon lost five wickets for 34.
W. H. H. Gurney (11) assisted
C. V. H. Cooper (35) to lift the
score to 66 for 6 and J. R. Collins
(12) helped Cooper to take the
total to 92 for 7, but the eighth
and ninth wickets fell without addition and the innings closed for 99.

BEDMOND WIN AT NASH MILLS.
Bedmond succeeded by three
wickets at Nash Mills, on Saturday.
Nash Mills had a first innings lead
of 18 and declared with 62 for 7
in their second venture, but Bedmond rose to the occasion and
replied with 83 for 7, made in
twenty-five minutes, to win the
game. H. Gentle, of Bedmond, had
to be taken to Hospital to have
stitches inserted in a cut over his

ANOTHER ELSTREE SUCCESS.
Elstree gained another victory on
Sunday, when they beat North
Highgate by 123 runs. Batting
first on a soft wicket Elstree made
226 for 9 before declaring. J.
Kellock was again top scorer with
a hard-hit 60, while A. Lyons,
who batted well, was unfortunate
to miss his half-century. There
was a lively last-wicket stand
between father and son—E. Evans
(22 not out) and M. C. Evans (20
not out). North Highgate never got
on top of the Elstree attack and
were dismissed for 103. Scores:—
