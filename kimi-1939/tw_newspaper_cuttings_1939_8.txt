BEDFORDSHIRE TIMES: FRIDAY 11 AUGUST 1939-5.

DEFEAT OF HENLOW
———

Welwyn's Moderate Total

Good Enough

Henlow lost on Monday to Welwyn Garden City. Welwyn, batting first, were soon in difficulties, losing four wickets for 37. Then Bullock and Howard added 29 for the fifth wicket. After they left the side collapsed and were all out for 83—a good total considering the state of the wicket. It was too big a score for the home side, and with wickets tumbling regularly they could reach only 52. There were three outstanding features. Swain, for Henlow, in taking six for 12, captured his last four wickets with four consecutive balls. Lanning bowled well to return figures of seven for 13. The third feature was the wicket-keeping of Carter, of Welwyn. He stumped three and caught one. Other bowlers were L. Lymn (3 for 28) and J. Reast (3 for 18). Scores:—

AMPTHILL'S SUCCESS

Good bowling was the feature of Ampthill's success when they entertained Wolverton Technical College team in the Park on Monday. With Reg Stearn the highest scorer (38), Ampthill totalled 130 runs. Wolverton were quickly dismissed for a total of 34, and when put in again made 54. In the first innings Taylor captured four wickets for for runs and Aldridge claimed five for 18. In the second innings R. Underwood took three for 7, and R. Manyweathers five for 14. Scores:—

A LOW-SCORING

MATCH
———

Biggleswade Defeated by

Caldecote

After a blank day on Saturday owing to the rain, Biggleswade entertained their neighbours, Caldecote, at Fairfield on Monday. A low-scoring game ended in a win for Caldecote on the first innings by 26 runs. Caldecote's first innings score of 57 was mainly contributed by the cap- tain, D. L. MacLean (26), and E. King (15). A. Giles bowled well and took six wickets for 13 runs. The home batsmen could do little against the good bowling of King and O'Dell, and were soon dis- missed for only 31.

In their second attempt both sides faired slightly better. Caldecote making 65 for six wickets declared, and Biggleswade reaching 44 for seven. Scores:—
