WEST HERTS AND WATFORD OBSERVER: FRIDAY 18 AUGUST 1939-5

CHIPPERFIELD'S TRIUMPH

CHIPPERFIELD 193 .. HEMEL HEMPSTEAD 91

SHARING Hemel Hempstead's wickets evenly, H. Goodman (five for 56) and J. A. Bates (five for 33), accomplished a fine performance for Chipperfield, whose batsmen exceeded the visitors' total by 102 runs.

Hemel Hempstead were in trouble from the start, losing Grange and Purves very cheaply. Wallers and Cruickshank shared in a useful stand, and the Cox..perfield attack had some trouble in dismissing Brandon. After his dismissal, however, they were easy meat, and Hempstead could total only 91.

With Smith in splendid form, and Goodman reaching the half-century, Chipperfield were always on top.      Score:

JUNCTION CENTURY STAND
AGAINST AQUATICS

Watford Junction (6 wkts., dec.) 139 .. Aquatics 89

Played at Shirley House, by kind permission of Mr. F. I. W. Stewart. The first Junction wicket fell at 15, but Hart and Hopkins in a fine stand took the total to 105 before they were parted. The Aquatics fought back gamely, but found the Junction's varied attack too much for them. Donaghue unfortunately badly injured a finger when fielding and could not bat.     Score:

BATTING BREAKDOWN
LED TO HEAVY REVERSE

WEST HERTS 100 .. MILL HILL PARK (4 wkts.) 168

PASSING West Herts poor score of 100 for the loss of only three wickets, Mill Hill Park, at Cassio-road on Saturday, proved much the stronger side. Losing Burgess at five, West Herts were always struggling for runs. Pearson left at 22, and ten runs later Dedman and Mitchell both left at the same total. Eade and Goodall and later Stimpson, offered some resistance, but the last three wickets failed to produce a run.

The wicket did not give the bowlers the slightest assistance, and 100 runs was no compliment to West Herts.

The visitors did not start with any degree of certainty, Hawes bowling Herbert at 18, and Kendrick making a brilliant catch and hitting L. A. Smith's stumps with a yorker claimed two further successes.

Goff and Oatway went steadily on, and when Goff was run out, after completing an enterprising half-century, the total was 126. The West Herts bowlers failed to dislodge Oatway, whose 72 contained six fours.     Score:

W. E. JONES AND B. CROWNSHAW
PUT ON 106

Abbots Langley
Bushey (6 wkts.) 137 .. (8 wkts., dec.) 201

W. E. Jones and B. Crownshaw batted well for Abbots Langley, taking the score from 42 to 147 for the third wicket, the former making 63 and the latter 55, both making good strokes all round the wicket.

Smee bowled steadily for Bushey, and was unfortunate in not getting more wickets.
Bushey, after losing two wickets quickly, made a good recovery. Brockwell, Walker, Golding and Chester showing commendable form, the end coming with Bushey still needing 64 runs with four wickets to fall.     Score:
