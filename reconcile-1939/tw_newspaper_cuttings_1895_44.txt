Stirratt, c Carter, b Fye, 0; H. Hooper, run out, 1; C. Hitchens, not out, 0; extras, 18; total (for seven wickets), 198. E. Aldridge and D. Skinner did not bat.
Clock and Champeny saved Bedminster and robbed Y.M. of victory, although Champeny was dropped when he had scored only 20. Rowlands in great form, 54 not out. Johnson Axe and Davis also batted well. Y.M. only had one and three-quarter hours to get the runs, first 100 coming in just under the hour. They only wanted eight to win when stumps were drawn. It was a really good match and delightful cricket. Y.M. greatly improved all round.
