LAST-WICKET STAND SAVES GAME.

A dogged last-wicket stand by C. J. S. Fltoot and I. W. Houghton saved the Old Berkhamstedians from defeat in their match with the Herts County Club at Bucklands on Friday. W. Brian Smith (48) and R. L. Eade (73) batted well for the County Club. Wilson, who was averaging not much more than three since, and A. M. Olsson (57) gave the Old Berkhamstedians a good start, but after they had been separated wickets fell quickly, the ninth falling when the Old Boys were 42 in arrears. Fltoot and Houghton, however, defended doggedly to save the game. Scores:—

ST. ALBANS' FINE VICTORY AT LUTON.

St. Albans brought off a capital
performance on Saturday when they
beat Luton by twenty-one runs at
Wardown Park.

St. Albans batted first, but A. S. Hacker (219) and S. H. Rabone (43) were the only players to reach double figures against good bowling, particularly by R. B. Webb (7 27). St. Albans had eight men out for 64, but C. J. Holt (3 not out) played very steadily to keep his end up while S. H. Rabone added 23 for the ninth wicket.

Luton lost both their opening batsmen at nine. With F. Minshull (3 - 21), J. Holt (2—27) and S. H. Rabone (1—10) all bowling effectively five wickets were down for 36 and seven men were out for 51. A. J. Burboys aided by C. J. Partridge tried hard to pull the game round. When they had raised the score to 70, Burboys, who had made 36, was caught by Weatherhead off Lismore (3—10), who finished off the innings for the addition of three runs by taking a return from Webb and getting Emery lbw. OLD ALBANIANS' SURPRISING COLLAPSE.

The Old Albanians suffered a sensational one-run defeat on Saturday, when they opposed Shenley Hospital at Porter's Park. Shenley, thanks largely to a good knock of 42 by A. Clarke, totalled 98. M. A. Wiggs (56), A. G. Owen (18) and K. A. Griffiths (16) gave the Old Boys a capital start; indeed they needed only nineteen for victory when the fourth wicket fell, but Brown (4—38) and Woodcock (2—21) brought about a surprising collapse. Eight wickets were down for 99. The ninth lifted the total to 97 and two runs were still required when W. Fenton, the last man, went in, but he was caught by Greenhaugh of Brown without addition to the total and Shenley succeeded, in a tense finish, by one run. Scores:—
