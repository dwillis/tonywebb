STANCOMBE PARK v. SHELL MEX
(Bristol)
In an interesting game at Stancombe Park the home side defeated Shell Mex by 12 runs. Stancombe batted first, but only D. Clark (28), J. S. Hughes (25) and W. Beale (11) eached double figures, the remaining batsmen falling on a difficult wicket. Shell Mex started disastrously. Beale in his opening over hitting the stumps on three occasions. The visitors' middle batsmen batted stubbornly, but their last wicket fell at 66 despite a good knock by Brinton. Scores:—
