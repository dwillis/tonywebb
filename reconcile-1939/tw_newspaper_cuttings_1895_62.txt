A. PALMER'S FINE BOWLING FOR MARKYATE.
Owing to holidays, Markyate fielded a weakened team on Saturday when they visited Westbourne at Luton and lost by 21 runs.
B. Norris (27) and H. Archer (10) were the only Markyate players to reach double figures. After their partnership had been ended with the score at 44 for 2, a collapse set in and the innings closed for 69.
Thanks to a fine innings by A. Vass (46), Westbourne passed the Markyate total with eight wickets in hand, but bowlers got on top after that. A. Palmer having two inspired overs in which he claimed five victims, all clean bowled, without conceding a run. He had the final analysis of 5 for 8, and it seemed that had he been brought back earlier, Markyate might have won.
