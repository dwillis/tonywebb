Hatfield Hyde were well beaten at Dunley, on Sunday, when the home team succeeded by 28 runs. Utley declared with 162 for 6. Gilroy, who retired after making 57, Harper (42 not out) and Negus (36) all batting well. Odell (2—29), Hulks (1—23), Ivory (1—38) and Williams (1—54) were the Hatfield Hyde bowlers to capture wickets. Williams batted splendidly to make 61 for the Hyde, who replied with 134. Bicken (23) and Ivory (13) being the only other players to reach double figures against the bowling of Harper (5—34), Halliwell (3—42) and Wilcox (2—22).
All clean bowled, Hatfield Hyde lost to Graveley by ten runs. They made 83 (A. Charnock 27, T. Jennis 23, M. Mottock 11) and Graveley replied with 93 (H. Davies 20), D. Turnbull (4—31), R. Gibson (4—44) and G. Hulks (1—12) taking the wickets.
