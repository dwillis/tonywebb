WEST HERTS AND WATFORD OBSERVER: FRIDAY 18 AUGUST 1939-3
LORD'S STAFF PLAYER HITS
101 AFTER SHOCKS

STANMORE (8 wkts.) 192 .. LORD'S GROUND STAFF 254

IN half-an-hour Lord's Ground Staff lost three wickets for 35 runs; all fell to K. H. Chapman, who hit the stumps with his first ball. Haycraft offered sterner
resistance, but two wickets fell before lunch, when the score was 110 for five wickets. Haycraft went on to make his century and, supported by the later batsmen, they batted briskly and showed great respect for the bowling of Windham Griffiths, who in 15 overs conceded only 17 runs and bowled out two of the more successful batsmen.
Stanmore were set to score 255 runs in three hours, normally an easy task but here the bowling proved to be too accurate to allow quick scoring with safety, and the game finished in a draw. Score:

ANOTHER DON YATES CENTURY
Hill End Hospital 23 .. (10 wkts., dec.) 156
Don Yates was at it again on Saturday with a fine innings of 108 not out. He and D. Edis (44) scored 125 runs without being separated. When Hill End Hospital went in to bat, they could do nothing against the spin bowling of H. Beach (four for 5) and J. Prescott (four for 4), for they were all out for 25. Score:
