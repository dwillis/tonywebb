THE BUCKS FREE PRESS, FRIDAY, AUGUST 25, 1939.

BUCKS ROUT
NORFOLK

Lund In Deadly Form
Again

WALKER SHINES

AFTER Vic Lund, the Slough bowler, had taken seven wickets for 24 runs to help dismiss Norfolk for only 50 in the first innings, Buckinghamshire gained an easy win in their last Minor Counties match of the season at Ascott Park yesterday and on Wednesday.
Bucks included in their team two new players, J. R. Tovey, 17-years-old Wycombe batsman, and C. G. Boddy, who has already had some experience of county cricket.
Franklin won the toss and took the bold step of putting in his opponents—a policy which proved a wise one. From the start the batsmen were always in difficulties against some splendid bowling by Lund and Battcock, and never recovered from a disastrous start.
Bucks started with Baker and O'Callaghan, and they soon demonstrated that nothing was the matter with the wicket, for they scored with freedom and carried the score to 25, when lunch was taken.
Six wickets were down for 206, and after three more wickets had fallen for one more run, Franklin declared the innings closed with the score at 207 for 9.
About 1½ hours were left for play when Norfolk entered upon their second venture.
When the first three wickets had fallen for 36 runs, it seemed as if the whole side would be out for another small total, but D. C. Rought-Rought and Langdale played out time, the score at the close being 86 for 3.
The game was resumed in a damp and misty atmosphere, the outfield being so wet that towels were in demand by the bowlers, who found great difficulty in holding a slippery ball. Norfolk, who wanted 72 runs to avoid an innings defeat, were out for a total of 202, leaving Bucks to score 46 runs for victory. For some unaccountable reason Bucks' early batsmen failed so badly with five of the best out for 30 that it seemed anybody's game. Macindoe and Battcock then confidently met the bowlers until they had carried the score to the necessary total, providing a rousing finish to the match, which completes the Bucks programme for the season.
