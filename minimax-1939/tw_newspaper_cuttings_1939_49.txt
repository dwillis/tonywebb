**DRAWN GAME AT HARPENDEN.**

Harpenden second team's match with Markyate was left drawn. F. C. Prescott (63) and A. R. Hassell (37) gave Harpenden a good start. A. Howell (29) also played well and Harpenden declared with 155 for 5. Markyate, left with 105 minutes in which to get the runs, relied on defence, with the result that the game became very slow and ended in a draw.
