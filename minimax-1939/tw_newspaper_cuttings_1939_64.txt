**ANOTHER DON YATES CENTURY**

Hill End Hospital 23 (0 wkts., dec.) 156 Odhams

Don Yates was seen at again on Saturday with a fine innings of 108 not out. He and D. Edis (44) scored 139 runs without being separated. When Hill End Hospital went in to bat, they could do nothing against the spin bowling of H. Beach (four for 50 and J. Prescott (four for 44), for they were all out for 23. Score.
