BEDFORDSHIRE TIMES: FRIDAY 18 AUGUST 1939-5.

HARPUR SPORTS WIN
Shire Hall Lose Grip on Close Game

A low-scoring game between Harpur Sports and Shire Hall ended in a narrow victory for Harpur by three runs in Bedford Park. After dismissing Harpur for 100 runs the Shire Hall, thanks to some splendid batting by Scrivener, reached 70 for five wickets. However, the steady bowling of Minns and Barker sent the next four men back for the addition of only eighteen runs. Warren and Furlong added nine runs for the last wicket, the former hitting a six before Barker captured his wicket. Scores:—

A THORN(E) IN STAGSDEN'S SIDE

Thurleigh batted first in their game with Stagsden on Saturday, but after scoring 40 runs for four wickets they were all out for 53. S. Tysoe (four for 19) and J. Almey (three for 12) were Stagsden's most successful bowlers.

A CALDECOTE RECRUIT

Caldecote first eleven return to League warfare to-morrow when they have a local "derby" with their near neighbours, Ickwell. The game is to be played at Caldecote, who will include in their side J. P Maclean, brother of the captain, who rendered such excellent service to the club a season or two ago. Caldecote are now practically certain of the championship of the Premier Division of the Bedford League.
