PINNER HAD FIVE WICKETS TO SPARE

Old Merchant Taylors    Pinner
112 ..... (8 wkts.) 144

Old Merchant Taylors were below strength and batted poorly on a slow wicket on which the ball kept low. H. Harold, this year's school captain, was an exception. The pitch became easier later, though never at any time difficult, and Pinner had no difficulty after losing a few early wickets. Pinner passed the Taylors' score for the loss of five wickets.
