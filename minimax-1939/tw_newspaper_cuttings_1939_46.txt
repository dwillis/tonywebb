WEST HERTS AND WATFORD OBSERVER: FRIDAY 16 JUNE 1939-2.

SHORT RUNS

TURNER AND BAILEY TAKE THE HONOURS
Rickmansworth British Legion 68.......Dickinson's (Croxley) II 129
R. Turner's six for 24, and C. Bailey's 59 were the main instruments in Croxley's victory.

J. ILLINGWORTH'S "HAT TRICK"
North Met. (St. Albans) 87................Bricket Wood and Munden 83
J. Illingworth was put on to bowl too late. He went on with the score at 85; only one more run was added. Illingworth took the last three wickets with the first three balls of his second over. T. Palmer (16), W. Clark (15), and H. Goldup (21) gave Bricket Wood and Munden a good start against a total of 87, but the tail failed, and North Met. just got home by four runs.

ST. JOHN'S EFFECTIVE REPLY
Courtfield (8 wkts. dec.) 158 ...St. John's (7 wkts.) 143
St. John's replied well to Courtfield's declaration, wanting only 16 runs, with four wickets to go when stumps were drawn.

PARENTS TAKE A CANING
Hemel Hempstead Grammar School (8 wkts. dec.) 155............Parents 75
The School easily beat the Parents by 80 runs.

SIX WICKETS WIN OVER RIVAL CLUBMEN
St. Mary's Men's Guild 54 & (4 wkts.) 40....St. Mary's Club 29 & 62
St. Mary's Guild continued their winning ways with a victory over their rival Churchmen, the St. Mary's Boys' Guild.

T. G. ARGILE HITS CENTURY FOR ALDENHAM SCHOOL
Aldenham School (7 wkts. dec.) 247............Merchant Taylors' School (4 wkts.) 117
After Argile (126), Hickson (31), and Scott (34 not out) had taken full toll of the Taylors' bowling, Aldenham left their opponents two hours in which to get the runs. With victory an impossibility, the Taylor's did the next best thing and avoided defeat.

KINGHAM WELL AHEAD OF HARROW
Kingham Institute Athletic 153...........Harrow Athletic 101
Once again fine bowling by Storrow, who took eight for 56, paved the way for a Kingham victory.

34 BALLS, 8 WICKETS, ONE RUN!
Berkhamsted II 29............Chesham II 186
Remarkable bowling by Piested skittled out Berkhamsted II at Berkhamsted. Piested bowled Todd and after Parry had dealt with Haslam and Weatherill who, with Todd, were the only players to score, Piested clean bowled the remaining seven batsmen without addition to the score. Piested bowled 34 balls and took eight wickets for the cost of one run!

WON WITH FEW MINUTES TO SPARE
Leighton Buzzard II (6 wkts.) 168............Tring Park II 155
An exciting match was seen on the Bell Close ground, where Leighton Buzzard entertained Tring and the home side won with only a few minutes to spare.

WRIGHT EQUALS BENSKIN'S TOTAL
Benskin's 60............Garston Willows 126
Wright's 60 for the Willows equalled Benskin's total.

R. T. ARGILE'S FINE ALL-ROUND FORM
Abbots Langley II (10 wkts.) 148............(5 wkts. dec.) 206
Rickmansworth had the best of the game at Abbots Langley. Batting first, they scored 206 for five declared, Argile, hitting hard, scored 105 (16 4's).

SHEARN'S WICKETS FOR HEMPSTEAD GARAGE
Amersham-Hill 81............Hemel Hempstead 98 I.P.T.B. Garage
Played at Amersham on Thursday, the Passenger Transport Board's side won by 17 runs. For Amersham Martin bowled well, taking six for 27, including the "hat trick," and for the Garage Shearn took seven for 54.

BUSHEY H'S GOOD RECORD
Harpenden II 124............Bushey II 134
Bushey II continued their run of success (played 6, won 5, drawn 1) in a very keenly fought game at Harpenden.

S. JORDAN HAS 7 WICKETS FOR 6
Watford British Legion 61......Northbank 18
S. Jordan's seven for 6 was the main reason why Northbank were dismissed for 18.

LEGION'S VICTORY OVER GASMEN
Watford British Legion 72............Watford Gas Co. 49
A. Smith hit 20, and G. Tiechurst had four for 13 in the Legion's victory

NASH MILLS OUT TWICE
Nash Mills 50 & 32............Balham Park 77 & (no wkt.)6
Balham won this first match which, although ending in a comfortable ten wicket win had Turner to thank, making a hurricane 31 (four 6's and one 4)

TURNER ROUTS B.D.V.
Nash Mills 104............B.D.V. 29 & 67
Nash Mills recorded an easy victory over B.D.V. by an innings and eight runs. Turner was in deadly form, capturing six for 15.

SUN OBSCURED BY L.M.S.
L.M.S. Watford 133........Sun Engraying 45
L.M.S. accomplished their best performance so far this season when they defeated the hitherto unbeaten "Sun" team in hollow fashion.

SCAMMELLS FIND A. SAWYER TROUBLESOME
Scammells 48....Rickmansworth Oddfellows 85
Oddfellows won by 37 runs. A. F. Sawyer played another good innings of 42.

CLOSE FINISH IN TABERNACLE TRIUMPH
Tabernacle 59..... ....St. James's Baptists 52
Tabernacle won after an exciting finish. Watson batted soundly and saved what might have been a collapse.

W. W. ARMSTRONG TAKES 100th WICKET

On Wednesday W. W. Armstrong, of Nash Mills, took his 100th wicket of the season. He has played in 15 matches.
