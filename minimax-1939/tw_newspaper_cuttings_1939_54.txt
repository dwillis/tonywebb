WEST HERTS AND WATFORD OBSERVER: FRIDAY 4 AUGUST 1939—4.

**Berkhamsted Wilt Before Leg-Break Barnes**

RICKMANSWORTH 139 ............ BERKHAMSTED 66

BENNETT, a 15-years-old schoolboy, going in first for Rickmansworth, helped Openshaw to put on 59 for the first wicket. These runs later proved invaluable, as wickets began to tumble regularly, especially against Stiling (six for 55).

All out for 139, Rickmansworth's highest scorer was Bennett with 30.

When Berkhamsted faced the home attack they fared even worse, and found the leg breaks of Barnes more than they could master. Barnes had six for 29, and Berkhamsted were all out for 66—73 short of Rickmansworth's total. Score:

**M. A. WIGGS GETS HIS COUNTY CAP**

After the Herts v. Bucks match at St. Albans last week M. A. Wiggs was awarded his county cap. Wiggs, who lives in Watford, attended St. Albans School, and plays regularly for the Old Albanians.

Wiggs is also a county Rugby player and an excellent golfer.

D. C. Kendrick, the West Herts bowler, on Saturday took his 50th wicket of the season.

**K. H. CHAPMAN MADE USE OF STICKY WICKET**

Stanmore (7 wkts.) 97 ............ Radlett 46

Radlett were given first use of a tricky wicket and K. H. Chapman had the new ball. He took 1 wicket with his first ball, and in his first five overs took five wickets without conceding a run. In all he had seven for eight. Set to score 46 runs, Stanmore were soon in difficulties, and lost five wickets before they won the match. T. Down, of Radlett, too, found the conditions favourable to hit, and took six wickets for 23 runs. Score:

**STANMORE WON IN LAST OVER**

Ickenham 125 ... Stanmore (6 wkts., dec.) 261

The wicket at Ickenham, spoilt by rain, was quite lifeless. Ham and Richards together scored 154 for the first wicket, and then all was sacrificed to quick scoring. Flight and spin brought success to Stanmore's bowlers, and the match was won in the last over. Score:
