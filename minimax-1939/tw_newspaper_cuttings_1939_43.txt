WESTERN DAILY PRESS: MONDAY 21 AUGUST 1939-2.

**OPTIMISTS' TOUR**

The Optimists' C.C. played five matches during their tour of Hampshire. Three matches were won and two lost. W. R. Jones was the most successful bowler, taking 34 wickets for 160 runs in 61 overs.

The last three matches resulted as follows:—
