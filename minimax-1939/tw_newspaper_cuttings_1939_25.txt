THE CHRONICLE FRIDAY AUGUST 18 1939

READING'S BATTING FAILS

SIMPSON ONLY ONE TO MAKE STAND AT SLOUGH

READING v. SLOUGH

Played at Slough on Saturday, and drawn. Having dismissed Slough for the moderate total of 125 (J. W. Davis 4 for 23, L. Clinch 4 for 28), and with two hours to bat, Reading seemed to have a good chance of winning the game, but only a sound and patient innings by K. L. Simpson, who was last out for 45, enabled them to avoid defeat. F. Bond took 5 for 43, but it was chiefly the accurate bowling of Lloyd and Power which tied down the Reading batsmen.

READING v. R.A.F. (UXBRIDGE)

Played at Earley on Sunday, and won by Reading by 5 wickets. R.A.F. (Uxbridge) batted first, and Reading did very well to dismiss a strong batting side for 70 runs in 86 minutes, 7 wickets having fallen before lunch for 58 runs. Machray and Percival accounted for the opening pair in their first over, and J. W. Davis (5 overs, 1 maiden, 4 wickets, 6 runs) and C. J. Woollhead (8.5 overs, 1 maiden, 4 wickets, 22 runs) disposed of the rest of the side. Reading started very badly, losing 5 wickets for 39 in 55 minutes, but G. E. Neilan (43) and H. F. Fox (27) batted very well, and passed the R.A.F. total before being parted. Reading declared at 53 p.m. with the score 112 for 9, and R.A.F. (Uxbridge) then showed their batting quality, scoring 144 for 5 in 60 minutes, P. O. J. M. Jewell making 63, including 4 fours and 5 fours. Reading replied with 100 for 7 in 68 minutes, having again started badly by losing 6 for 36. G. E. Neilan (14 not out) and J. W. Davis (54 not out) got the runs this time, and when stumps were drawn Reading were only 3 run short of winning the two innings match.

SUTTON'S v. HUNTLEY AND PALMERS

Played at Cintra on Saturday, and resulted in a draw. Sutton's made a bad start, losing 2 wickets for 13, but Cottrell and Messer then took the score to 104, both being undefeated at the close. Cottrell scored 31 and Messer 58.
