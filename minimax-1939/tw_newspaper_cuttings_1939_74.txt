WEST HERTS AND WATFORD OBSERVER. FRIDAY 13 OCTOBER 1939

*Cricket Averages*

**WEST HERTS BOWLERS BEST**

The bowling figures make the brightest reading in the West Herts averages. Lane easily tops the batting list with an average of 36.81, and is followed by Stimpson, who owes his success to seven not out innings in 16 visits to the wicket. From his 25.11 there is a drop to 22.75 by Goodall for third place.

With 36 wickets at a cost of 12.10, Hawes returns the best bowling average, but he is closely followed by Stimpson (12.40) and Kendrick, who, with 58 wickets, took the most (13.48).

**Cricket Club—Matrimony Agency**

During the past summer no less than three playing members of the Kings Langley Cricket Club were married, each to members of the Club's Ladies' Working Party. They were Mr. D. Baldwin to Miss Collins; Mr. L. Foster to Miss Shapcott, and Mr. A. Waller to Miss Dearman. On Friday at the usual meeting of the Social Section of the club in the Pavilion, Mr. B. W. Atkins, on behalf of the members, made a presentation to each of these couples. Mr. and Mrs. Baldwin and Mr. and Mrs. Foster each received a frameless wall mirror and Mr. and Mrs. Waller an oak-mounted barometer. In each case the husband thanked the members for the gift and good wishes which accompanied it.
