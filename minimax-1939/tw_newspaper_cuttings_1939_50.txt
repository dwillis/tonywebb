Hatfield Hyde were well beaten at Offley, on Saturday, when the home team succeeded by 28 runs. Offley declared with 162 for 6, Wilcox, who retired after making 57, Harper (42 not out) and Negus (36) all batting well. Odell (2-29), Hulks (1-23), Ivory (1-38) and Williams (1-54) were the Hatfield Hyde bowlers to capture wickets. Williams batted splendidly for Hyde, who at the fall of the seventh reply with 134, Picken (23) and Ivory (13) being the only other players to reach double figures against the bowling of Harper (5-54), Halliwell (3-42) and Wilcox (2-25).

All clean bowled, Hatfield Hyde lost to Graveley by ten runs. They made 85 (A. Charnock 27, T. Jennis 23, M. Mottock 11) and Graveley replied with 95 (H. Davies 20), D. Turnbull (4-51), R. Gibson (4-49) and G. Hulks (1-12) taking the wickets.

Hitchin Wednesday 202 (E. K. Norgate 70), beat Welwyn 42.

Welwyn Stores 176 (A. Robson 58, L. Owens 23, H. Oughton 19, H. Hall 19, S. Silverthorne 15) by 26 runs, G. Smith (5-40) bowling steadily for Hitchin.

London Colney (55) beat Bernard's Heath (41) by fourteen runs in a low-scoring game, at London Colney, on Sunday. A. Imison (14) was the only player to reach double figures for London Colney and W. Sheraton (9 not out) was top scorer for the Heath. B. Eames (3-0), R. Prior (3-9), G. Eames (3-26) and S. Scales (1-3) took the wickets for the winners, while G. Prior (5-20) and G. Taylor (3-23) bowled well for Bernard's Heath.

Tyttenhanger beat Watford Villages by 44 runs in a twelve-a-side game, on Sunday. Batting first, they made 82 (P. Childs 22, V. Nicki 20, S. Childs 17) against the bowling of P. Rowe (5-43), P.
