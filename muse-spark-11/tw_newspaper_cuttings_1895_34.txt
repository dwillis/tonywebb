Percival alone, of the visitors who made anything approaching a stand against their deliveries, and the whole side were disposed of for 34. WYCOMBE MARSH F.C.—A general meeting of the above was held on Tuesday, July 30, to consider if they should enter the Junior League, when after some discussion it was proposed and carried. It was decided to adjourn the meeting until August 13th, at the Red Lion, at 8 o'clock. All members are requested to attend, as the business will be election of officers, &c., for the next season.

CHALFONT ST. GILES v. BULAS (LONDON).—The annual match was played between the above, at Chalfont Park, on the 5th, and resulted in a win for the home team, who are to be congratulated, considering the number they had to contend with (21). Sidney Golding played excellent cricket, 32 not out in the first and 34 in the second innings. Cox bowled capitally, taking 16 wickets for 13 runs. The visitors' scoring was very low; Austin bowled fairly well for them. Chalfont made 53 in the first innings and 111 for six wickets in the second. The visitors' twenty-one were all got rid of for 22.

BURNHAM v. POSTAL TELEGRAPH (LONDON).—This match was played at Burnham on Saturday last, when the visitors were defeated by 3 wickets for the second time this season. Burnham batted first, but were all out for 67—Tilbury 11, Santer 16 (not out), and Painter 10. James bowled splendidly, taking 6 for 28 runs, Mockle 3 for 14, and Milner 1 for 10. The Postal team fared better than their opponents, knocking up 68, W. Milner (23) and Bordeau (23) doing best. Baldwin took 6 for 34 and Santer 4 for 16. Going in again, Burnham scored 81 for the loss of 4 wickets and then declared, Baldwin 32 (not out), Mason 20, and Tilbury 11; Hall (19) not out) batted in fine form. Rain, however, stopped further play, and the Telegraph Staff thus were beaten on the first innings. Mockle captured 3 wickets for 36 runs.

TAPLOW STATION v. BRYANSTON-SQUARE—Played on the Station Ground on Saturday, the homesters winning easily. After disposing of the visitors for 33 (F. Smith 13), Taplow scored 69 for 5 wickets when the game was stopped by rain. H. Painter made 15, G. Jones 10 not out, and C. Frost 10. For the winners Fisher obtained 7 wickets for 20 runs, Frost 2 for 1, and J. Wright 1 for 10. For Bryanston Howland (5 for 15 and Garrett (1 for 19) were the successful trundlers.

CULHAM GREEN v. GERRARDS CROSS—This popular match was played on the charming ground at Evolyns. Showery weather somewhat interfered with the game and only one innings each was played. The visitors scored 60; Gerrards Cross put together 117 and won by 48 runs. Captain Ponton made 34 and G. Lewis 38 for the home team. Mrs. Worsley's hospitality to the players was much appreciated.
