CATHEDRAL CHOIR v. ST. PETER PERMON
GATE.—Played at Lower Close on August 20th, and
resulted in a win for the Cathedral Choir by 23 runs.
Corbyn, Smith 1, and Hill played well for the
Cathedral, and Lee and Alcock for the losing side.

St PAUL'S v SAYER ROAD.—Played at Mousehold
on August 17th, and resulted in a win for St. Paul's
by 67 runs. The bowling of the Rev J Calvert and
Lubbock was good. The former captured four
wickets for two runs in his latter five for 9, and B P
Jackson also batted well. For the losers Thurlow and
Lubbock bowled well for the losers. St Paul's—B M
Jackson 6-1, Rev J Calvert 5-31, B E Jackson not out
19-11, G F Hill 3-10 not out, Gilbert Hill 0, H Hill

Boyce 0, Browne 0, Ramshottom 3, Lubbock 5, Payne
2, Burton 2, Gibbs 0, High 1, Simpson 0, Kiddell not
out 3, extras 1; total 17.
THORPE VILLAGE v. NORWICH UNION—Played at
Thorpe on Thursday, and resulted in a win for the
Village by 42 runs. Thorpe batted first, and thanks
to Black (21) and Newstead (13), they were able to
make 64 runs. On the N.U.F.C. going in to bat only
Smith could make any stand against the bowling of
Black and Newstead, who took five for 8 and four for
11 respectively. Thorpe Village—Pitta 1, Parker 5,
Wright 8, Black 21, Newstead 13, Dearborough 1, Sopp
0, Harper 4, Woodcock 2, Fox 5, Fean not out 0,
extras 3; total 64. Norwich Union—W. Walton
2, W. O. Walton 5, Stone 8, Cross 0, Smith not out 11,
Smith 4, Corbett 0, Colman 0, L. Drake 0, Dodd 0,
Ebrahim 0, extras 3; total 22.
ACLE v GAYTON SNOOP.—Played at Acle, and
resulted in an easy win for the home team by an
innings and 27 runs. For the winners H. Holmes
batted well, and W. Holmes and J. Hawes did good
service with the ball, each securing four wickets at a
cost of only 3 runs. For the losers Turner batted freely.
