BIRMINGHAM LEAGUE.

STOURBRIDGE v. ASTON UNITY.—This match was set
for decision at Amblecote, on Saturday, but owing to the
frequent showers, the umpires at six o'clock decided not
to proceed further with the game Unity won the toss,
and went to the wickets, being represented by J. Davey
and H. Widdowson. This pair remained together while
45 runs were put on, when, after a brief retreat for rain,
Widdowson was bowled by Hingley for 15. This was
the harbinger of a rapid fall of wickets, for, having found
a convenient spot, Hingley bowled with deadly effect, and
whereas he had been hit for 17 runs without a wicket,
after the last resumption he took four wickets for 11 runs,
so that he had an average of 7 per wicket. Davey played
a splendid innings of 37, the chief hits in which were
five 4's, two 3's, and two 2's, and he made 37 out of the
58 put on while he was in. Considerable comment was
caused by the action of the umpires in closing the game
so soon, and some very uncomplimentary remarks were
passed about it. Score:—

BIRMINGHAM LEAGUE.—DIVISION II.

STOURBRIDGE (2ND) v. ASTON UNITY (2ND).—Stour-
bridge journeyed to Aston with only seven men. The
Rev. A. C. Madden won the toss, and decided to bat, in
the hopes that others would turn up. Brown and Green
opened the batting for Stourbridge, but runs came very
slowly. Green batted well for his score. Madden and
Brooks became partners, and made a good stand, Brooks
contenting himself with keeping up his end, while
Madden hit out freely. Brooks batted very patiently for
an hour and a half for 6 runs. Madden's free cricket was
a treat to watch. Rain came on about five o'clock, and
stopped play for half-an-hour. Ultimately the match
ended in a draw owing to the rain. Score:—

BELBROUGHTON v. BROMGROVE 2ND.—Played at Bel-
broughton, and owing to rain the game ended in a draw.
The visitors at one time looked liked making a miserable
score, loosing eight wickets for 29 runs, but afterwards ran
the score to 61. The home team lost two wickets for five
runs. A. Martin took four wickets for 20 runs; C.
Southall four for 16 runs. Bromsgrove: Rev. Ferrell, c
