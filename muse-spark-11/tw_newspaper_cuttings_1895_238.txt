BERROW'S WORCESTER JOURNAL: SATURDAY 17 AUGUST 1895-3.

WICHENFORD v. T. O. RICHERS' XI.-This match
was played at Loughboro Hill on Monday, and ended
in a win for Wichenford by 13 runs after a most
exciting match. Mr. Richers won the toss and went
in, and put together a very smart 53, Messrs. Harris
and Humphreys playing well for their runs. Wichen-
ford then went in and began disastrously, as six
wickets were down for 3, but when B. Preece joined
J. Page an excellent stand was made, the former
hitting out with perfect ease. G. A. Pideowas the next
partner but one, and when these two got together
they hit the bowlers to all parts of the field and
eventually won the match. B. Preece's fine innings
included two 4's, two 5's, nine 3's, and nine singles.
Mr. E. L. Harkness kept wicket for the
losers in first-rate style. Williams and Caswell again
bowled very effectively for Wichenford. Score:-

WICHENFORD v. CATHEDRAL GUILD.-This match
was played at Loughboro Hill, and ended in an easy
win for Wichenford by 54 runs and 5 wickets. The
Guild won the toss and elected to bat on a good
wicket, but were soon dismissed, thanks to the excel-
lent bowling of W. Williams and H. Caswell. Both
bowled perfectly and with an excellent length.
Wichenford got 74 for six wickets and declared,
Messrs Hastings, T. O. Williams, and G. F. Jones
Williams getting most runs. The Guild then went in
again and did slightly better against the same two
bowlers, who were bowling capitally, and got for
eight wickets. Score:-

ST. JOHN'S 2ND XI. v. COLWALL.-Played at Col-
wall and resulted in an easy victory for St. John's.
Messrs. Beaumont, Hudson, Park, and South all
batted well for their runs, Mr. Beaumont being top
scorer with a well played innings of 45. Mr. Evans
was the only double-figure scorer for Colwall. Mr.
Robinson bowled splendidly for St. John's, his
analysis reading 6 overs 4 maidens 6 runs and 6
wickets. Mr. Beaumont also bowled well, capturing
three wickets for 15 runs. Mr. Dee was the most

ST. JOHN'S 2ND XI. v. BROMSGROVE.-Played at
St. John's on Saturday, and resulted in an easy victory
for the home team. The visitors batted first, but
were disposed of for 38. Messrs. Robinson and Cross
both bowling well, the former taking seven wickets
for 18 runs, and the latter three for 12. St. John's
then scored 39 for three wickets, when rain prevented
further play. Messrs. Park, Beaumont, and Combe

OLD MALVERNIANS' TOUR.
The following fixtures have been arranged:-
August 19 and 20, v. Hants Rovers; 21 and 22, v.
Horsham; 23 and 24, v. Eastbourne. The team will
be as follows: H. F. Lyon (capt), J. H. Copleystone,
C. J. Burnup, G. H. Simpson, H. B. Porch, J. Fowks-
Keck, E. H. Simpson, C. W. Killett, A. R. Thomp-
son, M. H. Apthorp, and W. Hooper.
