NORTHAMPTON HERALD: SATURDAY 10 AUGUST 1895-2.

BEDFORDSHIRE v. M.C.C. AND GROUND.
Twelve aside played in this two days' match, which
was commenced at Bedford on Wednesday in fine
weather. The visitors went first to the wickets, and
were dismissed for 123, Barnes heading the score with
22. W. F. Brown took seven wickets for 44. Bedford-
shire commenced batting at a quarter-past four, L. C. R.
Thring and Manton facing the bowling of Needham and
Richardson. The latter was soon dismissed for eight,
but the former made a stand of an hour and a half, in
which time he had compiled 76 in excellent style, when
he unfortunately played on. Pope, his partner, had
made 43 (not out), when stumps were drawn. The
match resulted in a creditable win for the home county
by an innings and 26 runs. Score:—
