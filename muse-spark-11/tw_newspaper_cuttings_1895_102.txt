GLOUCESTER v ROSS.—Played at Ross on Wednesday on
a good wicket. The home team were very weak, being
without the services of Shaw (captain), Wintle, Woodward,
and F. J. Smith. Mr Pollo, one of the Ross players, met
with an accident whilst fielding and sprained his left leg.
Phillips played good cricket for 72, not out. Amongst his
hits being three six's which brought loud applause from the
spectators. Gloucester won by 81 runs. Score:—
