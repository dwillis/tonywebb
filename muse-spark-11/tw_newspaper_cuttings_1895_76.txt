PLYMOUTH POLICE v. NOMADS.
This match was played at South Devon-place yesterday. The
home team batted first, but made a very poor start, six wickets
falling for 7 runs. Townsend and Armstrong, however, batted
in a very confident manner, and when the last wicket fell the
score was 47. Nomads followed and, singularly enough, reached
the same total as their opponents. The result was, therefore, a
tie.

SIDMOUTH v. EALING.
This match was concluded in glorious weather on a perfect
wicket at Sidmouth yesterday. Sidmouth batted the first day,
making 367. The home eleven concluded their innings at six
o'clock, and when stumps were drawn, the visitors had lost two
wickets for 2 runs. Resuming yesterday, however, a splendid
stand was made by F. D. Conry. Dr MacGregor also batted well,
and the innings closed for 216. Following on, the visitors were
outclassed, the bowling of Nepean and Fox being extremely
well directed. Sidmouth won by an innings and two runs.
