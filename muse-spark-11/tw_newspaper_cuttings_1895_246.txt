KILDARE MILITIA v. ROY. or ALEX.— The above match was played on the Barrack ground, Curragh Camp, on Saturday, resulting in a brilliant victory for the Militia. Fine cricket was shown by the two, and seals "Kerry" and Thornhill to the wickets, but in each case before he was well set, was run out by his partner. Thornhill was then joined by de Robert, who commenced batting in very catchy style, and after a somewhat lucky innings, had contributed 25. Loch went next to the wicket and hit out right and left for his 32 not out! Next in Dr. Colett's excellent bowling accounted for the remainder of the team, and the innings closed for 143. Kearns and Deane started the batting for the Military, and the former's acquaintance with the sticks was abruptly brought to a close, and his place was taken by C. Supple. Deane was smartly caught by Thornhill at point for a useful 29, and Sergeant Dr. Colett joining [unclear], the pair hitting out freely brought the score to 25 for 2 wickets. The only other double figure was 39 for [unclear]. Not a bad stand was made by Grennan, who, by the aid of scientific cricket, contributed 35. Wickets began to fall now with fatal regularity, Colett being the first to go after an excellent 67, without giving a single chance. Grennan was the only remaining member of the team who succeeded in getting double figures, being not out 39. The feature of the visitors batting was, as usual, more than in the cricket field, made 7, and shortly after the innings closed for 235. Score:—
