KILDARE MILITIA v. ROY OF ALLEX.—The above match was played on the Kildare ground, Curragh Camp, on Saturday, resulting in a brilliant victory for the Militia. The visitors won the toss, and sent "Kerry" and Thornhill to the wickets, but the latter before he was well set, was run out by his partner. Thornhill was then joined by de Robeck, who commenced hitting general Harrison's bowling, and in a few minutes 30. Thornhill left shortly afterwards, having contributed a very sound 23. Lech next went to the wicket where he and de Robeck for his 27 not out only. Dr. Collett's excellent bowling accounted for the remainder of the team, and the innings closed for 143. Kayrooch and Varda started the batting for the Militia and the former's aggressiveness with the sticks was rapidly brought to a close, and his place was taken by Young. Depeche was smartly caught by Thornhill point for a useful 29, and Sergeant-Dr. Collett joining glasson, the pair hitting out freely brought the score to 49. This partnership was broken up by Thornhill., A grand stand was made by Gressan, who, by the aid of scientific cricket, contributed 35. Wickets began to fall now with rapidity, but Collett having the first in to go for an excellent 67, without giving a single chance. Thornhill was the only remaining member of the team who succeeded in getting double figures, being not out 39. Harrison, who was top scorer last week, was more at home than in the cricket field, made 7, and shortly after the innings closed for 255. Score:—
