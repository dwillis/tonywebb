EVESHAM v. PERSHORE—Played at Evesham on
Saturday. Going in first Evesham fared badly, six
wickets being down for 33. However Goodall, Need-
ham, Blanchard, and later on Field made useful
scores, and the total realised 89. Pershore could do
nothing against the bowling of Field, but scored
rather freely off Heath. Their total realised 68,
Evesham thus winning by 21 runs. C. Phillips bowled
splendidly for his runs, and Watts also did well.
Field took four wickets for eight runs. Score :—

MALVERN COLLEGE v. M.C.C.
This two-days' match, which concluded at Malvern
on Saturday, was remarkable for the small score of
the Collegians in their second innings, and 42 is almost
the lowest record associated with the Malvernians.
M.C.C. had the assistance of Woodcock and White-
side, from Leicestershire. The former took seven
wickets in the second innings, and a grandly-played
century total by the Rev. R. T. Thornton, together
with some useful scores, left M.C.C. winners at the
finish by an innings and 65 runs.
