THE WREXHAM TOUR.

FIRST MATCH.—WREXHAM v. LLANDUDNO.
Wrexham played the first fixture of their tour at Llandudno, on Wednesday. The day was beautifully fine, and a capitally contested game ended in an even draw. F. H. Jagger, the brothers Hollings, L. Baker, C. Kershaw, Throckmorton, and the Rev L. F. Ward all did well for their runs, while with the ball F. H. Ward captured five wickets for 40 runs, and Read three for 25. At one time it looked as if Llandudno were going to make a very poor show with the bat, as their first six wickets fell for 48 runs. Scores:—
