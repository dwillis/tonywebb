THE WREXHAM TOUR.
FIRST MATCH.-WREXHAM v. LLANDUDNO.
Wrexham played the first fixture of their tour at Llan-
dudno, on Wednesday. The day was beautifully fine, and
a capitally contested game ended in an even draw. F. H.
Jagger, the brothers Hollings, Ll. Thomson,
and the Rev L. F. Ward, batted well for their runs, while
with the ball L. W. Ward, captured five wickets for 40 runs,
and Head three for 25. At one time it looked as if Llan-
dudno were going to make a very poor show with the bat,
as their first six wickets fell for 48 runs. Score:-
