SCORES OF OVER 200.
The following record of individual scores of over 200 in first-class matches has been compiled by Dr. G. H. Brand. It appears that Mr. W. Ward's record, made in 1820 against Norfolk, which was then first class, remained unbroken for 56 years, until in 1876 W. G. Grace scored 344 and 318 not out. In 1876 the former being still the highest score that has ever been made in a first-class match, and the latter the highest in a first-class county match. W. G. Grace has scored over 200 ten times, Shrewsbury seven, and Gunn four. Notts is the only county off whose bowling such a score has not been made. CLECKHEATON v. LIVERSEDGE.
To the Editor of The Yorkshire Post,
Sir,-In your issue of to-day, both in your notes and in the report of the match, you give the result of the Cleckheaton v. Liversedge match as a draw. This is an erroneous statement, as Cleckheaton won. There were eleven extras in the Cleckheaton total, and not one, as given in your report. I hope you will correct this in your next issue as it makes an immense amount of difference in the League results to my club.-Yours, &c.,
Cleckheaton, May 20. N. GIBSON, Scorer.
* * The error was not ours. Only one extra was given in the report officially supplied.-Ed.
