HEYTESBURY v. ST. BONIFACE COLLEGE.--On Satur- day St. Boniface College played Heytesbury at Warmin- ster, the game ending in a victory for the home team. Scores: Heytesbury, 34 and 27; St. Boniface College, 46 and 16 for one wicket.
WINGFIELD v. WEST ASHTON.--Played at Wingfield on Saturday: Wingfield, 65 and 42 for six wickets; West Ashton, 50.
