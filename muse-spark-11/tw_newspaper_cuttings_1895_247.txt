ROYAL MUNSTER FUSILIERS V. ARMY SERVICE CORPS.

BATTING EXTRAORDINARY—THE RECORD BEATEN
Captain W. Crake, Oxford, Royal Munster Fusiliers, the popular Captain of the Curragh District C.C., and Private Fitzgerald, of the same battalion, yesterday had the distinction of beating the batting record by compiling the immense score of 623 runs during their partnership, and as both batsmen were not out at the call of "time" when they seemed good for another 500, it is impossible to estimate what might have happened had the game been prolonged. The building up of this huge score occupied 5 hours, yielding an average of 131 runs per hour. 605 is supposed to have been the previous record, that being the score credited to Messrs A. H. Trevor and G. P. Vernon in a match, Orleans Club v. Rickley Green some years ago, but it is understood both batsmen were "out." Captain Oates, who has done so much for cricket, especially for the younger members of the Curragh garrison, can this year richly merits the distinction of having contributed the lion's share towards this remarkable performance, and it is to be hoped that [unclear] to the value of the improvements to the ground inaugurated by him eight months ago, that this new record score should have followed so closely the score of over 400 runs recently made by the Curragh District against Dublin University. IRISH CRICKET UNION.
A meeting of the committee of the Leinster branch of the Irish Union was held on Wednesday evening in the Wicklow Hotel, when there were present—Mr G. W. Frith (in the chair), Mr F. Stokes (Dundrum), Mr H. Thornton (Pembroke), Mr J. W. Dawson (Civil Service), Mr J. Cotter (Dundrum), Mr F. H. Browning (Dublin University), hon. sec.; Mr J. Foxall (Leinster), hon. treasurer. Mr Wall, hon. sec. of Irish Union, reported that the Leinster Gentlemen of Ireland v. M.C.C. match amounted to £52, and after the discharge of all liabilities a balance of close on £20 remains to the credit of the union—a very satisfactory state of affairs. The Junior Cup competition was discussed, and it was decided that clubs, in order to be eligible, should be affiliated with the Leinster branch by July 1st. Entries for the cup close on July 14th.
