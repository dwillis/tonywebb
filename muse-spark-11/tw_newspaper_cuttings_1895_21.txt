GUILDHALL C.C. V NEWTOWN C.C.—These clubs met on Saturday in their return fixture at Sandleford park The game, which ended in a win for Guildhall, was remarkable for low scoring, the bowlers taking everything before them, in addition to which the fielding of both sides was good. PARISH CHURCH INSTITUTE V HIGHCLERE—The return match between these teams was played, by kind permission, on the cricket ground on Saturday in splendid weather. Highclere winning the toss decided to bat first, but owing to the bowling of Gunning and Gayler, the first four wickets fell for 21, when Collins came in and made a useful stand, necessitating several changes in the bowling. Still runs came, Collins doing the lion's share, but Gunning going on at the top end fairly beat him; the beauty of his bowling being shown by his masterful stands. After the interval the Institute started batting with Cook and Pyke, the former hitting his wicket, whilst Pyke contented himself by keeping up his wicket. Mashin the next man, made things more lively putting on 13 in quick time. The others making double figures were Pyke, Young and Gunning. Both teams were entertained to tea through the kindness of Miss L. Godding and Mr H Godding. Score :—
