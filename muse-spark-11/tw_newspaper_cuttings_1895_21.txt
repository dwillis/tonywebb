PARISH CHURCH INSTITUTE V HIGHCLERE—The return match between these teams was played, by kind permission, on the cricket ground on Saturday in splendid weather. Highclere winning the toss decided to bat first, but owing to the bowling of Gunning and Gayfer, the first four wickets fell for 21, when Collins came in and made a useful stand, necessitating several changes in the bowling. Still runs came, Collins doing the lion's share, but Gunning going on at the top end fairly beat him with a beauty, which terminated a useful stand. After the interval the Institute started batting with Cook and Pyke, the former hitting his wicket, whilst Pyke contented himself by keeping up his wicket. Mashin the next man, made things more lively putting on 18 in quick time. The others making double figures were Pyke, Young and Gunning. Both teams were entertained to tea through the kindness of Miss L. Godding and Mr H Godding. Score :—
