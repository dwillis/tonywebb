cluded yesterday, the Incogs winning by an innings and 79 runs.

were drawn the visitors had made 55 for the loss of seven wickets.
Yesterday the wicket was altogether in favour of the bowlers.

were disposed of the first time for 61, runs before lunch. [unclear]
