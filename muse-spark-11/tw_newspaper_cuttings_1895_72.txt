RAGLAN BARRACKS v. NORTH DEVON.

Somerset Light Infantry and Berkshire Regiment. The Barracks

107. In reply the visitors knocked up 113 for six wickets, thus

SIDMOUTH v. INCOGNITI.
This match commenced at Sidmouth on Wednesday and con-
cluded yesterday, the Incogs winning by an innings and 79 runs.
Rain interfered with the match the first day, and when stumps
were drawn the visitors had made 155 for the loss of seven wickets.
Yesterday the wicket was altogether in favour of the bowlers.
The innings of the Incogs was soon finished, and Sidmouth
were disposed of the first time for 61 runs before lunch, and
in the afternoon they only raised 78. Higson and Fox did
splendidly, the former taking twelve wickets during the match
for 73 runs, and the latter eight for 82. Score:—
