PLYMOUTH v. STONEHOUSE.
This match was played at South Devon-place yesterday, and resulted in an easy win for the home team. Batting first, the home team put together 121, P. F. Tyacke, H. B. Spencer, and H. B. R. Harrison scoring most of the runs, and played very fine cricket against the good bowling of Laverty and Martin, both of the visitors. J. G. Davey bowled unchanged, and sent down 23 overs 14 maidens four wickets for 24 runs. The innings of the visitors was a poor display, owing to the brilliant trundling of H. B. R. Harrison. The Plymouth man bowled at a tremendous rate, and took eight wickets for but 5 runs, with accurate fielding [unclear]. Trillo batted, and scored 40 for eight wickets. The wicket- keeping of S. Sidney was very fine, only 2 leg byes being registered.
