BURTON LATIMER v. WELLINGBORO' ALL SAINTS B.—
Played at Wellingboro' on Saturday, the visitors
winning by 114 runs.
KETTERING WANDERERS v. GRAFTON.—Played at
Kettering on Saturday, the visitors winning by 29 runs
to 90.
LINCOLN.—The Bracebridge Asylum eleven met the
Lincoln Imps on Saturday. The Asylum went in first,
and scored 20 for eight wickets, Bennett contributing
11. The Imps then went in, but had only scored 11
when their last wicket fell. The losers would have
registered a very poor total had it not been for a well-
hit 25 by Cox.
ROTHWELL INDEPENDENT v. KETTERING BRITANNIA.—
At Rothwell on Saturday, an interesting game ending in
a draw.
