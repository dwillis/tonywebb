NORTHAMPTON HERALD: SATURDAY 17 AUGUST 1895-2.

NORTHAMPTON TOWN LEAGUE.
SECOND SECTION.
Victoria-road v. Excelsior Albions. — Low scoring
ruled in this match, which was played on the Borough
Ground on Saturday. Victoria-road compiled 31, F.
King's 8 being the highest contribution. Cupson took
four wickets for 12, and Tait four for 10. The Excel-
sior Albions, who only batted eight men, were dis-
missed for 26, W. Darby (9) being the highest scorer.
Victoria-road thus won by five runs. For Victoria-
road F. Roberts obtained three wickets for 10, and F.
Cooke four for 10.
Princess-street v. Doddridge. — Princess-street
suffered another reverse on Saturday on the Borough
Ground, Doddridge replying with 103 to their oppo-
nents' 61. J. Ingram (Princess-street's pro), who
who scored 28, was the only one to get into double
figures against the destructive bowling of J. Smith
(eight wickets for 20 runs). W. Leak obtained one
wicket for 28, and A. Sutton one for 9. The chief
contributors to the Doddridge total were A. Jones
(25), C. Sutton (14), C. Jones (12), and W. Tharley
(10). For Princess-street W. Dartnall took three
wickets for 29, F. Smith one for 12, W. Stacey two
for 5, W. Rogers two for 22, and F. Brooks two for 25.
Moss Rose v. Pioneer.—The Moss Rose administered
a crushing defeat to the Pioneer on the Borough
Ground on Saturday. Batting first, the former "de-
clared" when the score was 100 for six wickets, W.
Reed, who played an excellent not out innings of 44,
and W. Langley (36) being the chief contributors.
Stanford (two for 36), A. Knight (one for 18), Wilkin-
son (two for 16), and H. Wright (one for 20), trundled
for the Pioneer. The Pioneer were all out at 65, J.
Knight (11) and A. Knight (10) being the only bats-
men to reach double figures. Bert Goodall (six wickets
for 33) and Garrard (four for 21) were the bowlers
for the Moss Rose.

BEDFORDSHIRE v. OXFORDSHIRE.
This return match was concluded at Bedford on Satur-
day, when the home county had to bow to defeat to the
tune of an innings and 30 runs. It was very evident
after Bedfordshire's first venture that their opponents
were too strong for them, and Oxfordshire brought the
day's play to a close with the respectable total of 223,
thus leaving Bedfordshire the task of scoring 135 to
save an innings defeat. On Saturday they took up their
second stand, but the Brothers Rogers's bowling was
so destructive that they were all dismissed for 105. P.
Rogers taking eight wickets for 53 runs. Oxfordshire
thus gained their second victory over Bedfordshire this
season by an innings and 30 runs.

St. James' Excelsior C.C. v. St. James' Hall—
Played at St. James' End on Thursday, and won by the
former by 45 runs. The Hall, batting first, could do
nothing against the bowling of Dillingham (five wickets
for 9 runs) and Wright (five for seven runs). On the
Excelsior batting they made 54. O. Wright scored 17
and A. Kirton 8. Scores: Excelsior, 54; St. James'
Hall, 9.
St. Paul's v. Princess-street Y.M.C.A.—Played on the
Racecourse on Saturday, and won by St. Paul's by two
runs. Scores: St. Paul's 45, Princess-street 43.
St. Paul's v. Wellingborough All Saints Juniors.—
Played at Wellingborough on August 7th, and won by
St. Paul's by 85 runs. Scores: 91 and 50.
Wellingborough All Saints v. Kettering Working
Men's Club.—Played at Wellingborough on Saturday,
and won by the homesters, who scored 100 for nine
wickets against 42 of the losers. G. Hooper 20, R.
Vickers 17, A. Gant 15, W. Bennett 14, not out, and
J. Pettit 12, not out, batted well for All Saints, and
S. Browning, not out, 13, did best for the visitors.
