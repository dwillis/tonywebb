Berrow's Worcester Journal: Saturday 29 June 1895-2.

St. John's v. Pershore.—Played at St. John's on
Saturday, and resulted in favour of the home team by
38 runs and five wickets. Pershore, who batted first,
were disposed of for 78 runs, Messrs. H. Phillips and
H. Martin batting well for their respective scores.
Messrs. Mansell and H. Smith both bowled very well
for the home team, the former taking five wickets for
18 runs, and the latter three wickets for 12 runs. St.
John's then scored 116 for five wickets, Messrs. Dodson
Onslow, Mansell, and Curtis all batting well. Score:—

Worcester Grammar School v. Lyttelton
Grammar School.—Played at Fine Meadow on Satur-
day, Lyttelton batted first but could do little against
the bowling of Thorp and Spencer, the whole side
being dismissed for 13. Spencer took three wickets
for 7 runs, bowling six maidens, while Thorp took five
wickets for 6 runs. Worcester Grammar School, on
going in, passed the Lyttelton score with two wickets
down. Davis and Willcock bat well, the former
making 15, including a 5 and a 4, and the latter 12.
The next man (Thorp) made top score (24), which
included some fine cuts. The innings closed for 87.
Bloseme, who bowled very well, secured six wickets.
On going in a second time Lyttelton did slightly
better, but only managed to make 18. Willcock took
five wickets for 6 runs and Thorp four for 6, Worcester
Grammar School thus winning by an innings and 56
runs. Score:—

Worcester Grammar School v. Kidderminster
Grammar School.—Played at Kidderminster, on
Wednesday. Although the wicket was more in favour
of the batsmen, being hard with only the grass wetted,
the bowlers on both sides were most prominent.
Kidderminster batted first, but were all dismissed for
8. Thorp took five wickets for 2 runs, and Spencer
five wickets for 4 runs. The latter also accomplished
the "hat trick." Worcester Grammar School made a
better display with the bat than their rivals, but the
really excellent bowling of Tye and Woodward pre-
vented a large score. Both bowled very fast and kept
good length. This is the first defeat that Kidder-
minster have received for a long time, and the first
time they have succumbed to Worcester Grammar
School in any sport. Score:—

WORCESTERSHIRE CLUB AND GROUND
v. NORTON BARRACKS.

With only three wickets down for 128, it looked as
if the county side would run up a larger total, but the
last seven men only added 22. There was some excite-
ment when the military had only lost half their
wickets for the century, but the tail was very weak,
and they were defeated by 42 runs. Score:—
