LIVERPOOL POST: FRIDAY 13 SEPTEMBER 1895: TUESDAY 17 SEPT 1895

LIVERPOOL CRICKET CLUB.
BATTING AND BOWLING AVERAGES.
The first eleven played 37 matches, won 16, lost 6, and 15 were drawn. Won: Western, Werneth (twice), Sefton (twice), Rossall School, Boughton Hall (twice), Bootle, Warrington, Uppingham, Birkenhead Park, Preston, [unclear], [unclear], New Brighton, and Oxton. Lost: Huyton, [unclear] College, Manchester, Formby, and Liverpool (twice). Drawn: Ormskirk (twice), Birkenhead Park, Marlborough College, Clifton College, Cheltenham College, Northern (twice), Oxton, Rock Ferry, Pastonians, Lorettonians, Harrow Wanderers, Western, Warrington, and Hol [unclear] [unclear]. Runs to the number of 5,961 (including 353 extras) were scored for the loss of 341 wickets, against 5,880 runs (including 299 extras) for the loss of 342 wickets. The "A" eleven played 18 matches, won 5, lost 4, and 7 were drawn, [unclear] 2,282 runs (including 144 extras) for the loss of 139 wickets, against 2,053 runs (including 177 extras) for the loss of 119 wickets. The second eleven played 27 matches, won 8, lost 7, and drew 12, scoring 3,878 runs (including 311 extras) for the loss of 204 wickets, against 3,897 runs (including 314 extras) for the loss of 220 wickets. The third eleven played 6 matches, of which 4 were won and 2 drawn. In all the club played 88 matches, of which 33 were won, 17 lost, and 38 drawn. District matches were played against Yorkshire County and Cambridge University, the former being won and the latter lost. In addition, 3 extra matches were played, giving a grand total of 97 matches during the season. Although Mr. A. T. Kemble has only taken part in 21 matches this season, he has succeeded in stumping and catching 41 opponents.
