HEREFORD TIMES: SATURDAY 20 JULY 1895-3.

BROMYARD v LEDBURY.—Played at Ledbury on Saturday,
and resulted in a easy victory for the visitors, Frost, the
Bromyard professional performing the "hat" trick.
MONMOUTH v ROSS.—This return match was played at
Monmouth on Saturday, when the visitors won by 264 runs
and four wickets. For the winners Woodward played finely
for his 128, as also did Lieut. Vaughan for 76.
DOWNTON CASTLE v ASHFORD COURT.—Played at Ash-
ford on Saturday, and ended in a win for Ashford Court on
the first innings. Ashford played nine men. Score: Ash-
ford Court, 56 (F. Northover, 19; T. Matthews, not out 12);
Downton Castle, 41 and 37 for five wickets (J. Mitchell, 10
and not out 13; J. Bell, 7; T. Hotchkiss, 7; and W.
Hawkins, 12).
