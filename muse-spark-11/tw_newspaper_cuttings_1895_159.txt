EASTERN DAILY PRESS: WEDNESDAY 5 JUNE 1895-2.

DISS v. KING'S DRAGOON GUARDS.
Played at Diss on Monday, and resulted in a win
for Diss by 80 runs. Charming cricket weather
prevailed, and numerous spectators watched the game
with evident enthusiasm. Diss won the toss, and
electing to bat first, ran up a total of 182, to which
Tipple, Candler, Ireland, Nicholls and Dowson were
the chief contributors. The Dragoons then batted,
but made rather a poor show against the bowling of
Ireland and Nottidge, the former taking six wickets.
Harper scored freely towards the finish. Lieutenant
Searight bowled well for the losers. Scores :—
