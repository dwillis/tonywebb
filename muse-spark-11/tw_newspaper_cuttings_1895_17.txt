After the match, through the hospitality of Mr Hunt, the two teams partook of tea on the lawn. At the conclusion of the meal the captain of the home club (Mr F J Hunt), presented Mr H Roberts with a cap in recognition of his recent performance of the hat trick. A lengthy stand was then made until Edwards joined Pope; Edwards soon made 14. Drower was the only other man to reach double figures; and by free play he scored 22, the innings closing for 102. Newtown, on handling the willow, made a most disastrous start, six wickets falling for half-a-dozen runs. Then two wickets fell, then the score was—all for 18 not out. The innings closed for 27, Newtown being 60 runs behind their confreres. Allsop and Williamson at their next venture played admirably, scoring between them 101 before being parted.
