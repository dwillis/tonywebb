DEVIZES AND WILTSHIRE GAZETTE: THURSDAY 13 JUNE 1895-2.

Corsham v. Trowbridge.-LEAGUE MATCH.-This
match was played on the ground of the latter on Satur-
day last and was the second league match played by the
visitors, who again proved victorious by 72 runs, though
six of their batsmen were under 20 years of age, it being
the youngest team ever sent out to represent the Corsham
1st XI. Fudge again was extremely useful with his bat,
and was well supported by Mayo and F Hulbert. Scores:

Corsham 2nd XI. v. Avon Vale (Bath).-This match
was played at Corsham on Saturday last, and resulted in
a victory for the home team by two wickets and 51 runs.
Philo was in good form, contributing 57 in excellent style,
and was well backed up by West, Carter, and Johnson:
Carter bowled well, taking 4 wickets for 15 runs. Scores:
