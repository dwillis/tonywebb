THREE COUNTIES ASYLUM C.C.
A very creditable record is that of the Three Counties
Asylum Club for the past season—viz.: matches won, 11;
drawn, 2; lost, 6. With the "glorious uncertainty" of
cricket which gives so much more interest to the game,
the Asylum eleven were successful sometimes contrary
to their anticipations, and sustained one or two very
unexpected defeats. The matches won were with
Ampthill, Roxton, Biggleswade, Bedford Ramblers,
Hitchin, Stevenage, London Joint Stock Bank, West-
minster Wanderers, Nunsden, Sandy and Mr. A. W.
Tindall Loose's XI. The two drawn matches were those
with Hitchin Tradesmen C.C. and North Thames
L.V.C.C. Defeats were sustained from Mr. Gurney's
XI, London and Westminster Bank, Royston, Baldock,
Cottered and London Truro C.C.

SILSOE C.C.
The Silsoe club have had a very successful season.
Ten matches have been played, and nine have been won,
and the other ended in a draw in favour of Silsoe. In
batting H. Squire heads the list with an average of
17.2 runs; and good records are also held by Gudgin,
11.5 and O. Bottoms, 10. With the ball Gudgin and
E. Porter have been very successful, the former
taking 72 wickets at an average cost of 3.3 runs,
the latter 48 wickets at an average cost of 4.5.
