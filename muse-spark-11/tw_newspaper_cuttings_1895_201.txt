To secure insertion for Saturday's Cricket Matches they should be in hand before noon on Monday.

MONTGOMERY v. ABERYSTWYTH.—Played at Lymore Park, Montgomery, on Saturday, in rainy weather. The home captain won the toss and sent the visitors in. Williams and A. Eaton, the Montgomery bowlers, were in fine form, taking six wickets for 12, four for 17 respectively, and got rid of the Ystwythians for 32, of which E. Davies, a Montgomery substitute, made 13. The Montgomery innings realised 111, towards which A. Eaton (11), O. H. Williams (31), R. T. Harris (13), Shaw (13), and E. Griffiths (11 not out) were the chief contributors. E. Davies, the Montgomery substitute, bowled well for the visitors, who had a weak team.

WXM v SHAVINGTON.—Played at Shavington on Wednesday. Anthony and Clayton went to the wicket first for Wxm, and made out second of Lonsdale and Arkenstall. They batted freely, and the score rose to 67 before Clayton was caught and bowled by Exham. This broke the leg, Anthony being also dismissed in the next over for a freely hit 42. The rest of the wickets fell steadily, and the innings closed for 113 runs. Tea was then partaken of, and on resuming only one over had been bowled when rain came on heavily and stopped the play for the day. Score:—

WHITTINGTON v TESMORE, (RETURN).—The second fixture between these Clubs was played at Tesmore on Saturday, and resulted in favour of the visitors by 23 runs. For the winners Beckett captured five wickets. Humphreys played a spirited but somewhat fortunate innings of 21 before being bowled by W Hudson, this total for the first wicket fell for 40. No one else did much except W Jones who made 15, the innings realising 46. Rowson and Hudson bowled well. The home team made a fairly good start, the first wicket falling for 10, but all were disposed of for 24, no one reaching double figures. MONTGOMERY v PONTESBURY.—These teams played off their return match at the latter place on Wednesday, In dull weather. The home team, which comprised a strong eleven, batted first, and were all dismissed by A Eaton and Withers for thirty-five, Eaton taking five wickets for twelve and Withers five for an even dozen. Amos, who compiled a dozen, was the only man to make any stand. For the visitors the brothers Eaton were in rare form, Pryce Eaton carrying his bat from first wicket down for an admirable score of sixty-nine, while Alf Eaton did yeoman service, and, together with his senior, succeeded in dismissing all but only a single run being scored off W down. The other Montgomery men were accounted for by Amos and Cartwright, who secured four wickets each. Scores :—
