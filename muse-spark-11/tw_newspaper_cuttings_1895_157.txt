BASSALEG v. NEWPORT BANKS.
This annual match, played at Bassaleg on Thurs-
day, resulted, as the subjoined score shows, in a
narrow victory for the home team. For the home
team Jones captured four wickets. Cound, for the
visitors, being accredited the same number. Rodney
Williams and H. G. Davies were respectively the
top scorers.

CAXTONIANS v. NEWPORT NORTHERN.
This match was played on the Marshes on Satur-
day. This being the first match for the Caxtonians,
much interest was manifested by their friends,
a large number of whom put in an appearance.
The feature of the game was the splendid bowling
of E. T. Smith. Both eleven's were very keen in
the fielding.

RANJITSINHJI IN BRIGHTON.
At the conclusion of the Sussex v. Lancashire
Cricket Match on Wednesday, a member of the
staff of the "Brighton Examiner" had an interest-
ing interview with Mr. Ranjitsinhji in regard to
his connection with Sussex Cricket. The interview
is fully reported in the "Brighton Examiner" of
Friday, August 16th.
