# BEDFORDSHIRE TIMES: FRIDAY 25 AUGUST 1939-3.

## SCHOOL PLAYERS TO THE RESCUE

## Bedford Save the Game against Hitchin

It was a strange team which took the field to represent Bedford against Hitchin at Bedford on Saturday and the weakened home side gave one of its least attractive displays of the year. Apart from Marston, the Bedford School bowler, and P. Rawlins, the attack was weak; the work in the field was scrappy, and the batting mediocre. Hitchin were unlucky to be denied victory.

Hitchin, batting first, lost a wicket in the second over, but then King and Stanley began an association which put the visitors in a happy position. King hit very freely to score 102, and Stanley was enterprising in making 31, though he made a lot of chancey strokes. Of the batsmen who followed only Brown (35) and Barker (26) were able to face Marston with confidence. This young bowler took six wickets for 46. Rawlins, who got little help from the pitch, did well to take three wickets for 67.

Bedford's batting was disappointing, the feature being the stand of 61 between Audley, of Bedford School, and Dexter. It looked odds-on a Bedford defeat, but Ferriman and Hawkes, the last pair, held out. Scores:—

## BALL BEATS BAT

## A.ipley Guise Out for Their Lowest Score

The ball beat the bat most of the time on Saturday when Aspley Guise were beaten for the second time at home this season, and were dismissed for their lowest total. Bedford Town second eleven providing the opposition.

Clever bowling by Blofeld caused the visitors to lose four wickets for nine runs. With the total 21 for five Blofeld had captured four wickets for three runs, and he finished with six for 22.

The position of the Bedford team was relieved by a ninth-wicket stand which produced 22 runs. Barnwell had three for 22. He also made a smart one-handed catch in the slips. The Aspley fielding was keen and two good catches were made by Bedford.

Aspley were soon in trouble against Humphreys and Sleightholm. One batsman short, they were all out for 48. Royden, with twenty, was the only batsman to reach double figures. P. H. Humphreys took six for 17 in six overs and Sleightholm three for 27. Scores:—

## EVERSHOLT NO MATCH FOR THE ARGUS

The Argus easily defeated Eversholt in Bedford Park on Saturday. The visitors batted first and were all out for 68 runs. R. Oakley (25) was the only batsman who could cope with the bowling of R. J. Minns (three for nine) and J. Smith (three for six). F. R. Sharp was in splendid form for the Argus and scored a most attractive century. ## EIGHT FOR EIGHT Barker's Deadly Bowling for Harpur Sports

A Barker, the Harpur Sports bowler, put up a remarkable performance on Saturday in Bedford Park. He ran through the London Brick Company, Bletehley, who could total only forty runs on an excellent wicket. In taking eight wickets for eight runs, Barker bowled only six overs, and in his fourth he did the "hat trick". He also captured three wickets in his last over. Webster, at the other end, took the remaining two wickets for 14 runs. Harpur won the match by 91 runs.
