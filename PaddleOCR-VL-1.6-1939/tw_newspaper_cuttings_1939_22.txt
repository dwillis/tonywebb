# THE CHRONICLE FRIDAY AUGUST 11 1939

# ONLY THREE BATSMEN REACH DOUBLE FIGURES READING'S COLLAPSE AGAINST THE DUCKS

## READING V. THE DUCKS

Played at Earley on Sunday, and won by The Ducks by 69 runs. Taking first knock on a wicket that was very easy before lunch, The Ducks' early batsmen scored very slowly and carefully, and gave the side a good start with 106 for 3. Then, 6 wickets fell for 18 runs, but a last wicket stand added 31, the total being 155. C. L. Grieshaber (3 for 20) and A. V. Percival (3 for 32) were the most successful bowlers. Only three Reading batsmen reached double figures, Grieshaber (23) being top scorer, and the whole side was out for 86.

# WICKET FAVOURS THE BOWLERS

## READING V. OLD REDINGENSIANS

Played at Earley on Monday, and won by Reading by 39 runs. There had been heavy rain in the morning, and play did not begin until just before 2 p.m., Reading taking first knock. In two hours the whole side was out for 82, H. W. Neilan (24), who was well caught by C. N. West, making the most runs. A. W. Flatman (6 for 25) had bowled well for the O.R.S. Then C. J. Woolhead (3 for 16) and H. W. Neilan (7 for 26), bowling unchanged, dismissed the O.R.S. in 84 minutes for 43 runs. To play out time, each side batted a second time for 45 minutes, Reading again proving the better side with 89 for 5 (Thirklill 38, and Neilan 25 not out), against 42 for 7 (Percival taking 3 wickets for 0 runs in one over).
