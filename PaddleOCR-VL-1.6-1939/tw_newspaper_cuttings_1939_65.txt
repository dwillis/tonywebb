## PINNER HAD FIVE WICKETS TO SPARE

## Old Merchant Taylor 112.....(8 wkts.) 141

Old Merchant Taylor's were below strength and batted poorly on a slow wicket on which the ball kept low. H. Harold, this year's School captain, was an exception. The pitch became easier later, though never at any time difficult, and Pinner had no difficulty after losing a few early wickets. Pinner passed the Taylor's score for the loss of five wickets.
