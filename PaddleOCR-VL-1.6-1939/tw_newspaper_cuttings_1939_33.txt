# TEWHESBURY REGISTER AND GAZETTE: SATURDAY 13 MAY 1939-2

## EVESHAM'S OPENING MATCH

## TEWKESBURY WIN COMFORTABLY

Evesham made a rather medlocre start to the season on Saturday when, on their own ground, they lost to Tewkesbury by 37 runs.

Winning the toss, Tewkesbury batted first on an easy wicket and Evesham did well to get them out for 115. Their first wicket produced 26 and although two more men left with the addition of only two to the score, runs were afterwards obtained at a steady rate.

H. Robbins and N. Pulley, two medium right arm bowlers, kept a good length and took five for 35 and four for 15 respectively. A new recruit, R. Ingles, of Broadway, a slow left arm bowler, also maintained his length, but bowled within little luck and came out with the analysis of one for 32. Evesham's fielding showed a welcome improvement on last year and Dunn, a new wicket keeper, shaped well.

Evesham could only muster 78 in reply to the visitors. They were unfortunate to lose E. G. Righton, who was run out through a misunderstanding, with only nine on the board and although S. H. Johns, the other opening bat, played carefully he was third cut, having scored but four. F. Dunn and R. Randall, in a short-lived partnership, took the total from 27 to 51 for the fifth wicket but two more men were dismissed at this total. The eighth wicket partnership between H. Robbins and L. T. Phipps produced 22, but the remaining batsmen could muster five only.

For Tewkesbury, D Martin, who turned the ball considerably, took 5 for 33. I Gilbert 3 for 17, and W. T. Mustoe 1 for 24

## ALCESTER GAIN EASY WIN

## DUMBLETON'S POOR BATTING

Alcester and Ragley visited Dumbleton, on Saturday, and gained an easy victory by 122 runs.

Dumbleton had first use of the wicket, but made a poor show against the bowling of the brothers F. and E. Bunting, and were all dismissed for 46 runs, W. Launchbury scoring half of the total. F. Bunting and E. Bunting bowled unchanged with much success, taking seven for 22 in 5.6 overs, and three for 23 in five overs (one maiden) respectively.

Alcester passed their opponents' total before the fall of the second wicket and when stumps were drawn they had scored 168 for the loss of five wickets. R. Hewlett (43) one of the opening pair, almost equalled Dumbleton's score on his own and five other batsmen reached double figures.

Dumbleton tried seven bowlers, the wickets being shared by J. Slatter (1 for 13), E. Pulley (1 for 14), H. C. Meadows (1 for 38), F. Whatcot (1 for 10), E. Hopkins (1 for 21).

## VALE'S SECOND DEFEAT

Bourton Vale suffered their second defeat of the season on Saturday, when they lost to their Winchcombe visitors by two wickets. Batting first, the Vale found run-getting nigh impossible against the fine bowling of T. Headlam, who in 15.3 overs, seven of which were maidens, took seven wickets for 11 runs. The only man to stand up to his bowling was S. Hughes, who was caught out after making 26, the side being out for a total of 72.

A. Marshall (2 for 3) and G. E. Arthurs (1 for 10) also bowled well for Winchcombe. Vale bowlers were T. Garne (1 for 2), F. J. Hitch (2 for 26), L. Hill (2 for 28), R. Hitch (1 for 10) and F. Simmons (1 for 21).

Winchcombe, who were well served by H. Greening, A. Marshall and G. E. Arthurs, knocked off the runs for the loss of eight wickets, their total at the fall of the eighth being 91.
