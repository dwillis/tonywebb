# BEDFORDSHIRE TIMES: FRIDAY 11 AUGUST 1939-6.

## BOWLING PARKERS

## Help Pulloxhill Along to Victory

Pulloxhill played Mr. H. Westrope's XI at Pulloxhill on August Monday, the match resulting in a win for Pullexhill by 52 runs. Mr. H. Westrope's eleven batted first, and after losing a wicket at three the captain and K. Marshall took the score to 15. From then on wicket's fell fast against some good bowling by R. F. Parker (seven for 12) ad H. F. Parker (three for 15), and they were all out for 29.

Pulloxhill suffered an early shock, for W. S. Dollimore was caught at the wicket off the second ball of the innings. Ashton and E. H. Parker took the score to 12 before the former was bowled, and then B. Jellis helped the latter to add 1 and was caught. E. H. Parker, after scoring 17, left one run later. A fifth-wicked partnership between R. F. Parker and Ashton saw their opponents' total passed and contributions from the "full" bade-men helped to make a total of $1.

## A VALUABLE STAND

## Bedford Seconds Lively against Royston

The Bedford Town second eleven scored an easy victory at Royston on Monday, defeating the home team by 190 runs to 36 P. II. Humphreys was mainly instrumental in dismissing the home side so cheaply, his six wickets costing him only seven runs. Sleightholm captured the remaining four wickets for 24 runs.

Bedford batted first and lost two quick wickets. Ferriman and Humphreys being out with only two runs on the board. Gompertz followed icon afterwards, but a stand by Howard and Ward added 1 runs for the fourth wicket. Howard hit five fours and two sixes, and Ward and Sieghitholm also hit a six each. However, the last four batten had only 27 runs, and the inning closed at 150.

Slengthholm took a wicket in his second over, and after that wickets fell in quick succession and the Royston innings ended at 36. Taey fared better in a second innings, scoring 78 for four wickets. The scores were:
