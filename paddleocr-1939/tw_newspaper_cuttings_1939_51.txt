# HERTS ADVERTISER: FRIDAY 4 AUGUST 1939-5.

## REDBOURN'S COLLAPSE.

Redbourn suffered another defeat on Saturday—when they lost at Woodside Park by 62 runs—because their batting did not come up to the standard of their bowling and fielding. R. Claxton (80) hit hard for Woodside Park, who declared with 161 for 9. Redbourn soon lost five wickets for 34. W. H. Gurney (11) assisted C. V. H. Cooper (35) to lift the score to 66 for 6 and J. R. Collis (12) helped Cooper to take the total to 92 for 7, but the eighth and ninth wickets fell without addition and the innings closed for 99.

## BEDMOND WIN AT NASH MILLS.

Bedmond succeeded by three wickets at Nash Mills, on Saturday, Nash Mills had a first innings lead of 18 and declared with 62 for 7 in their second venture, but Bedmond rose to the occasion and replied with 83 for 7, made in twenty-five minutes, to win the game. H. Gentle, of Bedmond, had to be taken to Hospital to have stitches inserted in a cut over his eye, but returned to the fray and took five wickets for 39.

## ANOTHER ELSTREE SUCCESS.

Mistree gained another victory on Sunday, when they bent North Highgate by 123 runs. Batting first on a soft wicket. Eisfree made 226 for 9 before declining. J. Kellock was again top scorer with a hard-hit 60, while A. Lyons, who batted well, was unfortunate to miss his half-century. There was a lively last-wicket stand between father and son — E. Evans (22 not out) and M. C. Evans (20 not out). North Highgate never got on top of the Eisfree attack and were dismissed for 103. Scores:
