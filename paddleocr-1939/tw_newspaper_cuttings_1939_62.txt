## LEAVESDEN WHACK WEMBLEY WANDERERS.

Leavesden beat Wembley Wanderers by 119 runs at Leavesden on Saturday. Ward (5-20), Wells (4-13) and Watson (1-1) dismissed the Wanderers for 43. Leavesden passed that total with seven wickets in hand. R. Burke, who batted splendidly, appeared to be well on the way to a century, but at 89 fell to a brilliant catch by H. Gee in the long field. ## A. PALMER'S FINE BOWLING FOR MARKYATE.

Owing to holidays, Markyate filed a weakened team on Saturday when they visited Westbourne at London and lost by 21 runs. B. Norris (27) and H. Archer (19) were the only Markyate players to reach double figures. After their partnership had been ended with the score at 44 for 2, a collapse set in and the innings closed for 69. Thanks to a fine innings by A. Vass (46), Westbourne passed the Markyate total with eight wickets in hand, but bowlers got on top after that. A. Palmer having two inspired overs in which he claimed five victims, all clean bowled, without conceding a run. He had the final analysis of 5 for 8, and it seemed that had he been brought back earlier, Markyate might have won.
