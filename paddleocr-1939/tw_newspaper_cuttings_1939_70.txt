# STARRED SELECTIONS

IN the ideal cricket weather that only the start of the football season can bring, few matches were brought to a definite result. Abbots Langley preserved their unbeaten record by the skin of their teeth, beating Chipperfield by eight runs, Hemel Hempstead won very comfortably, but West Herts, Bushey, Chorleywood, Stannmore, Leavesden and Kings Langley were among the teams that drew their fixtures.

Of the five centuries that were scored, Don Yates obtained two-hitting 108 not out on Saturday, and 100 not out on Sunday. Yet another Yates achievement.
