# WEST HERTS AND WATFORD OBSERVER: FRIDAY 4 AUGUST 1939-4

# Berkhamsted Wilt Before Leg-Break Barnes

RICK MANSWORTH 139

BERKHAMSTED 66

BENNETT, a 15-years-old schoolboy, going in first for Rickmansworth, help. Openshaw to put on 59 for the first wicket. These runs later proved invaluable. as wickets began to tumble regularly, especially against Stirling (six for 53).

## M. A. WIGGS GETS HIS COUNTY CAP

After the Herts v. Bucks match at St. Albans last week M. A. Wiggs was awarded his county cap. Wiggs, who lives in Watford, attended St. Albans School, and plays regularly for the Old Albanians.

Wiggs is also a county Rugby player and an excellent golfer.

D. C. Kendrick, the West Herts bowler on Saturday took his 50th wicket of the season.

## All out for 139, Rickmansworth's highest scorer was Bennett with 30.

When Berkhamsted faced the home attack they fared even worse, and found the leg breaks of Barnes more than they could master. Barnes had six for 26, and Berkhamsted were all out for 60-73 short of Rickmansworth's total. Score:

## K. H. CHAPMAN MADE USE OF STICKY WICKET

## Stanmore (7 wkts.) 97.....Radlett 48

Radlett were given first use of a tricky wicket and K. H. Chapman had the new ball. He took a wicket with his first ball, and in his first five overs took five wickets without conceding a run. In all he had seven for eight. Set to score 46 runs, Stanmore were soon in difficulties, and lost five wickets before they won the match. T. Down, of Radlett, too, found the conditions favourable to his arts and took six wickets for 23 runs Score:
