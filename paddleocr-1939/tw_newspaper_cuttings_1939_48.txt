## E. T. KILLICK'S GOOD INNINGS AGAINST ST. ALBANS.

The Rev. E. T. Killick, the England, Middlesex and Cambridge University player, batted splendidly for Letchworth at Clarence Park, on Saturday. He had made 72 not out when Letchworth, who batted first, declared with 153 for 4. A 8. Hacker (28) and G. F. Reument (27). St. Albans opening pair, put on 57 before they were parted, but after that wickets fell quickly and at the close of the game, which was left drawn. St. Albans had made 92 for 5.

St. Albans beat the Old Albanians by three wickets at Clarence Park, on Sunday. With J. T. Marritt, who took 6 wickets for 22 runs in eight overs, bowling well. ## L. O. KING'S "HAT TRICK."

I. O. King, of Radlett, performed the "hat tick" for the Herts County Club on Friday, when they beat St. Albans by 184 runs. Taking seven wickets for 33, he was mainly responsible for St. Albans' cheap dismissal. King also batted well, his 67 being the top score of the game. The County Club lost two. wickets for 14, but J. A. R. Oliver (56) and King, whose partnership realised 104, won the game without further loss.

## BIG STAND FOLLOWS CHEAP WICKETS.

The County Club's match at Halleybury ended in a draw. The College batted first and the game opened sensationally, for Oliver got Chanter low off the first ball and had Souter caught at the wicket after three runs had been scored, but the third-wicket partnership realised 118, and Halleybury declared with 194 for 6. The County Club went for the runs, but a couple of stoppages owing to rain thwarted them, for at the close, with 6 wickets in hand, they still required 25 for victory. Scores:—

## K. A. GRIFFITHS BATS WELL FOR OLD BOYS.

The Old Albanians' match with Abbot's Langley, on Saturday, was left drawn. F. J. Glenister, R. G. Simons, A. W. Briers and F. Carlile all batted well for Abbot's Langley, while K. A. Griffiths (61 not out) was in capital form for the Old Boys. His opening partnership with R. F. George (32) yielded 61. Two more wickets fell etemly, but with G. F.
