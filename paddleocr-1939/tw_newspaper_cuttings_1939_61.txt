## WEDNESDAY'S WIN ON SUNDAY!

Sandridge again disappointed their supporters on Sunday by losing their home game with Radlett Wednesday by 44 runs. Dismissed in ten overs for 32, no member of the Sandridge team reached double figures, Caustin (9) and Roon (8) being top scooters. Radlett scored 26 of the necessary runs before the first wicket fell. Sampson going on first change then secured 3 wickets for 6 runs, finishing up with 4 for 12. W. Lamb subsequently hit out, and he and Hensley added 21 for the last wicket. The fact that two Sandridge players come their principal run-setter and the other who was holding up his end nicely were run out had disastrous effect on the side, but the bowling of Brennan was exceptionally good. ## A SETBACK FOR ELSTREE.

Flatree lost unexpectedly to the Old Johnlans on Saturday, E. Evans (6-32) was in great form with the ball and the Old Boys, who batted first, had a disastrous time. The first wicket fell at 11 and seven men were out for 14, E. Lumley (10) helped E. Keston (36) to stop the not, but the eighth wicket fell at 24. J. Stleb (15) and Keston took the total to 48 for 9, and J. Leach, who made one not out, kept his end up while Keston lifted the total to 74 before being bowled by J. Milner.

W. H. Ridout (22) and J. Milner (10) put on 30 in an opening partnership for Elstree, but things went badly after that and five men were out for 39. A. Wilshire (15) lifted the score to 54 for 6, but D. Kerraus (6-37) and C. Leigh (4-17) got on top again and the innings closed for 62. Scores:—
