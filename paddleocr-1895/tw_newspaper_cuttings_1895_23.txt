# BERKSHRE CHRONICLE: SATURDAY 24 AUGUST 1895-4.

AI: DREAMTON JUNIORS v. PADWORTH JUNIORS.—The above match was played at Paldworth on Wednesday when victory rested with the visitors by 24 runs, a result mainly due to the splendid bowling of A. P. Strange and I. Holdnott, Through the kindness and generosity of the Rev W. I. and Mrs Clinton ten and refreshments were provided for the teams. Subjected are the scores:—

BUTTON'S JUNIORS v. M. P. BUTTON'S XI.—On Wednesday, while the R.S.E.C.C. first eleven were by kind invitation of Mr Murdoch playing at Backhurst against his team, the R.S.E.C.C. Jailors were engaged at Kidmore Grauge in playing a boy's eleven got together by Mr. P. Button. It was a lovely afternoon, and the ground being in first-rate order the game was greatly enjoyed. The bowling of R. Sutton, Knight, and Allen, and the batting of Bowery, R. Sutton, and Mounds were particularly noticeable. The teams were hospitably entertained by Mr Sutton at the close of the match.
