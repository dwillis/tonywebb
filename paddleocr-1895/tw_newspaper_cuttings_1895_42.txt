# CAMBRIDGE EXPRESS: SATURDAY 17 AUGUST 1895-2

## CAMBRIDGE TOWN COUNCIL v. COUNTY COUNCIL.

This match was played on Trinity College Cricket Ground, on Thursday, and resulted in favour of the Town, who baited first, and sent H. J. Gray and W. Adams to the wickets. H. S. French played well for the winners, and Pearce and Fordham for the losers, the latter playing a not-out innings of 30. The match was suggested by the Mayor of Cambridge (T. Hyde Hills, Esq.)
