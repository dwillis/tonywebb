Wichenford v. St. John's 2nd XI.—This return match was played at Wichenford on July 18, and resulted in a win for Wichenford by 10 runs. He, John's batted first, the bowling of Messrs. Hobson and Williams proving a great deal too effectual for the opponents. Wichenford found a good point in the water billif. Messrs. Buckland, Holcom, and Jones Williams batted well for Wichenford; and Messrs. H. Appleyard, Parke, and Williams batted well for St. John's.
