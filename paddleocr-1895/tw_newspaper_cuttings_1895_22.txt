THANKS BANK IRON WORKS v. NOROUGH POLICE.—This match was on the Reading C.C. ground on Tuesday and resulted in a win for the Iron Works by an innings and 6 runs, making the sixth consecutive victory for them. For the winners E. Gradden batted splendidly for 77, not out. J. Lightfoot took 5 wickets for 3 runs, and E. Underwood 9 wickets for 16 runs. For the losers Grey took 6 wickets. Score:—
