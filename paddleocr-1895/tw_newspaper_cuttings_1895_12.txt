# DUNSTABLE ADVERTISER: SATURDAY 31 AUGUST 1895-2

DUNSTABLE SECOND ELEVEN V. CARTER'S O.C.—The long list of victories won by the Town second eleven was augmented on Saturday by another win, when Carter's O.C. was defeated on the Town ground, after a good game, by 40 runs. The batting of the homesters was good, Messrs. Love, Gray, Tompkins, Pouitou and Wright played a good game. For the visitors H. Bunker played well, as also did E. Mooring and J. Timm. Score:—
