# NEWCASTLE DAILY JOURNAL: WEDNESDAY 5 JUNE 1895-2

Eppleton 2nd v Hamsteels 1st.—Played at Eppleton, and resulted in a victory for the home team by 63 runs, Fife securing eight wickets for 19 runs. Score:—Eppleton 2nd, 95; Hamsteels 1st, 32.

Tudhoe v. Norton 2nd.—Yesterday Norton 2nd paid a visit to Tudhoe to play their return friendly. The game was commenced at 12 c'clock, when the home team went to the wickets. Jnerson bowled well for the visitors, taking five wickets for 9 runs. Score:—Tudhoe, 63; Norton, 77.

Morpeth YMCA v Ashington Excelior.—At Newbigger on Whit-Monday. The YMCA batted first, and declared their innings closed with 104 for five wickets. Ashington on going to the wickets completely broke down before the bowling of R J Carr and M Quinn, eight wickete falling for 1 run, the total only reaching 10. R J Carr doing the "hat" trick. Score: Morpeth YMCA, 104 (for five wickets); Ashington Excelior, 10.

## HETTON LYONS CLUB.

On Saturday afternoon the members of this club met in front of their new pavilion (which has recently been erected) to take part in the opening ceremony. There was a large attendance of the members and spectators. Mr John Burn, the hon. secretary, was called upon to preside. He called upon Mr John Brown, one of the oldest members of the club, to make a presentation to Mr G W Ross, engineer to the Hetton Coal Company. The present consisted of a gold-plated key and a solid silver salver, in consideration of his services in furthering the erection of the pavilion, and other kind services rendered to the club during the enlargement of the ground.—Mr Ross suitably replied.—The proceedings terminated with a match between the Hetton Lyons eleven and Mr Ross. Sixteen, and resulted in a win for Mr Ross' team by one run. Score: Hetton Lyons, 91; M Ross' team, 92.
