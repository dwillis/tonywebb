For Sawston, J Bailey was the only scorer, with five runs to his credit, extras being credited with six. ## MARRIED V. SINGLE.

On Tuesday a second match was played upon the same ground between Eleven Married and Eleven Single, Mr Howe himself taking the bat with the Married Eleven. It was a well contested game, the Single team being the victors by 5 runs only. F Baker was successful with both ball and bat. Refreshments were dispensed to players and spectators, and in the evening a substantial supper was provided for all that had taken part in the game. About 30 sat down, and Mr Howe joined the company. The tables being cleared, Mr Blake, in appropriate words, proposed the health of the host, which was drunk with musical honours. A social and harmonious evening was spent, such as Sutton ericketers have not often the opportunity of spending. Score: $ ___. J Marriott, run out .. C Dunkling, b Darby .. H E Howe, b Darby ..
