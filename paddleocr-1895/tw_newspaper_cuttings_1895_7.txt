## VOLUNTEE AT CHICKET.

A match was played at Churn Camp on Wednesday last week, between the Luton Detachment and the Hefreinder of the 3rd Volunteer Battalion, resulting in a win for Luton by 28 runs. Scores: - Luton Detachment, 75; Remainder of Battalion, 47.

## HOOKLIFE

Cyclist.—A cricket match was played at Hockliff on Saturday, between the local cricket eleven and a Woburn team, resulting in a victory for the home team, the scores being:—Hockliff, 96; Woburn, 75.
