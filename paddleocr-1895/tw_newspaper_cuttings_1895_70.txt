# ORIOKET.

Flymouth gained an easy victory over Stonehouse yesterday by 99 runs, scoring 121 to 22.

The return match between Torquay and H.M.S. Britannia was decided at Torquay yesterday, and resulted in a victory for the home team, who scored 100 to the ship's 78. A. Dmaley (28; not out) batted well for the winners, whilst W. J. Troughton (32) did well for the Britannia.

Mr. Kinderley took a team to Exmouth on Wednesday, but were beaten by the first two men of Exmouth's side. The visitors scored 110, and Copleston and Salter put on 132 for the first wicket. At the call of time the home XI, had made 253 for three wickets. Scores:—
