# South Wales DAILY STAR: Monday 19 August 1895-2.

BASSALEG V. NEWPORT BANKS. This annual match, played at Bassaleg on Thursday, resulted, as the subjoined score shows, in a narrow victory for the home team. For the home team Jones captured four wickets. Found, for the visitors, being accredited the same number. Kodney Williams and H. G. Davies were respectively the top scorers.

CAXTONIANS v. NEWPORT NORTHERN. This match was played on the Marhes on Saturday. This being the first match for the Caxtonians, much interest was manifested by their friends, a large number of whom put in an appearance. The feature of the game was the splendid bowling of B.T. Smith. Both eleven's were very keen in the fielding.

## RANJITSINHJI IN BRIGHTON.

At the conclusion of the Sussex v.
