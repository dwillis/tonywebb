Played at the fete at Shotley Spa on Whit-Monday, the game being watched by a vast assemblage of spectators. Elswick won the toss, and Conset began batting with Williamson and Siddell, to the bowling of Elsey and Robinson. In the third ever Williamson was dismissed without a run having been scored. Godfrey joined Siddell, when a splendid stand was made, 68 runs being recorded before Siddell was caught by Robinson for a capital 32, he having played all the bowling with the greatest ease and confidence. Godfrey was the only one who subsequently offered any defence, getting 61 before he was clean bowled by Elsey. The Conset total reached 126. Only an hour remained for batting when Elswick began operations to the bowling of Jones and Wood. After batting just over an hour Elswick completed their innings for 28 runs. James took four wickets for 9 runs, and Wood six wickets for 18. Secret:
