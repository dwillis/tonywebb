## GULVAL V GOLDSITHNEY.

Played at Gulval on Friday, the home team winning mainly through the batting of R E Hichens and W Tregoning, and the bowling of Robert Tregoning, who took seven wickets for 13 runs, performing the “hat trick” with the three last balls. For Goldsmithey, the Rev W H Hughes played a useful innings of 16 not out. Scores:—

## KEHELLAND v. LELANT.

The return match was played at Kehelland on Saturday. The ground is not apparently suitable for crickets, which somewhat interfered with the play of the visitor's The Visitors, however, played a good game, Messrs H Howard Batten, J Maxwell Batten, and Osborne being the most conspicious. It will be remembered that when the Visitors on Saturday were the homestars, they suffered defeat, it being the only time Lelant has been defeated. Kehelland's chief feature was their bowling, for which Chinn and Pendray deserve great praise.

DANIEL'S ELEVEN v. BREWER'S ELEVEN.—Played at Penzance, and resulted in a win for Daniel's eleven, which scored 64, against 42 knocked up by their opponents. For the winners T. D. White (13), O. Hall (10), W. Daniel (8), W. Knight (6), and C. Thomas (6) were the chief scorers, and for the losers B. Jenkin (29) was the principal contributor. Thomas and Daniel bowled well for the winners and Jenkin and B. Sower for the losers.
