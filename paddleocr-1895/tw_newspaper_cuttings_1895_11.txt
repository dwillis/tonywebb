# DUNSTABLE BOROUGH GAZETTE: WEDNESDAY 28 AUGUST 1895-3.

Played at Dunstable on Thursday, and ended in a win for the home team by 24 runs on the first innings. The batting of the visitors was exceedingly feeble; the only one who seemed able to play the bowling was Samus, who carried his bat for 11; he also took five of the home wickets. The runs for the win were mainly from the bats of three men—Anderson 15, Love 13, and Marohant 10. In bowling, Suthit took seven for 18 runs, Gray two for 18.

The town second eleven added one more to their long list of victories, on Saturday last, on the town ground, by defeating Carter's C.C. by 45 runs after a good game. The home batting was fairly good, the principal scores being Love 21, Gray 21, Tompkins 14, Poulton 10, and Wright 18 (not out). On the visitors' side Junker played well for 21, as also did Mooring and Timm for 11 and 10 respectively. In bowling Giddings for Carter's took six of the town wickets at a small cost, while the wickets of the visitors were equally divided among four of the home bowlers.

## DUNSTABLE VOLUNTEERS v. LUTON DETACHMENT.

This match was played at Luton on Saturday and resulted in an easy win for Dunastalo. Luton batted first, and were all disposed of for 19 runs, owing to the very successful bowling of Corporal Smith. Dunstable scored 71, thus winning by 52 runs. The best scores for the winners were made by Corporal Smith and Private Bucket (21 each). The following comprised the Dunstable team: Colour Script, Elliott, Sergt. R. Hambling, Bergt. W. D. Hambling, Corporal Smith, Private Bunker, Private Sharrat, Private Bonham, Private Burch, Private Darby.
