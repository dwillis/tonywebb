## MADRON 2 $ ^{nd} $ XI V. LELANT.

The return match Madron 2nd XI v. Lelant was played on the ground of the first named. Madron was first to take the batting against the bowling of Perry and Dunstan and contributed 35. (S. Mollard 13 and E. Nicholls 10.) The Lelant team then went to the wicket to wipe off the runs, but so well did Friggens and Tregoning bowl, that they were all out for 19 runs. I. Adams kept his wicket to perfection, as he did not let a ball pass him. Friggens wickets only cost him 8 runs.

FALMOUTH v. REDRUTH — This match was played at Falmouth. The home team, with six wickets down, declared their innings closed at 90, and Redruth, forcing the game, won on the stroke of time by two runs, making a total of 92.

SENNEN V. NEW DOWNS LARKS.—A match, Sennen v. the New Downs Larks, was played at Bollowall on Saturday in a field belonging to Captain Boyne, of Boswidden. The visitors were the first to handle the willcw, and succeeded in running up a good score. The home team, whose prospects in the earlier part of their innings did not appear bright, eventually woke up, and by the help of Semmens and Chirgwin, who did well, scored a victory.
