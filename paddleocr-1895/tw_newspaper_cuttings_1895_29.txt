# FARINGDON ADVERTISER: SATURDAY 2 NOVEMBER 1895.

CRICKET CLUB—The annual dinner of the Lechlade Cricket Club, was held at the New Inn Hotel, on Thursday evening last. About 24 members and friends sat down to the very excellent repast provided by Mrs Eichens, to which ample justice was done. The proceedings were presided over by Mr H. W. Prior-Wandesford, who was supported by Captain Tosswill, Dr. J. O. March, Mr W. Crosland, the vice-chair being occupied by Mr H. Nilward. It being the first time the president had been present at the dinner, he most generously provided after dinner a very old-fashioned but acceptable drink viz., “rum punch,” with which the toasts were drunk. The following was the toast list. “The Queen,” by the chairman, “The President,” by Mr H. Milward, responded to by the chairman, “Lechlade Cricket Club,” by the chairman, responded to by Mr S. Campbell and Mr G. A. Davis, who, as is usual, read the averages for batting and bowling during the season, “Donor of Prizes,” by Mr Davis, responded to by Mr H. Milward and Mr S. Campbell, “Tradesmen of Lechlade,” by Captain Tosswill, responded to by Mr F. Strond, “The Visitors,” by Mr Crosland, responded to by Dr. J. O. March, “The Ladies,” by Mr T. Slatter, responded to by Mr E. Bowley, “Mrs Richens,” by Mr B. F. Innoosent, responded to by Mr Richens, “Vice-President” (Mr H. Milward), by the chairman, “Captain,” by Mr Davis, responded to by Mr B. F. Innoosent. During the evening, Mr Campbell and Mr Milward presented the bat and ball, which was won by Mr W. Johns, to his father, Mr J. Johns, to be forwarded. Some capital songs were sung by the following—Captain Tosswill, Mr W. Crosland, Messrs. B. E. Innoosent, Gibbs, Hudson, and Davis.
