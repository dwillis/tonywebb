# BERKSHIRE CHRONICLE: SATURDAY 10 AUGUST 1895-2.

## READING CRICKET WEEK.

A new departure in connection with the Reading Cricket Club has been inaugurated this year in the direction of a cricket week. The matches have been played during the last few days, and the cricket week has been a conspicuous success. Reading have been able to rely on excellent teams for each of their matches, such powerful exponents of the summer pastime as Capt. Nepan, Mr L. G. A. Collins, Mr H. J. Collins, Mr W. O. Nares, Mr P. E. Chapman, Rev C. E. Chapman, F. W. Finch, F. Doane, rendering assistance, &c., and some interesting cricket has been witnessed.

## First Match—v. Many know C.U.

The work opened with this annual fixture against the M.O.O. The visitors had the assistance of four professionals, Landford, Pike, Sherwin, and Hylott, and two members of the Reading Club, Mr P. T. Baker and Mr Wm. Greenhold. Mr O. T. Murdoch, M.P., captained the visiting team.

Inclement weather greatly interfered with the game, and it had to be finally abandoned. Reading went in first to bat, and thanks mainly to A. Much (85), H. J. Collins (25), H. O. A. Collins (27), succeeded in compuling 186 runs for nine wickets, when they declared their innings closed. M.O.O. then went in, but after scoring 16 for one wicket, stumps were drawn owing to the rain, Mrs Murdoch kindly entertained the teams at lunch and tea. In addition to Mr Murdoch, M.P.,

Third Match—V. Mr W. Howard Palmer's XI.—This match was commenced on Wednesday on the Rending Biscuit Factory C.C. Ground. Heading had spared no effort in getting together a really powerful eleven, whilst Mr Palmer had whipped up an exceedingly strong team, including those well-known professionals, Bacon, of Hampshire, and Handford, of Notts. Some good play was again witnessed on Thursday by a large gathering. In the end Mr Palmer's eleven won by 35 runs.
