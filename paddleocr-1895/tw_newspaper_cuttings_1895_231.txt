## MALVERN COLLEGE v. M.C.O.

This two-days' match, which concluded at Malvern, on Saturday, was remarkable for the small score of the Collegians in their second innings, and 42 is about the lowest record associated with the Malvernians. M.C.C. had the assistance of Woodcock and Whiteside, from Ivesetemshire. The former took seven wickets in the second innings, and a grandly-played century total by the Rev. H. T. Thornton, together with some useful scores, left M.C.C. winners at the finish by all innings and 68 runs.
