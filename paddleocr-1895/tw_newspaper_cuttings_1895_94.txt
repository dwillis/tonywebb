# South Wales Daily News: Monday 5 August 1895-2.

## OENTLENEN OF MONMOUTHSUIRE v. R. M. E. MILITIA.

This two-day match, which was played on Lord Trelegas Park, was concluded on Saturday, and owing chiefly to the bowling of Porter, and the fine batting displays by G. F. Harding and H. Bircham, ended right on time in a victory for the Gentlemen by five wickets and 75 runs. Scores:—

On Monday next the Bristol Schoolmasters will meet a fairly strong Cardiff eleson on the Cardiff Army Park. Wickets pitched at 11.30 sharp. The Cardiff team is as follows. Lowe accompanying the 2nd on their tour to Warminster, France, and Wolfe, while J. H. Brain will play for Fairwater: = J. G. Clarke (capt.) T. M. Barlow, Selwyn Bigga, S. Sweet-Escott, V. A. Hickley, W. W. F. Pullen, A. H. Mann, E. W. Hodge, C. C. McCall, J. E. McKay, and E. H. Schle.
