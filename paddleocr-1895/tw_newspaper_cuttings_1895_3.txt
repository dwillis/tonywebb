SILSOR V. MAULDEN.—The return match was played in West Park, Silsoo, on Thursday afternoon, Silsoo again proving victorious by nearly 100 runs on the first innings. For the winners, J. Gray made 30, M. Squire 23, H. Squire 18, and A. Bottoms 16 not out. Maulden were dismissed for 19, Gudgin taking 6 wickets for 6 runs, and Porter 3 for 10 runs. In their second innings, Maulden compiled 37 for 8 wickets.

Houghton C.C.: MARRIED V. SINGLE.—Played at Houghton on Monday. Noore:—Single, 39; Married, 100 for seven wickets.

MISSERS FORDNE'S EMPLOYEES.—A match between the employees of Sowell Line Works and Blows Down Line Works was played at Houghton Green on Saturday, and resulted in a win for the Sowell men by one run, the scores being 61 and 60 respectively.

WATERLOW'S V St. MATTHEW'S (LUTON),—Played at Luton on Saturday. Score;—St. Matthew's, 01; Waterlow's, 114.

DUNSTABLE 2ND XI. V. MARKYATE STREET.—Played at Cheverells on Saturday. Rain considerably interfered with the play, and when Dunstable had made 70 for the loss of two wickets, the villagers declared they had had enough of it, and stumps were drawn.
