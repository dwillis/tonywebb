The eleven has played 23 matches, won 12, lost 6, and drawn 5, having scored 3,803 for 207 wickets, an average of 10.25 runs per wicket, against 2,371 for 232 wickets, an average of 10.22 per wicket, by their opponents. Four times they scored over 200, six times over 160, and six times over 100, whilst their rivals never reached 200 only five times exceeded 150, and six times exceeded 100. The highest score was 253 for 7 wickets, and the lowest 60. The highest score against them was 178 for 7 wickets, the lowest 25. R H Jackson has taken 14 catches at the wicket, while in the field H A Turner caught 10, A T Cliff and F W Farfitt S and F G Clarke and J Hodge 7. FIRST ELEVEN.
