# South Buchs Free Press: Friday 16 August 1895-4.

## BLACKWELL, HEATH 2nd XL. v. LITTLE MARLOW. LOW SOORING.

A match between the above clubs was played on the ground of the latter on Saturday last, and resulted in a victory for Flackwell Heath by 25 runs on the first innings. The batting of Musk, Baker, and Smith was very good. Almond accomplished the hat trick, taking 8 wickets in 34ns. active balls. Clifford also bowled well. The second innings of the latter proved most disastrous, they being dismissed in 31 minutes for 9 runs. No doubts the result would have been different had time permitted a second innings for the visitors. Davis and Penny batted in good form. In the second innings of Flackwell Heath Almond again accomplished the hat trick, and took 6 wickets for 1 run. Penny obtained 4 for 6 runs. Score:

## MARLOW v. HIGH WYCOME.

## BIG NITTING BY H. H. MASSIE.

The return match between these old rivals was played at Marlow, on Saturday, in fine weather, on a wishes that favoured the bowlers. It will be remembered that the first match at Wycombe was left drawn much in favour of Marlow. On this occasion the riverside town gained an easy victory, thanks to a fine innings by H. H. Massie, the Australian batsman (who is staying at Marlow this summer), and some excellent bowling by W. H. Lannon and Barber. Marlow won the toss, Langley and Barber facing the deliveries of Page and H. B. Parker. Runs came steadily until the total reached 21, when both batmen were out with a dose to their credit. Massie and Lannon advanced the total to 55, when the latter was caught by Whites, and as Ballsile failed to score, and Want only hit a 4, five wickets were down for 62. Bower then came to the aid of Massie, and a useful stand was effected. The 10 went up amidst cheers, but with four added a great change came over the game, H. B. Parker sending back Browor, Burton, and Mackenzie in one over. Six, seven, and eight for 104. Massie was now hitting with tremendous force, and was especially severe on Page, a girantic hit for 6 into Court Garden delighting the outlookers hugely. Morgan batted steadily while the Australian hit until the total was 140, when he was joked by Parker. Just when it appeared certain that Massie would take out his hat he was captured in the slips, and the innings closed for 146. Massie's dashing score of 19 included two 6's, eight 4's, two 8's, and eight 3's. He was deservedly applauded on retiring to the pavilion. Both Parker and Keen bowled well. Wycombe commenced their innings with F. M. Parker and Langley. The bowlers were W. H. Lannon and Barber. A more disastrous start could not well be imagined. Lannon caught and bowled Parker and clean bowled Baines in his first over. Two for 0. G. George scored a couple of singles, and Langley was then caught by Mackenzie off Barber. Three for 2. H. B. Parker helped George to put a better aspect upon affairs, but the former was out at 23, after hitting up 16. James followed in and was bowled by Lannon at 28. The brothers George then got together and carried the score to 40, when three wickets fell—the George brothers and White, all clean bowled. Page hit up 14 by means of a 6, a 4, and two 2's, and the innings closed for 60. Lannon bowled uncommonly well, his seven wickets costing 51 runs each. Wycombe followed on, and made 50 for one wicket. Langley batted well for 35 not out. Marlow therefore won by 16 runs on the first innings—a rather unexpected result, seeing that the team which was in the field was by no means a strong one. Score and analysis:

## BLYTHEWOOD v. CIPPEXHAM.

This match, at Blythewood, on Saturday last, proved a very close one, Cippeham only winning by 5 runs. Doing in first Blythewood was got rid of for 22, A. E. Kent (not out, 9) doing best. Cippeham got 37, and a remarkable piece of play was D. O. Greyay's, who went in first and carried his hat for only three runs. For the winners F. Woodley took to wickets for 15 runs and J. Gregory 1 for 7. In a second attempt Blythewood obtained 1b runs for the loss of two wickets. First (for Blythewood) secured 4 wickets for 5 runs and Baker 4 for 14.
