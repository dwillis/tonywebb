# STORBRIDGE COUNTY EXPRESS: SATURDAY 10 AUGUST 1895-3.

## SPORTS AND PASTIMES

## ORIOKET.

Secretaries of clubs will oblige by forwarding lists of fixtures to the head office. Reports of matches should reach us not later than Wednesday afternoon, and should be written on one side of the paper only. Names should be distinctly written, to prevent mistakes. Birmingham League matches are denoted thus "B.L." and "H.J.L." denotes Birmingham Junior League.

## Fixtures non To-day.

## Innings closed 178

BIRMINGHAM LEAGUE—DIVISION II. The reserves of Kidderminster and Dudley met at Dudley, and owing to the rain this likewise ended in a draw.

MITOHELL'S V. STOURBRIDGE.—Played on Saturday, at Cape Hill. T. Stevenson bowed well for the home team, taking four wickets for 27 runs, this including the hat trick. J. Hingley bowled splendidly for Stourbridge, and had three for 16. Owing to the rain, the game was stopped in an interesting condition. Score:—

STOURBRIDGE (2ND) v. MITOHKELL'S (2ND).—Played at Amblecote, on Saturday. Bowen, James, and Brooks batted well for their respective soots. A very interesting match ended in a draw. No doubt the town team would have won had time allowed of the match being played out.
