NORTHERN NOMADS ▼ THE BORDERERS.

These teams met on Mr Bowick's ground at Close House yesterday. Hatting first, the Nomads ran up a total of 228, chiefly by the fine play of P Ashworth (85) and H Fenner Fort (57), both of whom scored freely off the bowling, their partnership caring the score from 114 to 219.

On the Borderers going in wickets fell in rapid succession until Fogg-Elliot and O Iiddell became associated, when nine men were out for 67. By steady play they increased the total to 102, when the heavy rainfall caused the game to be abandoned, thus rubbing the Nomads of a certain victory. Score:—
