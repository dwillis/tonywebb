## BIDMOUTH v. M.O.C.

This match was concluded as Bldmouth yesterday, the result being a draw. The home eleven batted the first day compiling 24, to which E.A. Nepan contributed 121, and the Rev. E. B. Brutton 61, both playing admirable oricket. When stumps were drawn M.C.U. had made 2 runs for no wickets. Resuming yesterday, they closed their tunings for 192. Carlin and Titchmarsh handled the willow finely. C. Wells did the "hat trick," bowling from the east end of the pitch, a performance which gained him warm applause from a large concourse of onlookers. The weather was all that could be desired, and the wicket in perfect condition.
