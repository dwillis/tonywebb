## WUOMBE Y.M.O.A. v. WYOOMBE PRIMITIVES.

This match was played on the Rye on Saturday last, and resulted in a win for the Y.M.C.A. by 30 runs on the first innings. For the winners, Tubb (20) was highest scorer. Neal taking five wickets for 10 runs and Britnell five for 20, while neither of the losers attained double figures in the first innings. In the second innings the Young Men made 83, and the Primitives 44 for 6 wickets.

## MR. W. PEARCE'S (WYCOMBE) XI. v. BOUTHALL.

On Saturday last Mr. W. Pearce, of the "Nap"s Head, London-road, captained an eleven to visit Southall to try conclusions with an eleven representing the Southall Gas Works. Although the Bucks men made a fine innings, having 112 runs to their credit, Southall were far and away the best team, and for the loss of only 3 wickets they had contributed 157 runs. Dixon and Hudson were said to be classed as belonging to the Middlesex team of Colts, and the way in which Dixon contributed his 76 showed that he was a hatman of no mean orlier. His total included one 5, three 4's, fifteen 3's, and five 2's. William Cooper, too, knocked up eight boundaries in succession. In fact, against such formlable opponents the bowling of the Wyombre representative was taxed to its utmost; but, nevertheless, a pleasant game was the result. Both W. Pearce and O. W. Harper showed good form for the loers. Score and analysis:—

## MR. COLLINS CLASS v. BOOKER TEMPERANCE.

The return match between these clubs took place at Booker on Saturday last, and a pleasant game resulted in a win for the Class by eight runs on the first innings. The meeting of these teams has usually been a close game; last year it was a win for the Class and a drawn match, and this year the result of the fixtures has been the same. Eatting first, the Class made 50, J. Keen (23) and Collins (10) being chief contributors. Booker then went in, and the total had reached 42 when the last wicket fell, out of which number Burnham had made 1d. In their second venture the Class could only compile 27, neither bateman being able to make a stand against the under-arm bowling of U. Pearce; Heather (15) was the only one to get into double figures.
