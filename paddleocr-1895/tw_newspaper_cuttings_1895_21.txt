# READING STANDARD: FRIDAY 23 AUGUST 1895-3.

Guldhall C.C. v Newtown C.C. — These club, met on Saturday in their return fixture at Sandleford park The game, which ended in a win for Guildhall, was remarkable for low scoring, the bowlers taking everything before them, in addition to which the fielding of both sides was good. Humphries and Lucas for Guildhall bowled splendidly, the former capturing 12 wickets for as many runs, and the latter 7 for 8. Allsop and Williamson took the bowling honors for Newtown. The teams were entertained on the ground, and a hearty vote of thanks was accorded Lord Arthur and Lady Butler (who were present) for their kindness, also to Mr Allsop. Score: —

Parish Church Institute v Highclear.—The return match between these teams was played, by kind permission, on the cricket ground on Saturday in splendid weather. Highclere winning the loss decided to bat first, but owing to the bowling of Gunning and Gayler, the first four wickets fell for 21, when Collins came in and made a useful stand, necessitating several changes in the bowling. Still runs came, Collins doing the lion's share, but Gunning going on at the top end fairly beat him with a beauty, whilst the Thirkills made some useful stands. After the interval the Institute started batting with Cook and Pyke, the former hitting his wicket, whilst Pyke contented himself by keeping up his wicket. Mashin the next man, made things more lively putting on 18 in quick time. The others making double figures were Pyke, Young and Gunning. Both teams were entertained to ten, through the kindness of Miss L. Godding and Mr H Goddard. Score: —

SREZK v. BURGHCLERE.—This match was played at Spreon on Saturday. Speen, winning the toss, sent Awhby and Prebody to the wickets, the former being unfortunately bowled; the ball hitting him on the chest and rebounding on to the wickets. A. Smith then joined Frebody and a long-stand-was made. Spreon putting on 148 before the innings was declared closed. Both Smith and Frebody played good oricket for their respective scores, 71 and 64. Burgholere then went in and totalled 63 before being dismissed. Sherman and Storie did best. Frebody was most successful with the ball, obtaining five wickets. Speen thus won a pleasant game by 85 runs and nine wickets.
