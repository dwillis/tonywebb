## The Wycombe Y.M.O.A. O.O. play Newland Bible Class on the Hye to tomorrow (Saturday). Wickets pitched at 2.80.

Wyombe First XI, have no fixture for to-morrow but the Reserves go to Marlow to play Borlhes School (return). On Thursday—not Wednesday as at first arranged—the Grammar School Fast and Present play the Wyombe Club, on which occasion we hear that Mr. and Mrs. Peachell have kindly offered to provide inboon and tea for the teams. This match ought to prove a good even game, as several of the Club's First XI, are old boys and will play for the School. We also hear that on Saturday week, when the final in the Bastick Cup Competition takes place, the Vicar and Mrs. Shaw intend inviting all the members of the Club to partake of tea. Mrs. Shaw has consented to present the Cup and medals at the conclusion of the game, but we shall be able next week to give fuller details of the arrangements for the day.

St. John's C.O. play the West End United vs the Reaction Ground tomorrow at 2:30.—St. John's C.O. want matches for the Saturdays in August.—R. Ford, West End road, Wyrombe.

Church from O.O. play Wheeler Knd Elue Star, at Wheeler End, to tomorrow, at 2:30.
