At Dean Park, United v. St. Andrew's.

Poole Park gained a very easy victory over an eleven representing the L. and S.W.R. Co. at Bournemouth East, on Saturday. The visitors were beaten at every department of the game, their batting display especially being very poor. Poole, who started batting, did not look like making a big score, for they lost several good men for just over 20, but when Adams and Myers became associated a charge came over the game. The pair hit out in fine style, and before a separation was effected the Poole score had reached a respectable number. Myers was the first to leave, his share being 33. Adams still continued to hit out, but with the fall of another wicket, the home captain declared, the total standing at 124 for six wickets, with Adams 64 not out. This is the highest score Adams has ever obtained on the Park. The visitors started badly, and did not improve as time went on, the fine bowling of Gill, sen., and Myers being much too good for them. PLEWELL PARK v. BOLDE C.C.—This match was played at Pylewell on August 24th, and resulted in an easy win for the home team by an innings and 14 runs. The visitors, who won the toss, elected to bat, and were dismissed for the miserable total of 30. Lane, who alone reached double figures, batted well for his 16. Plowman bowled with deadly effect, taking 6 wickets for 9 runs. The home team were not disposed of until they had made 106, of which Shephard and Hibbert were the chief contributors, making 21 and 30 respectively. The visitors were more fortunate in their second venture, and made 62 before they were all out. Lane again reaching double figures, leaving Pylewell easy winners as above stated. Scores:
