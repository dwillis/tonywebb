## STOCKPORT AND DISTRICT SEIELD COMPLETION. FINAL I.E.

STOCKPORT CONGRESSIONAL V. REDDISH ST. ELIASBURY'S.—This interesting event, for a beautiful shield presented by Mr. Sydney Hollins, was commenced on the football ground adjoining the Stockport Oricket Ground on Saturday afternoon. The pitch, which had been prepared for the occasion, was saturated by the rain, and all in favour of the bowlers. The Congregational have vanquished the Lads' Club and St. Peter's, whilst for St. Elizabeth's position they have had to meet and defeat the Lads' Club 2nd and last year's winners, Hanover. The Congregational batted first, sending in Dowbiggin and B. Jebson, they having to face the bowling of Hardman and Hunt. Both batemen hit well. The inning only realised 49.
