# DEVIZES AND WILTSHIRE GAZETTE: THURSDAY 12 SEPTEMBER

1895-2.

## WILTS COUNTY ASYILUM CRICKET SEASON, 1895.

With a drawn game against Erchfont, the Asylum eleven closed their season on Saturday. They have played 21 matches, winning 11, losing 5, and 5 were drawn. The wins were against Rowde, Allanings (thrice), Erchfont, Holt, Wilts Depot, Manton, Monkton Farleigh, Rodwell Rovers, and Wilts County Police; the losses were against Powsey (twice), Corham, Rev. Ransome's XI, and Mr. Bolwell's XI, while matches against Holt, Rodwell Rovers, Westwood, Wilts Depot, and Erchfont were drawn. The total number of runs scored for the Asylum was 2,402 for 198 wickets, an average per wicket of 12.1, and their opponents 2,012 for 223, an average of 9.1.
