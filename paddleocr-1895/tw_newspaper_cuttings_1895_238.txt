WICHENFORD v. T. O. RICHENFORD XL.—This match was played at Laughern Hill on Monday, and ended in a win for Wichenford by 15 runs after a most exciting match. Mr. Richens won the time and went in, and put together a very smart 53. Meere. Harris and Humphreys playing well for their runs. Wichenford then went in and began disastrously, as six wickets were down for 5, but when B. Preero joined J. Page an excellent stand was made, the former hitting out with perfect ease. G. A. Fidos was the next partner but one, and when these two got together they hit the bowlers to all parts of the field and eventually won the match. B. Preero's fine innings included two 4's, two 3's, nine 2's, and nine singles. Mr. F. I. Harkness kept wicket for the levers in first-rate style. Williams and Caswell again bowled very effectively for Wichenford.

Wichenford v. Cathedral Guild.—This match was played at Laughern Hill, and ended in an easy win for Wichenford by 54 runs and 5 wildets. The Guild won the loss and elected to bat on a good wicket, but were soon dismissed, thanks to the excellent bowling of W. Williams and H. Caawell. Both bowled perfectly and with an excellent length. Wichenford got 74 for six wickets and declared, Messrs Haating, T. O. Williams, and G. F. Jones Williams getting most runs. St. John's 28th XL v. Broughton.—Played at St. John's on Saturday, and resulted in an easy victory for the home team. The visitors batted first, but were disposed of for 38. Members, Robinson and Cross both bowling well, the former taking seven wickets for 18 runs, and the latter three for 12. St. John's then scored 39 for three wickets, when rain prevented further play. Members, Park, Heaumont, and Combe batted well for their respective antics. Note:
