## BOYEN HILL V. BISHEAM.

This match was played at Boyn Hill on Saturday, and resulted in a win for Risham. In their first innings Bayu Hill were disposed of for 17. No fewer than 11 of these were gained by W. Thatcher. A. Paine took 8 wickets for 4 runs, while S. T. Morton captured 6 wickets for 10 runs. Risham made 23. A. Paine contributing 11 not out. S. Gibbens took 6 wickets at a cost of 14, and Jones 3 for 5, and, in the second innings of Risham, 3 for 3. Score:—

## "FIXZ APPLE" v. "KING'S HEAD."

This match was played on Monday, August 5th, in a meadow at Bird-in-hand, kindly lest by Mr. A. Gillett, and resulted in a win for the "King's-head" by six runs and nine wickets. ## COLLEGHILL C.O. v. St. MARGARET'S C.O.

The return match between the above clash was played at Colshill on Saturday, August 10th, St. Margaret's again proving successful, which was chiefly owing to the briefness all-round cricket of Mr. C. O. Powell, who not only made the highest score for his side, but actually took 5 wickets for 6 runs. Mr. J. Dean also bowled wonderfully well, taking the other 5 for 20 runs. Colshill went to the wickets first, and were soon disposed of, Mr. E. M. Wetherty being top scorer with 11, and the innings closed for 20. St. Margaret's now commenced their task of making 20 to win, which was seen accomplished. Fountain made 11; Mr. Powell made 51, which was compiled without the slightest chance, and the innings realized 70. Colshill went to the wickets a second time, and did much better, having made 72 for 8 wickets when time was called. Full score and analysis
