# LINCOLN TUTLAND AND STAMFORD MERCURY: FRIDAY 11 OCTOBER 1895

# CRICKET.

BURGHLEY PARK CRICKET CLUB. The club played 13 matches, winning six, losing five, and two were drawn. Atkins and T. Blades bowled very well, and practically did all the work to that department. Atkins kept the ground in a lendid condition, and has been engaged again for next season.
