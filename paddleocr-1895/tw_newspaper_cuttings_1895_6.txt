# DUNSTABLE BOROUGH GAZETTE: WEDNESDAY 14 AUGUST 1895-5.

HOUGHTON & WESTONING. Played on Houghton Green on Monday, and resulted in a victory for the home team. The principal feature of the game was the excellent form of F. Loro, who carried his bat through the innings for 40; A. Cook (87) also batted well.
