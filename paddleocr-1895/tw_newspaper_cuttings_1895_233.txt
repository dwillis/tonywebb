WELLAND AND DISTRICT V. LEDBURY.—Played at Welland on 25th July. The wicket was soft, and the run came very slowly. Ledbury went in first and were all dismissed for 40, owing to the bowling of Parr and Hill. Welland started well by making 45 for three wickets, chiefly owing to Gunn (25) and Coventry (22). Farley bowled well for Ledbury.
