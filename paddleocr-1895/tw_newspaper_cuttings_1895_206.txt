## CREWE.

For two weeks rain has outforced with two of the most important fixtures on the Alexandra list, and it is still a mystery whether Grave would have beaten Nantwich, or whether Leek would have knocked the champions off their perils. Still the fact remains that the Moorlanders had made 111, while Grave had lost four of their very best bats for 32.

Judging from the remarks which have been made by the Crews contingent, it would appear they had a most enjoyable thing in the Moorlands, and I venture to predict that it would not take much permission to entice them into that district again. They speak highly of their drive to Dovedale, and also for the hospitality they received at the hands of the Leek gricketers, who did everything to make their visit a convivial one. I am given to understand "Little Jerry" was the recipient of a heavy vote of thanks for his assidious attention towards his guests.

Speaking from a cricket point of view, the most successful man on the Grave side appears to be Riley, who certainly distinguished himself as a bowler on Saturday against Look. He took seven wickets for 28 runs, while on Monday against Highfield he was also responsible for five wickets for 20 runs. The others have not done anything extraordinary.

Mr. Abraham, the financial secretary of the Alexandra Club, has just issued his annual financial statement and report for the past year. From a brief perxul it would appear that this important club is in a flourishing condition. Last year the balance carried forward was £51.5s. Ltd. while on the present occasion the balance is £119.6s.5d. This is a matter for congratulation, as no club or organization can expect to prosper without good financial prospects, which can only be obtained by good management, and this is one special feature in the history of the Crowe Alexandra Club.

Don't forget the Alexandra sports next week. There will be several important encounters, the presence of Charley Bradley, the English champion, and A. H. Downer, the Scottish champion in the 100 yards' scratch, will be worth witnessing; while E. C. Bradua, the half-mile champion, and other high-class luminaries in the athlete world should make the forthcoming festival of the Crowe Alexandra one of the best yet held.
