WELLAND AND DISTRICT V. MALVERN WELLIN.— Played at Welland, on August 1st. Malvern Wells went in first. They did not fare a' all well against the bowling of Malsell and Farr, and they were all dismined for 40s Berrington (18) and Harris (11) being the highest scoreers. Welland made 166 for seven wickets, owing to good batting by Gann (31), Lowell (40), Michael (51) not out, and Farr (50). Malvern Wells in their second innings made 55, Berrington (11), and Wright (16 not out). Bourse:

Mr. G. A. Buckland's N. V. Mr. T. C. Richens' N.I.—Played at Hewick on Monday. Mr. Buckland's N.I. batted first, and started with Meers. G. A. Buckland and F. A. Turley. With the score at 5 Turley was dismissed by a good ball from Poste. G. F. Jones Williams then joined Buckland, and the score had reached 70 before Jones Williams was howled by Watts. Meers, H. W. Buckland and H. A. Turley also batted well for the visitors. Mr. Richens' N.I. commenced to the bowling of Meers. G. A. Buckland and F. A. Turley. Six wickets were down for 41, when rain come on, and the game ended in a draw very much in favour of Mr. Buckland's N.I. For Mr. Richens T. G. Jones Williams played a fine inning, and Mr. Watts also batted well.
