WORCESTER GRAMMAR SCHOOL v. KIDDERMINITKA GRAMMAR SCHOOL.—Played at Kidderminates, on Wednesday. Although the wicket was more in favour of the batmen, being hard with only the grass wetted, the bowlers on both sides were most prominent. Kidderminster batted first, but were all dismissed for a. Thorp took five wickets for 2 runs, and Spencer five wickets for 4 runs. The latter also accomplished the "hat trick." Worcester Grammar School made a better display with the bat than their rivals, but the really excellent bowling of Tye and Woodward prevented a large score. Both bowled very fast and kept good length. This is the first defeat that Kidderminster have received for a long time, and the first time they have succumbed to Worcester Grammar School in any sport. ## WORCESTERSHIRE CLUB AND GROUND v. NORTON BARRACKS.

With only three wickets down for 128, it looked as if the county side would run up a large total, but the last seven men only added 22. There was a one excitement when the military had only lost half their wickets for the century, but the tail was very weak, and they were defeated by 42 runs. Score:—
