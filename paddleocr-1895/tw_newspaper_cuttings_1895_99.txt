# HEREFORD TIMES: SATURDAY 27 JULY 1895-3.

## “HEREFORD TIMES” ORICKET CHAL

## PRFSTEIGN V MALVERN.

This match was played at Prestiegn on Thursday in last week, the home side winning by 33 runs after a good game. For the winners the Rev. H. C. Green-Price, F. L. Evelyn, W. T. Williams, and W. Green-Price batted finely, whilst P. Davies met with most success with the ball. The feature of Mulvern's display was the all round play of W. H. Hartland, who secured four of the Prestiegn wickets, besides carrying his bat through the innings for 24.

## SHROPSHIRE V LUDILOW AND SOUTH SHROP.

This match was played on Saturday on the Shrewsbury ground. Ludlow went in first, and scored the good total of of 201 for nine wickets, although F. L. Evelyn was alient. F. H. Sitwell (52), Clerko (30), Leake (21), Hurling (21), and Wills (16), all batted well. On the homo team going in, only Sprott and Wood made any stand, and eight wickets fell for 77 runs, Hinds and Leake bowling exceedingly well.
