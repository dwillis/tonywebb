Devon County Asylum v. Mr. Newbery's XI.— This match was played at Exminster on Saturday, and ended in a win for the Asylum by the narrow margin of 4 runs. Scores:—Devon County Asylum, 32 and 60; Mr. Newbery's XI., 62 and 30. Exmouth v. Mr. W. O'Leary's Team: This two days' match was commenced at Exmouth yesterday. The visitors were disposed of for 174 runs, the highest scorer being H. Andrew (run out) 51. Deane took seven wickets. At the close of play last evening, Exmouth had made 210 for two wickets down, J. S. G. Greenfell 30, R. O. Groves 80; J. A. Gibb (not out) 78. Wivelicombe v. Wellington (Home Team).— This match was played at Wivelicombe on Saturday, and ended in a win for Wivelicombe by 20 runs. The best scers for the winners were H. L. Hodges 25 (not out) and A. D. Hickley 7. For the losers, T. S. Page 12, and W. H. Brown 6. Scores:—Wivelicombe, 62; Wellington, 42. Bradfield v. Ashill.—This return match was played on the ground of the former, and won easily by the home team by 10 wickets. H. Lane made 10 and 13 for Ashill, and played exceedingly good cricket, helping his side considerably, and F. Matthews and W. Miller were of great assistance to the winners. Ashill, 20 and 30; Bradfield, 66 and 1 for no wicket. Bridge Mills (Silverton) v. Uffouime.—Played on the ground of the former on Saturday, and won by Uffouime by 14 runs. The Rev. J F Powning byled dexterously, taking six wickets for 17 runs, and Ponford the remaining four for 10 runs. Frank Welland, for Uffouime, showed brilliant style for 28. Ponford played twitcha cricket. Foster was neatly stumped. Scores:—Uffouime—First inning, 64 second inning, 36 for seven wickets; Bridge Mills, 40.

## SIDMOUTI v. FALING.

This two days' match was concluded yesterday. Overnight Sidmouth had made 367 in their first innings, and Ealing had lost two wickets for two runs. On resuming the visitors missed the total to 216. The principal feature of the innings was the 101 by Coury, who was unfortunately ran out, and the fine defence of McGregor for 58. Oa going in a second time they did not do quite so well, and were ultimately beaten by an innings and two runs. The Ealing scores were as follows:—
