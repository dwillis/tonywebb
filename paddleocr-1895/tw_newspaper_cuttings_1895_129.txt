## HEMEL HEMATED V. X.Y.Z.

This return match resulted in a win for the Londoners, on Saturday last at Boxmoor, by one run on the first innings. Hemel Hempsted batted first and made 86. Nine wickets of the visitors were down for 85, and when the veteran, G P Field, and his son were in there was much excitement; however a hit for two settled the matter. The bowling on both sides was exceedingly good, and the fielding of the visitors excellent. The home team did not field so well, and lost the match in a great measure through the extras; they had no long-stop until the mischief had been done.
