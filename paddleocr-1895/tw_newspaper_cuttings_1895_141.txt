## THREE COUNTIES ASYLUM O.O.

A very creditable record is that of the Three Counties Aylam Club for the past season—viz. matches won, 11; drawn, 2; lost, 6. With the “glorious uncertainty” of cricket which gives so much more interest to the game, the Aylam eleven were successful sometimes contrary to their anticipations, and sustained one or two very unexpected defeats. The matches won were with Amphill, Rxton, Bigglewade, Bedford, Ramblers, Hitbin, Stevenage, London Joint Stock Bank, Westminster Wanderers, Neaden, Sandy and Mr. A. W. Tindall Locas's XI. The two drawn matches were those with Hackney Trademen O.O. and North Thames L.V.O.O. Defents were sustained from Mr. Gurney's XI, London and Westminster Bank, Roxton, Baldock, Gotterad and London Turo's O.O.

## SILSOE O.O.

The Silsoe club have had a very successful season. Ten matches have been played, and nine have been won, and the other ended in a draw in favour of Silsoe. Is batting H. Squire heads the list with an average of 17.2 runs; and good records are also held by Gudgin, 113.5 and O. Bottoms, 10. With the ball Gudgin and H. Porter have been very successful, the former taking 73 wickets at an average cost of 3.5 runs, the latter 48 wickets at an average cost of 4.5.
