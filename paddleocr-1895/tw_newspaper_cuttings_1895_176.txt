# NORTHAMPTON HERALD: SATURDAY 17 AUGUST 1895-2.

## NORTEAMPTON TOWN LEAGUE. SECOND SECTION.

Victoria-road v. Exetior Albions. - Low sensing ruled in this match, which was played on the Borough Ground on Saturday. Victoria-road compiled 31. F. King's 8 being the highest contribution. Copson took four wickets for 13, and Tate lost for 10. The Exetior Albions, who only batted eight men, were dismissed for 26. W. Darby (9) being the highest scorer. Victoria-road thus was by five runs. For Victoria-road F. Roberts obtained three wickets for 16, and F. Cooke four for 10.

Princess street v. Doddridge. — Princess Street suffered another reverse on Saturday on the Bever� Ground, Doddridge replying with US to their opponents' 61. J. Ingram, Princess Street's "stomewoller" who scored 24, was the only one to get into double figures against the destructive boating of J. Smith (right wickets for 20 runs). W. Lack obtained one wicket for 28, and A. Sutton one for 9. The chief contributors to the Doddridge total were A. Jones (11), C. Sutton (14), C. Jones (11), and W. Thurley (10). For Princess Street W. Dartell took three wickets for 29, F. Smith one for 12, W. Stacey two for 5, W. Rogers two for 22, and F. Brooks two for 25.

Moss Rose v. Pioneer.—The Moss Rose administered a crushing defeat to the Pioneer on the Borough Ground on Saturday. Betting first, the former "declared" when the score was 105 for six wickets, W. Kent, who played an excellent not out innings of 44, and W. Langley (36) being the chief contributors. Stanford (two for 38), A. Knights (one for 18), Wilkinson (two for 16), and E. Wright (one for 20), trundled for the Pioneer. The Pioneer were all out at 66, J. Knights (11) and A. Knights (10) being the only baker men to reach double figures. Bert Goodall (six wickets for 38) and Garrard (four for 21) were the bowlers for the Moss Rose.

## BIDPORTSETIR v. OXFORDSHIRE

This return match was concluded at Bedford on Saturday, when the home county had to bow to defeat to the turn of an inning and 50 runs. It was very evident after Bedfordshire's first venture that their opponents were too strong for them, and Oxfordshire brought the day's play to a close with the respectable total of 223, thus leaving Bedfordshire the task of scoring 151 to save an inning defeat. On Saturday they took up their second stand, but the Brothers Rogers's bowing was so destructive that they were all dismissed for 103. P. Rogers taking eight vickets for 53 runs. Oxfordshire thus gained their second victory over Bedfordshire this season by an inning and 50 runs.
