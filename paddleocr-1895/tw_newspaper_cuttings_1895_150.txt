Lincoln.—The Bracesbridge Asylum eleven met the Lincoln Lumps on Saturday. The Asylum went to first, and seemed (if for eight wickets, Bennett containing his. The Imps then went in, but had only scored if when their last wicket fell. The losers would only have registered a very poor total had it not been for a wall-hit 25 by Uox.
