# HERTFORDSHRE ADVERTISER: SATURDAY 6 July 1895-4

## M.C.C. AND GROUND v. HAILEYBURY COLLEGE

## COLLEGE

On Saturday the Collegians beat a strong team of the M.C.C. by nine runs on the first innings. Mr. J. Carter displayed great skill in his innings of 56, which included six 4's and six 2's; while Mr. C. H. Jupp batted well for 47, in which were three 4's. The M.C.C. started well, Mr. J. Douglas, of the Cambridge and Middlesex elevens, especially batting well, his 51 including six 4's; while Mr. Howard Fowler, the old Oxford wicket-keeper, hit very hard, and knocked up twelve 4's in his 67. Mr. J. F. Carter took five wickets for 56 runs. Under the fostering care of the Hon. and Rev. E. Lyttelton, the well-known Eton and Cambridge cricketer, the Halleybury boys have developed into an good a school team as there is in the country. Umprus, H. Batt (Soxex) and Attewell. Score: —

## LADIES V. GENTLEMEN.

By kind permission of Sir J. Blundell Maple, the above match was played on the Childwickbury ground on Monday, the ladies gaining a well earned victory by 23 runs. The usual restrictions, the use of a broomstick, in liqu of bat, and the left hand only in each department of the game, were imposed on the gentlemen. The features of the game were the fast overhand bowling of Miss Woods, who, although she took ten wickets for 39 runs, bowled with singularly bad luck, the tall time after time just clearing the top of the stumps, and the fine stand made by Miss M. Stogdon and Mrs. Law in the ladies second innings. Going in with the score at 0 for two wickets, they were not separated until they had carried the total to 65. The former's 46 and the extraordinary fielding of her and her sister show that the cricket of the family by no means confirms itself to the brothers who have done so much to make the name famous. ## ST. ALBANS “A” v. PROGMORE 2nd.

Frogmore and entertained the St. Albans "A" team on Saturday at Frogmore, and the game ended in a draw in favour of the visitors. Going to the wickets first, the Saints compiled 91 for five wickets when they declared, Baum and George being not out for 18 and 17 respectively. Taylor contributed a useful 22 and Hartley 16. The homesters followed, and totalled 54 for eight wickets, when stumps were drawn, P. Lee being responsible for 20. The game was not an enjoyable one. Scores:

## COLNEY HEATH v. WHEATHAMPSTEAD.

The above teams played their return match on No. Man's-Land, on Saturday. As Wheatampstead had won the previous match by one run, considerable interest was aroused in this meeting. Austin was in rare form for the visitors, while with the bat Holmes appeared likely to carry out his bat, but he was given out low ten minutes before time. For Wheathampstead Oakley bowled well, Kulberg was much to venturesome at the wicket, and receiving a nasty blow in the face was placed here de combat. Score:—
