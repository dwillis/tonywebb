# EASTERN DAILY PRESS: MONDAY 2 SEPTEMBER 1895-3.

Carrow Second Elsevier v. New City.—Played at Lakeubam, on Saturday last, and resulted in a win for Carrow by 48 runs after a good game. Only Cooke and Dickerson could successfully cope with the bowling of Moore and Jermyn. SILVER ROAD v. St. Paul's.—Played at Household on Saturday, and resulted in a win for Silver Road by eight wickets and 3 runs. For the winners King was the best bowler, taking twelve wickets for 21 runs, and also the best bat, scoring 11 and 11 not out. Lubbock also batted finally for 15 not out in the second inning. St. Philip's v. St. Philip's Institute.—Played on Dix's Land on August 31st, and resulted in the defeat of the Institute by 38 runs. The Institute could make no stand against the bowling of Scott (seven wickets for 2 runs), Prior (five for 6), and Clapham (three for 6), and compiled 7 and 22. St. Philip's replied with 29 and 38. For the Institute P. A. Hunt and Metcalf were the most successful bowlers. St. Philip's—Scott 6-2, Millill 6-8, Prior 8-7, Clapham 0-10, Stringer 4-3, Allward 2-0, Saunders 4-0, Nicholson 1-1 not out, Baron 0-0, lead 0-0, extras 1-7; total, 28-38.

OATFIELD v. OULTON AND BLICKLING PARK, — A most enjoyable game was played between these clubs on Thursday at Cattield, the Park winning by 40 runs. The visitors were entertained by the Cattield Cricket Club Committee, and, after an excellent lunch, Mr E Le Neve, on behalf of the Oulton and Blickling Club, tendered their best thanks for the capital way they had been received and entertained. Dr Gordon, on behalf of the Cattield Club, suitably responded. LADIES V. GENTLEMAN. - Played at Heydon Park on August 29th, and resulted in a victory for the Gentlemen by 54 runs. This enjoyable match was played in superb weather before a large number of speakers. The Gentlemen, who were handicapped in the usual style, declared their second innings closed with three wickets down, and were kindly allowed by their opponents' captain, Mrs. Sullivan, to play beyond time in order to finish the second inning of the Ladies.
