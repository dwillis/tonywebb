## FALMOUTH v. VISITORS

Played at Falmouth, the visitors being beaten easy. Score: - Falmouth, 132 (Captain Fanshawe not out 59, F. Fanshawe 24, H. Vincent 19, R. Fanshawe 12, Visitors, 90 (J. W. Symons 25, Rev. W. Willmell 20, C. 8, Horley not out 13, Rev. R. Robinson 14).

## LAUNCESTON v. 13 ST. MARY'S (LAUNCESTON).

This match, which was originally fixed for Thurs- day week, but was postponed in consequence of the rain, was played at Pennygillum, and resulted in a win for the Towners by four wickets and 108 runs. Score: -84. Mary's, 53. Lameeston, 141 for 6 wks. (Tedstone not out 0, Lyne 26, Martyn 19, Rev. J. H. Arundell 12).

## TRURO v. PENZANCE.

The match at the close was very exciting. Truro played really against time and though at one time there appeared to be no hope to defeat a draw, wickets were so disposed of as to dismiss the inst of the visitor's just as the clock struck the hour for drawing stumps; thus leaving Truro the winners, Score:—
