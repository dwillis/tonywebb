# CHESHIRE COUNTY NEWS: FRIDAY 23 AUGUST 1895-2.

## CRICKET. SATURDAY'S MATCHES. Stockport v. Werneth. STOCKPORT WIN BRILLIANTLY.

This match between these old rivals was played at Gale Green Park in magnificent weather, and before a large crowd of spectators, it being also set apart for the benefit of the professional. Brown and Disney, who throughout the season have done splendid work, and it is hoped that they will receive a substantial reward. It will be remembered that the first time those teams met at Werneth the season Stockport won a most interesting match by eleven runs, due on the whole to the splendid bowling of the avengreen Sam Brown. On Saturday skipper Hartley won the toss, and of course chose to bat, on a splendid wicket, taking in with him J. Bates to face the bowling of Hall and Hodgkinson. The start was hope too good from a Stockport point of view, as Bates in trying to get a full toss from Hodgkinson to leg was elegantly caught at slip. Arthur Torhington joined the captain who sent Hall splendidly for four, but after scoring ten he was caught at the wicket. Sam Brown came next and was warmly greeted by the crowd. He immediately got to work, scoring a bank faster than Torhington. The score had renews to 37 when Torhington was caught by Redfern after that player had scored six. Disney joined his brother professional but only to see him brilliantly catch at short slip by Colliison, after playing a splendid game. H. R. Hronley-Davenport of Cheshire county and Cambridge University farms came next. And here a splendid start took place, both men defying all the efforts of the Werneth bowlers. Davenport delighted the spectators with his big bitting, white Disney, on the other hand, was playing a most finished came, his cutting being a treat. At 72 Davenport was run out in stopping forward to a fast ball from Beano, which hit the wicket keeper's pads and the three back into two wicket before Davenport could recover his ground, he had hit up 17 in a very few minutes and he was loudly cheered on retiring. F. Tyler joined Disney, but shortly after the latter was caught at mid off for a splendidly played inning of 21. The telegraph board now reading 87-6-21. K. Selby came next, but little needs for further comment with the exception of Frank Tyler's display, which was a reality mention. One performance. His inning of 44 not cut included six four's two three's, and three two's. He was ably assisted by J. Bates's, who played a late inning of eleven before being caught on the boundary. The inings closed for the respectable score of 139. After the final interval Werneth opened their venture by sending in Wharmby and Isaton, the attach being entrusted to Sam Brown and Bremley-Davenport. Brown opened with a maiden over, but Eaton got the match's list ball away for three, but soon after lost Wharmby, who was nicely caught by Belby at cover point, the first wicket falling at nine. Engage took Wharmby's place his with an addition of six inexcellent Eaten that player was defeated by a grand bell from Brown. Redfern was next in and next ent, Rlby taking him certainly at points of Davenport, and three wickets were down for 15 runs. Matters never altered much for with the exception of Heworth Hall, and Nesson, no one seemed able to play the bowling, the inhouse clearing for 70. Reckport thus being crooked with a win over Werneth for the second time this month. I must just make a note of the splendid bowling of Sam Brown and Harry May, the former taking time for 18, and the latter for 15 and this along with the splendid fielding of the Stockport then, who never missed a chance, was due in no small measure to their brilliant victory. The spectators seemed to fairly enjoy the afternoon's report, and especially so to see their own men come in on the right side.
