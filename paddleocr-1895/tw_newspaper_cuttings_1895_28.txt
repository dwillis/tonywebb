# BERNSHRE CHRONICLE: SATURDAY 21 SEPTEMBER 1895

## ROYAL BERKS SEED ESTABLISHMENT CRICKET CLUB.

The above club has had a very successful season. Of the 19 matches played by the "A" team, 12 have been won, 5 lost, and 2 drawn. The total of runs obtained numbers 2,162, against 1,741 by opponents. The "B" team won 6 matches, and lost 4, and the junior team won 4, lost 3, and one ended in a tie. The Meares Sutton have, as usual, been most liberal in supporting the club. ## CRICKET.

Heckfield v. Major Mildmay's XI.—Plaved at Highfield Park, Heckfield, on September 10, and resulted in an early victory for the home team, a fact mainly due to an excellent innings by Bye, marred only by a single chance. For the visitors, Major Mildmay played sound cricket for twenty-seven. A large and fashionable gathering watched the game with much interest. Score:

Heckfield: Rev H. Salter, 6–37; E. Barnes, 8–5; I. Bradfield, 5–12; J. Bre, 59 (not out)–10; Rev G. S. Davies, 0–2; F. H. Martrin, 1–2; P. F. Law, 8–4 (not out); C. A. Law, 0–6 (not out); W. H. P. Law, 0; A. Dodd, 24–18; Dr Comber, 2; extras, 4–1, Totals, 117 and 101 (for seven wickets).

Major Mildmay's XI: E. Beagley, 5; C. Armiger, 0; L. Warren, 6; Major Mildmay, 27; P. Mildmay, 2; Rev. J. Saundersonf 0; G. Mildmay, 0; W. G. Cape, 8; M. I. Cope, 0; A. White, 4; J. Pavey (not out), 6; extras, 7. Total. 59.
