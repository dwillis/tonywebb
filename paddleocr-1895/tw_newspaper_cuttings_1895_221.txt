HERTESBURY V. St. BOWSPACE COLLEKE.-On Saturday St. Boniface College played Heytosbury at Warminster, the game ending in a victory for the home team. Scores: Heytosbury, 34 and 27; St. Boniface College, 46 and 16 for one wicket.

Winsorfield v. West Asurros.—Played at Wingheld on Saturday: Wingheld, 65 and 42 for six wickets; West Ashton, 50.
