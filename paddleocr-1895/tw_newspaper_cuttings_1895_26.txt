## INNIFEN beat FALLAND C.C.

The visitors to meet the Inkpen C.C. on their own around were rather late in coming, the time fixed for the match being two o'clock, while the game did not commence till about four. The home team won the loss and play quickly began. The chances of winning looked rather small at one time, four widness having fell for 20 runs. B.Bance soon turned the tide by adding R.S. more in capital style, his score beginning of a splendid drive for 1, a 3, two Fe, and the rest singles. The Rev. H. D. Butler made a good hit for 4, and when the innings was closed carried out his back. G. Butler and J. Hopkins did not bat. The visitors were dismissed for 29 runs, mainly owing to the first-rate bowling of T. May, who took 7 wickets, his average being 19.

## LAMBOUNN beat CHILTON.

The return match was played on Saturday last, on the Lambourn ground, and, as the score will show, resulted in a win for Lambourn, the homesters thus avenging their defeat in the previous match. The game did not commence till rather late, and consequently had to be decided on the first junge. The weather was delightful and the wicket really good. Hilton won the two, but elected to end the home teams in. A very poor start was made, as two Lambourn wickets went down for 0, but a wonderfully different complexion was put on the game, when, after H. Penfold had scored seven in good style, F. Harris

and J. Bryant got well set. F. Harris made some splendid hits, and the spectators loudly cheered his play play, his score of 35 being made in a very short time. J. Bryant played capital cricket for his 38, and was greatly applauded upon his retirement. V. Hobbs and C. J. Mabertey rendered great assistance to the score, before the inflows was declared closed at 128. The Chilton team did but little, only one player (S. Pratting) obtaining only five figures. The bowling of Mews. Hobbs and Bryant was very effective, and F. Harris made two capital catches in the long field. Chilton followed on, and at the call of time no wicket had fallen, the score being 61. H. New and Rumball having scored well during the innings. Score:

## NEWTOWN bent PARIN CHULCH INSTITUTE.

This return match was played in the beautiful grounds of Sandland Park, on Saturday, August 31st, there being a goodly number of spectators present, including Lady Butler and family and company. Newtown went first to the wick-to, and by good play and hard hitting on the vast of Eiffel (22), and Gardiner (16), made a total of 45 runs, Ganning's bowling being too good for them, he being well backed up by Matthews and Newtrell. On the Institute handling the willow, however, they were soon disposed of for 30 runs, Vickers being top scorer wish 15. Gibbs Field, Allsop, and Bayerstock being the bowlers. Tea was then partaken of, and Newtown again went to the wickers, but the Institute not being able to go in again, the home team won a pleasant game on the first lunage by eight runs.
