## MAIDEN ERLEGH CRICKET WEEK.

Third Match v. Frank Konkstra.—This match, played on the Maiden Krlegh ground, terminated on Friday in last work, when again there was a large and fashionable resembling to witness the game. The result was an easy victory for the home team, who were superior to their opponents both in batting and bowling, and also in fielding.

Sutton's JUNIORS v. SULHAMPSTEAD JUNIORS. — This match was played at Sullhampstead, on Wednesday, and resulted in an easy victory for the former, by an inuring and nine runs. Score: Sutton's Juniors, 72; Sullampstead, 20 and 43.

Mr T. W. GIRLERATONE'S XI v GINDLESTONEITES (CHANTENHOUSE). This match was played at Sunningdale School on July 31 and won by 107 runs by the home side, A. Daffen performing the "hat trick." The school thus concluded a very successful season's cricket, the Boys' Eleven having only lost five match-out of nine.
