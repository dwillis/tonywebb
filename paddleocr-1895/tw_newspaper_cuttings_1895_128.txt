## CHEMICAL WORKS C.O. v. HUNNYSIDE CHURCH

Played on Saturday, August 10th, on the ground of the latter. Owing to the fine bowling of Mr Williamson, who captured all the wickets for 11 runs, and also to his batting, the visitors were enabled to win by 8 runs only. Williamson was missed no less than three times.
