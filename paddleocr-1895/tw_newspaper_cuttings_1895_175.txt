# NORTHAMPTON HERALD: SATURDAY 10 AUGUST 1895-2

## NORTHAMPTON TOWN LEAGUE FIRST SECTION.

Excelsior v. Star.—This match was played on the County Ground on Saturday, and ended in a draw, rain on this occasion spoiling what would otherwise have been a most interesting event. Batting first, the Excelsior compiled 145 for eight wickets, when they declared their innings equal C. M. Lucas scored 71, included in which were one 5, six 4's, and five 3's, he being, however, missed three times J. Wigley 33, and M. Tull 12, O. Squire's obtained four wickets for 37 runs, W. Kirby two for 20, H. Davis one for 10, and C. Roberts one for 16. Upon the Star batting they made a rather poor start, P. Kendall, who was in good tranding form, obtaining three wickets in the first three overs. P. Cory, who scored 22 and A. Tilt, 21 not out, however, made a good stand, and when stumps were drawn the score was 35 for four wickets. Kendall had three wickets for 16 and S. Kirchmann one for three.

Clarence v. Colts. — Rain also interfered in this match on the County Ground on Saturday. Batting first, the Clarence, who only played ten men, scored 152. J. A. Bedford compiling 40. P. Twist 30, and Y. Kelley 15. For the Colts A. Mackness took four wickets for 31 runs. A. Ingram two for 36, and J. Brown two for 51. The Colts obtained 86 for two wickets. T. Lightwood not out 39. A. Mackness 17, and A. Deslaw 15. Vann took both wickets at a cut of 19 runs.

## SECOND SECTION.

Travis v. Regent-square.—This match was played on the Racecourse on Saturday, when the Trade won by nine wizards and nine rains. Stores: Regent-square, 101 (W. Downing' 34); Trade, 110 for one wicket (Thomas not out 63, Law not out 32). On behalf of the winners J. Potter took five wickets for 36. The Trade made their runs in an hour.

Excelsior Albions v. Artisan Star.—This game on the Racecourse on Sightway ended in a draw in favour of the latter. Score: Excelsior Albions, 57 for six wickets (Swannell 31); Artisan Star, 134 for six wickets (A. Underwood 40), inings declared chasea. A. Underwood (two for seven) for the Artisan Star, and Kenjall (four wickets) for their opponents, were the most effective bowlers.

Primitives v. St. Katherine's. — On the Racecourse the "Prime" won by four wickets. Bours: Primitives, 129 for six (W. Parkin not out 36); St. Katherine's, 127 (W. Taylor not out 39, Aletow); Y. Ouelle 28). W. Taylor took three wickets for the winners, and Thurby three wickets for the losers.

## THIRD SECTION.

Y.M.C.A. v. St. Bencheiro's B.C.—On the Racecourse on Satu's by the Y.It.C.A. won by seven runs. Sources: Y.M.C.a. 69; St. Bencheiro's B.C., 62.

Qok, at 44 Westeyans v. Wesleyan Star.—The former won this match on Saturday on the Borough Ground by 18 runs, scoring 37 runs to their opponents' 19. Catholic Ahna v. St. Ulies'.—Played on the Borough Ground on Saturday, and won by the former. Score: Catholic Ahna, 35; St. Ulies', 33.

## BYDPORDSHTRE v. HERTFORDSNIRE.

This match was concluded at Belford on Tuesday, and ended in a draw, Overnight Bedfordshire had been dismissed for 182, and their opponents had scored 15 for one. The play on Tuesday, owing to rain, did not commence until nearly three o'clock, when C and H. Pigg, the not only, resumed their innings, the former being bowled by Manton when he had 25 to his credit, and the latter 31. The innings of 165 did not close until six o'clock. In the remaining half-hour Bedfordshire lost four wickets for 47, and the game ended in an even draw.
