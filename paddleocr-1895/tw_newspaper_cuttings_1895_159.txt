# EASTERN DAILY PRESS: WEDNESDAY 5 JUNE 1895-2.

## DISS v. KING'S DRAGOON GUARDS

Played at Diss on Monday, and resulted in a win for Diss by 60 runs. Charming crisket weather provalled, and numerous spectators watched the game with evident enthusiasm. Diss won the tuss, and electing to bat first, ran up a total of 182, to which Tipple, Candler, Ireland, Nicholls and Downen were the chief contributors. The Drugeons then batted but made rather a poor show against the bowling of Ireland and Netherg, the former taking an wimbler. Harper scored freely towards the finish. Lieutenant Searight bowled well for the losers. Scores:—
