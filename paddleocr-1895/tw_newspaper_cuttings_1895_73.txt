# DEVON AND EXECERDALLY GARE THE: THURSDAY FRIDAY 15 16 AUGUST 1895-2

## EXETER v. NORTH DEVON.

This match was played at Graslawn yesterday in beautiful weather and before a good number of spectators. Exeter went to the wickets first, and were not dismissed until they had put together the good total of 278 runs. The chief scorers were J. N. Nicholls 140, whose total included five 6's and nine 4's, W. Ashford 51, and W. B. Carter 28. North Devon were all dismissed for 149. Exeter thus winning by 129 runs. The best scorers for the visitors were F. Townsend 58 (not out), E. H. Topham 18, and S. F. Middleton 14.

## EXMOUTH V. SOUTH HAMPSTEAD.

This two days match was commenced at Ex-month on Wednesday. Play was continued yesterday in brilliant weather, and proved a most interesting game. In their first innings South Hampstead only totalled 73, but they did much better in their second, running up a score of 308, the chief contributors being Hassell (69), E. Robinson (not out 71) and Brown (39). Exmouth made 152 and 103, their top scorer being J. H. Copleston (85-39).

## YORKSHIRE v. KSSEX.

In this match, at Harrogate yesterday, Esser scored 57 in their first innings, and 38 for three wickets in their second. Yorkshire's first innings produced 117.

## TORQUAY CRICKET MONTH. INCOGNITI V. ISHMAELITIS.

This two days' match was concluded at the Torquay Cricket ground yesterday, the result being an even draw. For the Ishmaelites, A. E. Heatley made a fine 83, which included a 6-and seven 4's, and for their opponent's, A. C. S. Glover scored 60, and this also included a 6. Scores: —Inconditi, 213 and 18 for two wickets; Ishmaelites, 100 and 262 for 8 wickets, innings declared closed.
