WYCLIFFR V. CHRISTCHURCH.—These very old rivals met again on Saturday, when Wycliffe again won, owing largely to the bowling of W Gatcombe (tour wickets for four chalks) and W Higgs (five wickets for six runs). Brown for Christchurch also bowled well, taking eight wickets at a small cost.

NEWBURAY v. MR C E KEYSER'S XI.—This match took place at Aldermaston Court on Friday, in splendid weather. The home team were pretty strongly represented, so much so that they kept the Newburians in the field nearly the whole time. The game ultimately ended in a draw. Mr Keyser's eleven put together 240. The chief contributors were E F Hill (79), H M Harford (62), and W N Greenfield (27). The two first-named treated spectators to a fine display of batting. Hill's figures comprised five fours, nine threes and three two. Harford's score was composed of four fours, five threes, and eight two. At the call of time the visitors had made 93 for three wickets, A B Belcher and Cave being not out with 25 and 27 respectively.
