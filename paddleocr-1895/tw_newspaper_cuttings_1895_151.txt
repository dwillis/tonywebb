# LINCOLN RUTLAND AND STAMFORD MERCURY: FRIDAY 13 SEPTEMBER 1895-1.

## SKEGNESS AND DISTRICT CRICKET CLUB ATERAGES.

This club, having won 16 of the 25 matches played, may be congratulated upon having had a successful season. Of the remaining nine games five only were lost, the other four being drawn. The excellent performances of Dench, the professional, have contributed in no small measure to the success attained by the club. He heads the butting averages with 41-1, and the bowling with 85 wickets at an average of 967 runs each. Dr. Cross also trundled with great success. Appended are the averages:—
