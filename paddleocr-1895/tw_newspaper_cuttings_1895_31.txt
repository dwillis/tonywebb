# BRECON AND RADNOR COUNTY TIMES: FRIDAY 30 AUGUST 1895-2.

# LOCAL SPORT.

## CRICKET --BRECON v. LIANDOVERY.

This match was played at Landover by on Wednesday the 21st inst. Brecon batted first and made a poor show, being all dismissed for 49. D. T. M. Jones took 6 wickets for 20. Landovery were dismissed for the comparatively small total of 95. W. L. Thomas taking most of the wickets. Brecon fired little better in their second innings, being only able to compile 62. This left Landovery 7 to win, and they lost 3 wickets before they got that number. The wickets were in a very bad condition and played badly.
