The above is the completed League title for 135% and on Saturday last the League flag, which remains in possession of the winning team for a season, was hosted on the Perth ground.

That Portill chases its position no one will gain any, and, as a matter of fact, this club has ever more points since the commencement of the league; than any other club, which it has never before been actually first, in 1843-3 in occupied the second position. Barlott and Crew are in a slightly inferior position to that occupied first year. The greatest improvement has been shown by back and Silverdale who this year occupy positions far in advance of those they follow last season. Fenton, the County Club, and Tunsall have all slightly deteriorated.

In consequence of the unfavourable state of the weather in July and August the number of points obtained do not bear a favourable comparison with those of former years, and the list of drawn liabilities is the heaviest on record since the institution of the League, notwithstanding the facts that the time for play has been extended for this season.

Bursden, in 1821, proceed its ability to go through a season undefeated, and it has remained for Norton to establish the unenviable reverse record of going through a full season without seeing a single win. The four points standing to the credit of the club is the lowest season's aggregate over inside.

## CONGLETON.

The last game of the season of the Forge Mill was decided as Congloton on Saturday, in the return match with Knypenley. The visitors won the toss, and batted first. Bourne and Shufflebotham played first-clues cricket for 43 and 34 respectively. Knypenley declared their innings closed when five wickets were down for 101. The Forge had only an hour to跖, and at the call of time had two wickets down for 42 runs. A. Minalall was the top scorer with 16.
