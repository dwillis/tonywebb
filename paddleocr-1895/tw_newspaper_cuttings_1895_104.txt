# HEREFORD TIMES: SATURDAY 10 AUGUST 1895-4.

## LUDLOW AND SOUTH HILLOPSHIRE V FREE FORESTERS

This two-days' match was concluded on Saturday on the Ludlow ground. Ludlow failed completely before the bowling of Harding and Toppin. The match ended in a win for the Free Foresters.
