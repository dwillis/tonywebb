KING'S LYNN V. DIES—This Junior Usp match was brought off at Lynn on Saturday last. It must be said that the Dies men were greatly disappointed after making the long journey to find such a bad ground and wicket to play on. All was in favour of the bewlers, as in consequence of a dip towards each wicket the ball shot in very quickly. Werman and Bussex only reached double figures. The feature of the innings was a catch in the long field by Gaussen. Dies commenced with Hipkin and A. G. Bobby, both of whom played carefully. When the score stood at 17, Hipkin was unfortunately run out, and Cole shared the same fate. At lunch time the score was 29 for three wickets. The match terminated in a victory for Dies by 13 runs. Leggett four for 28, Bussex three for 15, Ireland six for 28, and Hipkin four for 18 were the bewlers.
