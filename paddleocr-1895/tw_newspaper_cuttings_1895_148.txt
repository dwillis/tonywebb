## RICHARDSON CHALLENGE CUP. SECOND ROCK.

Froidingham v. Brica.—On Saturday over 500 spectators assembled on the Frodingham ground to witness the above match. Our readers will remember that on the previous Saturday Froidingham had totalled 161 in the first innings, and Brigg had made 83 for the loss of five wickets, when time was called. The Committee of Management ordered the game to be played out on the Froidingham ground, and also instructed the umpires that if possible two innings should be completed, the time to be extended to half past seven if necessary in order to finish the game. The match was resumed on Saturday, Brigg continuing their first innings, and such a good fight did they make that it was anybody's game right up to the fall of the last wicket, the total being 155 at the close of the innings, Flowers, who played splendidly, being top scorer with 30. On Froidingham going in a second time a sensational start was made, wickets failing rapidly to Barley's slows. Seven men were out for 25, but Childs, Crooke, and Sinclair made a better stand, and 82 was the total when the last wicket fell. Barley did the "hat trick," and took eight wickets for 37 runs. With 91 runs to get to win Brigg commenced their second innings a little before six o'clock, and in an hour had knocked off 82 for the loss of six wickets, when the game was stopped, notwithstanding the instructions given to the umpires that it was to be continued till half past seven should there be any prospect of finishing the two innings. With nearly half an hour to play and only nine runs to get and four wickets to go down it was hard lines for Brigg. However, the matter will be brought before the Committee for them to decide what is now to be done. It was a grand game all through, and the pity is that it was spoiled by any unpleasantness just at the close. Score:
