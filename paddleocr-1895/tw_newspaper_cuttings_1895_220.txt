# DEVIZES AND WILTSHIRE GAZETTE: THURSDAY 13 JUNE 1895-2

Corsham v. Trowbridge.—LKAGUK MATCH.—This match was played on the ground of the latter on Saturday last and was the second league match played by the visitors, who again proved victorious by 72 runs, though six of their batsmen were under 20 years of age, it being the youngest team ever sent out to represent the Corsham last XI. Fudge again was extremely useful with his bat, and was well supported by Mayo and H Hulbert. Hcores:
