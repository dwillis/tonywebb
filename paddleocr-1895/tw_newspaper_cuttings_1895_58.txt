# LIVERPOOL POS1: FRIDAY 13 SEPTEMBER 1895: TUESDAY 17SEPTI 1895

## LIVERPOOL CRICKET CLUB.

BATTING AND BOWLING AVERAGES. The first eleven played 37 matches, won 15, lost 6, and 10 were drawn. Won: Western Westernth (twice), Fetton (twice), Rossall School, Boughton Hall (twice), Boots, Warrington, Lillyton, Birkenhead Park, Preston, Rosewell, New Brighton, and Orton. Lost: Hurton, New College, Manchester, Formbr., and Lorim (twice). Draw: Ormakirk (twice), Birkeshead Park, Marlborough College, Clifton College, Cheltenham College, Northern (twice), Oston, Rock, Ferry, Fettesian, Lorestonians, Harrow Wanderers, Western, Warrington, and Hol Pepmuenemol. Runs to the number of 5,951 (including 333 extras) were scored for the loss of 311 wickets, against 5,890 runs (including 322 extas) for the loss of 342 wickets. The "an eleven" played 16 matches, won 5, lost 4, and 7 were drawn, serving 2,282 runs (including 144 extras) for the loss of 12 wickets, against 2,053 runs (including 177 extas) for the loss of 119 wickets. The second eleven played 27 matches, won 8, lost 7, and diet 12, sooring 3,873 runs (including 311 extas) for the loss of 204 wickets, against 3,897 runs (including 352 extas) for the loss of 320 wickets. The seven eleven played 6 matches, of which 4 were won and 2 drawn. In all the club played 86 matches, of which 33 were won, 17 lost, and 13 drawn. District matches were played against Yorkshire County and Cambridge University, the former being won and the latter lost. In addition, 3 extra matches were played, giving a grand total of 97 matches during the season. Although Mr. A. T. Kembel has only taken part in 21 matches this season, he has succeeded in stumping and catching 41 opponents.
