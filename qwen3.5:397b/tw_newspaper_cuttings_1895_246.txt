KILDARE MILITIA v. ROY OF ALLEN.—The above match was played on the Garrison ground, Curragh Camp, on Saturday, resulting in a brilliant victory for the home team. The visitors won the toss, and sent "Keery" and Thornhill to the wicket, but the former, before he was well set, was run out by his partner. Thornhill was then joined by de Roebek, who commenced hitting out at once, and quickly ran up a fine innings of 58. Thornhill left shortly afterwards, having contributed a very sound 23. Loch ran well to the wickets and played good cricket for his 32 (not out). Sergeant Dr. Cahill's excellent bowling accounted for the remainder of the team, and the innings closed for 143. Keogh and Dennis started the batting for the Kildares, and the former's acquaintance with the sticks was ably brought to a close, and his place was taken by Gibson. Dennis was smartly caught by Thornhill at point for a useful 29, and Sergeant-Dr. Cahill joining them, the pair hit till out freely brought the score to 100 before the latter was dismissed for 28. The next stand was made by German, who, by the aid of scientific cricket, contributed 35. Wickete began to fall now with great rapidity, Colnett being the first to go after an excellent 67, without giving a single chance. Wrenham was the only remaining member of the team who succeeded in getting double figures, being not out on 38. Mr Butler-Brooke, who is better known in the hunting line than the cricket field, made 7, and shortly after the innings closed for 235. Score :—
