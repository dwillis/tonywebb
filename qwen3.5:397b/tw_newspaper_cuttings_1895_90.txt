NEWCASTLE DAILY JOURNAL: FRIDAY 12 JULY 1895.

CRICKET.
Chilton Moor Wednesday v Pitlington Wednesday.—At Pitlington, and resulted in a win for the visitors. Score:—Pitlington, 21; Chilton Moor, 60.
