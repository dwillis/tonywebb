BERROW'S WORCESTER JOURNAL: SATURDAY 24 AUGUST 1895-4.

WICHENFORD v. A. W. ISAAC'S XI.—Played at Boughon Park on Monday, and ended in a win for Wichenford by four runs after a most exciting finish. A. W. Isaac's side won the toss and elected to bat on a perfect wicket, but were dismissed for 45. H. C. Isaac playing well for his runs. Williams bowled extremely well. Wichenford then went in and exceeded their total by four, after some good bowling by H. C. Isaac, and careful batting by Messrs. Taylor, Williams, and Caswell. A. W. Isaac's XI. then went in again and played out time, getting 105 for eight wickets, after some most brilliant batting by Messrs. H. C. and A. W. Isaac, they were both dismissed by two good catches in the long field by R. Pidge.

LADY HUNTINGDON'S v. ST. MARY'S.—The re-played League fixture between these clubs took place on the ground of the former. The "Ladies" batted first and were dismissed for 25 runs, A. Harbor and T. Brooks bowling remarkably well, the former taking seven wickets for eight runs, the latter three for 17. St. Mary's started very disastrously, losing five wickets for five runs, but J. Braithwaite put a better complexion on the game by knocking 13 in fine style, St. Mary's winning an exciting game by nine runs.
