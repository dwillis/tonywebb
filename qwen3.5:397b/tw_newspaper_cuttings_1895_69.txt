R.M.L.I. v. PLYMOUTH COLLEGE.
The return match between these teams took place at Mount Wise yesterday in splendid weather, the Marines winning by 111 runs. Batting first, the visitors were all disposed of for 31. R. A. Lake contributing 15. The Marines, in reply, scored 111, Captain Curteis, Lieutenant Moore, and Corporal McKeean being the principal scorers. Following on, the visitors scored 120, Shacklock 52, Lake 32, Mcre 21, and Shanks 20, batting well.

PLYMOUTH v. STONEHOUSE.
This match was played at South Devon-place yesterday, and resulted in an easy win for the home team. Batting first, the home team put together 121, F. P. Tyacke, H. B. Spencer, and H. R. K. Harrison scoring most of the runs, and played very fine cricket against the good bowling, coupled with accurate fielding, of the visitors. J. G. Davey bowled unchanged, and sent down 23 overs 14 maidens four wickets for 24 runs. The innings of the visitors was a poor display, owing to the brilliant handling of H. R. K. Harrison. The Plymouth men bowled at a tremendous rate, and took eight wickets for but 2 runs. Following on the visitors fared a little better, and scored 56 for eight wickets. The wicketkeeping of S. Sydney was very fine, only 2 leg byes being registered. Scores :—
