THE COUNTY CHAMPIONSHIP
SURREY v NORTHUMBERLAND
at The Oval, July 25, 26, 27
(Scored by W. G. Grace)

First Day's Play (Saturday) — Good
weather and fine ground. A good
fielding party from the Northum-
berland side showed up, but could
not prevent Surrey from taking the
lead in points by a considerable
margin; the visitors had only one
player who was really good, the
bowler being the most dangerous of
the lot. The bowling of the Surrey
men was excellent, and the fielding
very fair.

LONDON COUNTY CRICKET CLUB
v. MIDDLESEX
at Lord's, July 26, 27, 28
(Scored by W. G. Grace)

First Day's Play (Saturday) — The
London County Cricket Club were
very well represented in the field,
and their batting was very good.
Middlesex had a very good team,
but they could not prevent the
London County from taking the
lead in points by a considerable
margin.
