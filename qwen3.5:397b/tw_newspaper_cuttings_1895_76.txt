SIDMOUTH v. EALING.
This match was concluded in glorious weather on a perfect wicket at Sidmouth yesterday. Sidmouth batted the first day, making 357. The home eleven concluded their innings at six o'clock, and when stumps were drawn, the visitors had lost two wickets for 2 runs. Resuming yesterday, however, a splendid stand was made by F. D. Coury. Dr MacGregor also batted well, and the innings closed for 215. Following on, the visitors were completely overpowered, the bowling of Nepean and Fox being extremely well directed. Sidmouth won by an innings and two runs.
