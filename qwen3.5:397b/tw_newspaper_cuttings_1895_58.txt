LIVERPOOL POST: FRIDAY 13 SEPTEMBER 1895 : TUESDAY 17 SEPT 1895

LIVERPOOL CRICKET CLUB.
BATTING AND BOWLING AVERAGES.
The first eleven played 37 matches, won 16, lost 6, and 15 were drawn. Won v Western Werneth (twice), Sefton (twice), Russell School, Boughton Hall (twice), Bootle, Warrington, Luyton, Birkenhead Park, Preston, Rock Ferry, New Brighton, and Orton. Lost v Huyton, Rock College, Manchester, Formby, and Liscard (twice). Draws : Ormskirk (twice), Birkenhead Park, Marlborough College, Clifton College, Cheltenham College, Northern (twice), Victoria, Rock Ferry, Pettesian-Loretomians, Harrow Wanderers, Western, Warrington, and High Farnesmenol. Runs to the number of 5,051 (including 335 extras) were scored for the loss of 311 wickets, against 5,830 runs (including 322 extras) for the loss of 342 wickets. The "an eleven 7" played 14 matches, won 5, lost 4, and 7 were drawn, scoring 2,252 runs (including 144 extras) for the loss of 139 wickets, against 3,857 runs (including 177 extras) for the loss of 119 wickets. The second eleven played 27 matches, won 8, lost 7, and drew 12, scoring 3,878 runs (including 311 extras) for the loss of 294 wickets, against 3,857 runs (including 332 extras) for the loss of 226 wickets. The seven-eleven played 6 matches, of which 4 were won, and 2 drawn. In all the club played 85 matches, of which 33 were won, 17 lost, and 35 drawn. District matches were played against Yorkshire County and Cambridge University, the former being won and the latter lost. In addition, 3 extra matches were played, giving a grand total of 97 matches during the season. Although Mr. A. T. Kemble has only taken part in 21 matches this season, he has succeeded in stumping and catching 41 opponents.
