SOUTH WALES DAILY NEWS: Monday 5 August 1895-2.

GENTLEMEN OF MONMOUTHSHIRE v. R. M. E. MILITIA.

This two-day match, which was played on Lord Tredegar's Park, was concluded on Saturday, and owing chiefly to the bowling of Porter, and the fine batting displays by C. P. Harding and H. Bircham, ended right on time in a victory for the Gentlemen by five wickets and 75 runs. Scores :—

CARDIFF v. BRISTOL SCHOOLMASTERS.
On Monday next the Bristol Schoolmasters will meet a fairly strong Cardiff eleven on the Cardiff Arms Park. Wicket pitched at 11.30 sharp. The Cardiff team is as follows, Lowe accompanying the 2nd on their tour to Warminster, Fynon, and Wells, while J. H. Brain will play for Fairwater :—J. G. Clarke (capt.), T. M. Barlow, Selwyn Biega, S. Sweet-Escott, V. A. Hickley, W. W. F. Pullon, A. H. Mann, E. W. Hodge, C. C. McCall, J. E. McKay, and E. H. Schurle.

WINDSOR v. ORANGE CONSERVATIVES.—Played at Grangetown, resulting in a win for the Windsorfs. Score :—Windsor, 50 (for seven wickets) ; Orange Conservative eleven, 25.
