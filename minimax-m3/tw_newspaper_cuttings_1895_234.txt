WELLAND AND DISTRICT v. MALVERN WELLS.—
Played at Welland, on August 1st. Malvern
Wells went in first. They did not fare at all well
against the bowling of Mansell and Parr, and they
were all dismissed for 49, Berrington (13) and Harris
(11) being the highest scorers. Welland made 166 for
seven wickets, 7 of which good batting by Gunn (51),
Lowe (40), 2 [unclear] (31) not out), and Parr (50).
Malvern Wells in their second innings made 35,
Berrington (11), and Wright (16 not out). SCORE:—

MR. G. A. BUCKLAND'S XI. v. MR. T. C.
RICHENS' XI.—Played at Henwick on Monday. Mr.
Buckland's XI batted first, and started with Messrs.
G. A. Buckland and E. A. Turley. With the score at
5 Turley was dismissed by a good ball from Postle,
G. F. Jones-Williams then joined Buckland, and the
score had reached 70 before Jones-Williams was bowled
by Watts, Messrs. H. W. Buckland and H. A.
Turley also batted well for the visitors. Mr. Richens'
XI commenced in the batting of Messrs. G. A.
Buckland and E. A. Turley. Six wickets were down
for 41, when rain came on, and the game ended in a
draw very much in favour of Mr. Buckland's XI. For
Mr. Richens' XI G. Jones-Williams played a fine
innings, and Mr. Watts also batted well:—
