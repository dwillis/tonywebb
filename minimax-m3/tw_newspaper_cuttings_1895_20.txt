READING v. ABINGDON.—That the cricket field is one of the favourite haunts of King Chance was evidenced beyond doubt in this engagement, which was decided at Reading on Saturday. On the occasion of the first match, played a short time since, at Abingdon, Reading were represented by a fairly powerful combination, nevertheless they were ill recipients of a decisive thrashing. The Berks county town, however, created a mild surprise in their engagement, as despite that their eleven on paper was particular, they returned the compliment they received at Abingdon with slight interest, inasmuch as they secured more than an innings victory.
