KILKENNY MILITIA v. HON. or ALLEYNE.—The above match was played on the Mounth at the Curragh Camp, on Saturday, resulting in a brilliant victory for M'Kinney and Thornhill to the wickets, but the former, before he was well set, was run out by his partner. Thornhill was bowled twice by Babcock, who, with quick runs, and play of good cricket for his 32 (not out). Sergeant-Dr. Colenel's excellent bowling accounted for the remainder of the wickets for 144, four of which were caught by Lynch and Demails started the bowling for the Kildare, and the former's singlestancy with the stick was apparently brought to a close, and his place was taken by Gaven. Demails was smartly caught by Thornhill at point for a useful 20, and Sergeant-Dr. Colenel joining Gaven, the pair hitting freely brought the innings to 100 before the latter was disposed of for 25. The next stand was made by Crossan, who, by the aid of splendid cricket, contributed 32. Wickens began to fall now with great rapidity, Colenel being the first to go after an excellent 67, without giving a single chance. Fermanagh was the only remaining member of the team who appeared in getting double figures, being not out for 39. Mr ButhelcEchede, who is better known in the hunting line in the cricket field, made 7, and, shortly after the innings closed for 153. Score:—

HIGH SCHOOL THIRD XI. v. REE COMPANY BOYS' BRIGADE.—This match was played at Delgraye square, and resulted in a win for the former by 5 runs. Davis (1), Williams (2) played best for the winners; while Wakelely (6) did best for the losers. Manners and Fergason took most wickets for High School.
