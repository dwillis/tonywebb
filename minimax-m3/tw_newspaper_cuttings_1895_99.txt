"HEREFORD TIMES" CRICKET CHAL-LENGE TROPHY.
SEMI-FINAL.
PRESTEIGN v MALVERN.

This match was played at Presteign on Thursday in last week, the home side winning by 33 runs after a *good* game. For the winners the Rev. H. C. Green-Price, F. L. Evelyn, W. T. Williams, and W. Green-Price batted finely, whilst P. Davies met with most success with the ball. The feature of Malvern's display was the all round play of W. H. Hartland, who secured four of the Presteign wickets, besides carrying his bat through the innings for 24. Score:—

SHROPSHIRE v LUDLOW AND SOUTH SHROP-SHIRE.

This match was played on Saturday on the Shrewsbury ground. Ludlow went in first, and scored the good total of 201 for nine wickets, although F. L. Evelyn was absent. F. H. Stilwell (62), Clarke (29), Luske (21), Hamling (21), and Willis (19), all batted well. On the home team going in, only Spratt and Wood made any stand, and eight wickets fell for 77 runs, Hinds and Luske bowling exceedingly well. Score:—
