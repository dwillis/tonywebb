**SCORES OF OVER 200.**
The following record of individual scores of over 200 in first-class matches has been compiled by Dr. G. H. Brand. It appears that Mr. W. Ward's record, made in 1820 against Norfolk, which was then first-class, remained un-broken for 59 years. Dr. W. G. Grace scored 344 and 318 (not out) in 1876, the former being the highest score that has ever been made in a first-class match, and the latter the highest in a first-class county match. W. G. Grace has scored over 200 ten times, Shrewsbury seven, and Gunn four. Notts is the only county of whose bowling such a score has not been made. **CLECKHEATON v. LIVERSEDGE.**
*To the Editor of the Yorkshire Post.*

SIR,—Your issue of to-day, both in your notes and in the report of the match, you give the result of the Cleckheaton v. Liversedge match as a draw. This is an erroneous statement, as Cleckheaton won. There were eleven extras in the Cleckheaton total, and not one, as given in your report. I hope you will correct this in your next issue as it makes an immense amount of difference in their position on your table.—Yours, &c.,
Cleckheaton, May 20. N. GISSON, Scorer.
P.S. The error was not ours. Only one extra was given in the report officially supplied.—ED.
