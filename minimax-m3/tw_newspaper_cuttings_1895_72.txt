**SIDMOUTH v. INCOGNITI.**
This match commenced at Sidmouth on Wednesday and concluded yesterday, the Incogniti winning by an innings and 2 runs. Rain interfered with the match the first day, and when play was drawn the visitors had made 155 for the loss of seven wickets. Yesterday the wicket was altogether in favour of the batsman. The innings of the Incogs was soon disposed of, and they were disposed of the first time for 51 runs before lunch. Owing to the fact that only they only raised 78.
