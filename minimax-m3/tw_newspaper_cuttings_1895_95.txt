HEREFORD TIMES: SATURDAY 6 JULY 1895-2.

HEREFORDSHIRE C. AND G. v. CATHEDRAL SCHOOL.—Played at Widemarsh on Thursday. Score:—(Cathedral School, 141 (Shepherd 56); Herefordshire C. and G., 111 for four wickets (Phillips, not out, 71).

MALVERN COLLEGE v SHERBORNE SCHOOL.—This two days' match at Malvern ended in a win for the College by an innings and 27 runs.

LUDLOW 2ND XI. v LUDLOW GRAMMAR SCHOOL.—Played on Wednesday. The School won on the first innings. Score: Ludlow 2nd XI., 64 and 77; School, 51.

Usk 2ND XI v NEWPORT 3RD XI.—This match, which was played on Saturday, had to be abandoned, in consequence of the rain after Usk, who went to the wickets first, had made 30 runs for four wickets.

LEOMINSTER v. HEREFORD CATHEDRAL SCHOOL.—Played at Leominster on Wednesday. Score:—(Cathedral School, 51 and 128 for two wickets (Shepherd 56), and C. H. Boutlon, not out, 50); Leominster, 124 (H. G. B. Phillips 64).
