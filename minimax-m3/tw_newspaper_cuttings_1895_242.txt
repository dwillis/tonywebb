BERROW'S WORCESTER JOURNAL: SATURDAY 24 AUGUST 1895-4.

WICHENFORD v. A. W. ISAAC'S XI.—Played at Boughton Park on Monday, and ended in a win for Wichenford by four runs after a most exciting finish. A. W. Isaac's side won the toss and elected to bat on a perfect wicket, but were dismissed for 49. H. C. Isaac playing well for his runs. Williams bowled exceedingly well. Wichenford then went in and exceeded their 4th total by four, after some good bowling by H. C. Isaac, and careful batting, by Messrs. Taylor, Williams, and Caswell. A. W. Isaac's XI. then went in again and played out time, getting 105 for eight wickets, after some most brilliant batting by Messrs. H. C. and A. W. Isaac, they were both dismissed by two good catches in the long field by E. Fidoe. SCORE:—

LADY HUNTINGDON'S v. ST. MARY'S.—The re-played League fixture between these clubs took place on the ground of the former. The "Larries" batted first and were dismissed for 25 runs. A. Harber and T. Brooks bowling remarkably well, the former taking seven wickets for eight runs, the latter three for 17. St. Mary's started very disastrously, losing five wickets for five runs, but J. Braithwaite, put a better complexion on the game by knocking 15 in fine style, St. Mary's winning an exciting game by nine runs. SCORE:—

LEDBURY v. BROMSBERROW PARK, LONDON.—At Ledbury, on Saturday. This was the annual benefit match for the ground man (Farley). SCORE:—Ledbury 58, Bromsberrow Park 57.

OLD MALVERNIANS v. HORSHAM.—The Old Malvernians continued their tour and on Wednesday and Thursday opposed Horsham. The Malvernians made a grand total of 292, the W. Brymer playing brilliantly for 125. In the first innings Horsham were dismissed for 180 and in their second attempt did worse, making 167. With 10 wickets in hand, the visitors only required 55 to win, but for want of time the match was drawn.

ARLOTTA MORTON v. BADSEBROOK AND COOKHILL.—Cookhill narrowly escaped a beating in this match at Allsots Morton on Saturday last, as, with but two wickets in hand, they required 55 to win when time was called. Baldwin, Walker, and Pugh scored freely for the home team, Baldwin's score being put together in very good style. For Cookhill R. H. Upton was largely responsible for saving the game.

KIDDER CASTLE v. BROMSGROVE.—Played at Bromsgrove, on Saturday, and resulted in a win for the visitors by 11 runs.
