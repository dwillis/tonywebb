PLYMOUTH v. STONEHOUSE.
This match was played at South Devon-place yesterday, and resulted in an easy win for the home team. Batting first, the home team put together 121, P. F. Tyacke, H. R. Spencer, and H. R. E. Harrison scoring most of the runs, and played very fine cricket against the good bowling, coupled with accurate fielding, of the visitors. J. G. Davey bowled unchanged, and sent down 23 overs 14 maidens four wickets for 24 runs. The fielders of the visitors was a poor display, owing to the brilliant handling of H. R. E. Harrison. The Plymouth man bowled at a tremendous rate, and took eight wickets for but 2 runs. Following on the visitors fared a trifle better, and scored 45 for eight wickets. The wickets keeping of S. Spencer was very fine, only 2 leg byes being registered. Score:—
