R.M.L.I. v. PLYMOUTH COLLEGE.

The return match between these teams took place at Mount Wise yesterday in splendid weather, the Marines winning by 111 runs. Batting first, the visitors were all disposed of for 31, R. A. Lake contributing 15. The Marines in reply scored 142, Captain Cortis, Lieutenant Moore, and Corporal McKeen being the principal scorers. PLYMOUTH v. STONEHOUSE

This match was played at South Devon-place yesterday, and resulted in an easy win for the home team. Batting first, the home team put together 121, P. P. Tyacke, H. B. Spencer, and H. R. E. Harrison scoring most of the runs, and played very fine cricket against the good bowling, coupled with accurate fielding of the visitors. J. G. Davey bowled unchanged, and sent down 23 overs 14 maidens four wickets for 24 runs. The innings of the visitors was a poor display, owing to the brilliant trundling of H. R. E. Harrison. The Plymouth run level at a tremendous rate, and took eight wickets for but 2 runs. Following on the visitors fared a trifle better, and scored 46 for eight wickets. The wicket-keeping of S. Spinney was very fine, only 2 leg byes being registered. Score :—
