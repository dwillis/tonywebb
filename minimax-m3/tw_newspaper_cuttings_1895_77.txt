**TIVERTON v. SWANSEA TOURISTS.**
The touring team of cricketers for Swansea visited Tiverton yesterday, and played against a team representing the town and district, on the Athletic Ground. The tourists went to the wickets first, and solely with the bowling of Shaw and Cranfield, were disposed of for 48. This total was quickly excelled by the Tivertonians, their innings closed for 57. **SIDMOUTH v. M.C.C.**
This match was concluded at Sidmouth yesterday, the result being a draw. The home eleven batted the first day compiling 244, to which M.C.C. contributed 121, and the Rev R. B. Shepherd 51, both playing admirable cricket. When stumps were drawn M.C.C.C. had made 2 runs for no wicket. Yesterday, they closed their innings for 192. Curtis and Titchmarsh handled the willow finely. C. Wells did the "hat trick," bowling from the east end of the pitch, a performance which gained him warm applause from a large concourse of onlookers. The weather was a 6 that could be desired, and the wicket in perfect condition. Scores:—
