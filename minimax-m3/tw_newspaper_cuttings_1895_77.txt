TIVERTON v. SWANSEA TOURISTS.
The touring team of cricketers for Swansea visited Tiverton yesterday, and played against a team representing the town and district, on the Athletic Ground. The tourists went to the wickets first, and chiefly with the bowling of Shaw and Cranfield, were disposed of for 48. This total was quickly exceeded by the Tivertonians, their innings closed for 67. SIDMOUTH v. M.C.C.
This match was concluded at Sidmouth yesterday, the result being a draw. The home eleven batted the first day compiling 244, to which E. A. Nepean contributed 121, and the Rev E. B. Bruton 61, both playing admirable cricket. When stumps were drawn M.C.C. had made 2 runs for no wicket. Yesterday, the day, they closed their innings for 192. Carlos and Titchmarsh handled the willow finely. C. Wells did the "hat trick," bowling from the east end of the pitch, a performance which gained him warm applause from a large concourse of onlookers. The weather was all that could be desired, and the wicket in perfect condition. Score:-
