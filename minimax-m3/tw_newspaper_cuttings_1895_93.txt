SWANSEA v. LLANDEVERY.
This match was played at Swansea on Saturday. Owing to the heavy rain which fell up to mid-day, the game did not commence till 3 o'clock. The visitors came down two men short, and were assisted by C. Coke and E. Bishop of the home club. Swansea went in first, but there was nothing worthy of note in the innings except a fine display of batting by H. Gage, who compiled 69, including 6 fours. Ross took three wickets for 33 runs, and Prytherch three for 15. Llandovery gave a very poor display of batting, not a single player getting into four figures. The Swansea professional bowled with great effect, and he was almost unplayable, taking 7 wickets for 22 runs. Swansea won by 57 on the first innings. Score :—
