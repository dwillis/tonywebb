EASTERN DAILY PRESS : WEDNESDAY 5 JUNE 1895 - 2.

DISS v. KING'S DRAGOON GUARDS.
Played at Diss on Monday, and resulted in a win for Diss by 60 runs. Charming cricket weather prevailed, and numerous spectators watched the game with evident enthusiasm. Diss won the toss, and electing to bat first, ran up a total of 132, to which Tipple, Candler, Ireland, Nicholls and Dawson [unclear] the chief contributors. The Dragoons then [unclear] but made rather a poor show against the [unclear] of Ireland and Notidge, the former taking six wickets. Harper scored freely towards the finish. Lieutenant Searight bowled well for the losers. Scores:—
