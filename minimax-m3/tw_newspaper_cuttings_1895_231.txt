HANLEY CASTLE GRAMMAR SCHOOL v. LITTLETON GRAMMAR SCHOOL, MALVERN.—Played at Malvern on Wednesday, and resulted in a victory for the Hanley Castle boys, who, batting first, made 67 runs, Lyttelton making 44. Chead maintained his reputation as a batsman and bowler, making 40 runs in good style, and taking five Lyttelton wickets for 16 runs. Bloxsome (16) and Mulliner (10) batted well for Lyttelton. MALVERN COLLEGE v. M.C.C.

This two-days' match, which concluded at Malvern, on Saturday, was remarkable for the small score of the Collegians in their second innings, and 42 is about the lowest record associated with the Malvernians. M.C.C. had the assistance of Woodcock and White-side, from Leicestershire. The former took seven wickets in the second innings, and a grandly-played century total by the Rev. H. T. Thornton, together with some useful scores, left M.C.G. winners at the finish by an innings and 68 runs.
