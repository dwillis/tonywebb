EVESHAM v. PERSHORE.—Played at Evesham on Saturday. Going in first Evesham fared badly, all wickets being down for 21. However Goodall, Needham, Blanchard, and later on Field made useful scores, and the total reached 65. Pershore could do nothing against the bowling of Field, but scored rather freely off Smith. Their total realised 66, Evesham thus winning by 31 runs. C. Phillips batted splendidly for his runs, and Watts also did well. Field took four wickets for eight runs. Score :—

HANLEY CASTLE GRAMMAR SCHOOL v. LITTLETON GRAMMAR SCHOOL, MALVERN.—Played at Malvern on Wednesday, and resulted in a victory for the Hanley Castle boys, who, batting first, made 67 runs, Lyttelton making 46. Chadd maintained his reputation as a batsman and bowler, making 49 runs in good style, and taking five Lyttelton wickets for 16 runs. Blossom (16) and Mulliner (10) batted well for Lyttelton. MALVERN COLLEGE v. M.C.C.

This two-days' match, which concluded at Malvern, on Saturday, was remarkable for the small score of the Collegians in their second innings, and 42 is about the lowest record associated with the Malvernians. M.C.C. had the assistance of Woodcock and White-side, from Leicestershire. The former took seven wickets in the second innings, and a grandly-played century total by the Rev. H. T. Thornton, together with some useful scores, left M.C.C. winners at the finish by an innings and 68 runs.
