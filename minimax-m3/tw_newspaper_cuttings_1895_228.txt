DEVIZES AND WILTSHIRE GAZETTE: THURSDAY 12 SEPTEMBER 1895-2.

WILTS COUNTY ASYLUM CRICKET SEASON, 1895.

With a drawn game against Bradford, the Asylum eleven closed their season on Saturday. They have played 21 matches, winning 11, losing 5, and 5 were drawn. The wins were against Rowde, All Cannings (thrice), Erchfont, Holt, Wilts Depot, Manton, Monkton Farleigh, Radwell Rovers, and Wilts County Police; the losses were against Pewsey (twice), Corsham, Rev. Ransome's XI, and Mr. Bolwell's XI, while matches against Holt, Radwell Rovers, Westwood, Wilts Depot, and Erchfont were drawn. The total number of runs scored for the Asylum was 2,402 for 198 wickets, an average per wicket of 12.1, and their opponents 2,012 for 223, an average of 9.1.
