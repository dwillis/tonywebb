DUNSTABLE BOROUGH GAZETTE: WEDNESDAY 14 AUGUST 1895 - 5.

HOUGHTON v WESTONING.

Played on Houghton Green on Monday, and resulted in a victory for the home team. The principal feature of the game was the excellent form of F. Love, who carried his bat through the innings for 46; A. Cook (37) also batted well. Score:—
