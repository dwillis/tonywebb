NORTHAMPTON HERALD: SATURDAY 17 AUGUST 1895—2.

NORTHAMPTON TOWN LEAGUE.
SECOND SECTION.

Victoria-road v. Excelsior Allstars. — Low scoring ruled in this match, which was played on the Borough Ground on Saturday, Victoria-road compiling 21, St. King's 8 being the highest contribution. Crease took four wickets for 25 and Tate four for 10. The Excelsior Allstars, who only batted eight men, were dismissed for 26, W. Darby (9) being the highest scorer, Victoria-road thus won by five runs. For Victoria-road F. Roberts obtained three wickets for 16, and F. Cooke four for 10.

Princess-street v. Doddridge. — Princess - street, suffered another reverse on Saturday on the Borough Ground, Doddridge replying with 36 in their opponents' 61. J. Ingram, Princess-street's stonewaller, who scored 24, was the only one to get into double figures against the destructive bowling of J. Smith (eight wickets for 20 runs). W. Lord obtained one wicket for 28, and A. Becton one for 9. The chief contributors to the Doddridge total were A. Jones (13), C. Nallice (14), C. Jones (12), and W. Tinsley (10). For Princess-street, W. Darrell took three wickets for 29, F. Smith one for 12, W. Stacey one for 5, W. Rogers two for 22, and F. Brooks two for 25.

Moss Rose v. Pioneers.—The Moss Rose administered a crushing defeat to the Pioneer on the Borough Ground on Saturday. Batting first, the former "declared" when the score was 106 for six wickets, W. Kent, who played an excellent not out innings of 44, and W. Langley (36) being the chief contributors. Stanford (two for 35), A. Knight (one for 18), Wilkinson (two for 16), and H. Wright (one for 20), trundled for the Pioneers. The Pioneers were all out for 56, J. Knights (11) and A. Knight (10) being the only batsmen to reach double figures. Bert Goodall (six wickets for 32) and Garrard (four for 21) were the bowlers for the Moss Rose.

BEDFORDSHIRE v. OXFORDSHIRE.

This return match was concluded at Bedford on Saturday, when the home county had to bow to defeat to the tune of an innings and 36 runs. It was very evident after Bedfordshire's first venture that their opponents were too strong for them, and Oxfordshire brought the day's play to a close with the respectable total of 272, thus leaving Bedfordshire the task of facing 153. It was an innings defeat. On Saturday they took up their second stand, but the Brothers Rogers's bowling was so destructive that they were all dismissed for 105, F. Rogers taking eight wickets for 53 runs. Oxfordshire thus gained their second victory over Bedfordshire this season by an innings and 36 runs.

M.C.C. AND GROUND v. BUCKINGHAMSHIRE.

It did not take long on Saturday to bring the match at Lord's between these teams to a conclusion, Buckinghamshire beating the M.C.C. by an innings and 28 runs—some such result having been foreshadowed by the state of the game overnight. St. James' Excelsior C.C. v. St. James' Hall.—Played at St. James' End on Saturday, and won by the former by 45 runs. The Hall batted first, and do nothing against the bowling of Billson (four wickets for 20 runs) and Wright (five for seven runs). On the Excelsior batting they made 84. O. Wright scored 17 and A. Kirton 8. Scores: Excelsior, 84; St. James' Hall, 9.

St. Paul's v. Princess-street K.I.C.B.—Played on the Racecourse on Saturday, and won by St. Paul's by 9 runs. Scores: St. Paul's 64; Princess-street 43.

St. Paul's v. Wellingborough All Saints Juniors.—Played at Wellingborough on August 7th, and won by St. Paul's by 35 runs. Scores: 67 and 36.
