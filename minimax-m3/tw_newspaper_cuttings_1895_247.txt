ROYAL MUNSTER FUSILIERS V ARMY SERVICE CORPS.
BATTING EXTRAORDINARY THE SECOND SEASON.
Captain W. Cnuige Oates, Royal Munster Fusiliers, the popular Captain of the Curragh District C.C., and Private Fitzserald, of the same battalion, yesterday had the distinction of topping the batting record by compassing the immense score of 623 runs during their partnership, and as both batsmen were not out at the call of "time," when they retired good for another 600, it is impossible to estimate what might have happened had the game been prolonged. The building up of this huge score occupied exactly 4½ hours, yielding an average of 1.31 runs per hour, 659 is supposed to have been the previous record, that being the score credited to Messrs A. H. Trevor and G. F. Vernon in a match, Ormeau Club v. Hockey Green, some years ago, but it is understood both batsmen were "set." Captain Oates, who has done so much for cricket, especially for the ground on which the immense score has been compiled, richly merits the distinction of having contributed the lion's share towards this remarkable performance, and it bears eloquent testimony to the value of the improvements to the ground inaugurated by him eight months ago, that this new record score should have followed so closely the score of over 400 runs, recently made by the Church District, against Dublin University. A meeting of the committee of the Leinster branch of the Irish Union was held last evening in the Wicklow Hotel, when there were present—Mr C. W. Frith (in the chair), Mr F. Stoker (Dundrum), Mr H. Thornlow (Pembroke), Mr C. Mitchell (Pembroke), Mr J. Cotter (Dundrum), Mr F. H. Browning (Dublin University), hon sec.; Mr J. Tindall (Leinster), hon. treasurer. Mr Tindall announced that the receipt at the recent Gentlemen of Ireland v. M.C.C. match amounted to £52, and after the discharge of all liabilities a balance of close on £30 remained to the credit of the union—a very satisfactory state of affairs. The Junior Cup competition was discussed, and it was decided that clubs, in order to be eligible, should be affiliated with the Leinster branch by July 1st. Entries for the cup close on July 14th.
