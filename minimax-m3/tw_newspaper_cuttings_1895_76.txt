SIDMOUTH v. EALING.
This match was concluded in glorious weather on a perfect wicket at Sidmouth yesterday. Sidmouth batted first, making 357. The home eleven included their lastings at all four, and the stump were drawn, the visitors had lost two wickets for 2 runs. Resuming yesterday, however, a splendid stand was made by F. D. Comry. Dr MacGregor also batted well, and the innings closed for 216. Following on, the visitors were outclassed, the bowling of Nepean and Fox being extremely well directed. Sidmouth won by an innings and two runs. Score :—
