SIDMOUTH v. EALING.
This match was concluded in glorious weather on a perfect wicket at Sidmouth yesterday. Sidmouth's hard luck in making 357. The home eleven defended their innings at all hazards, and when stumps were drawn, the visitors had lost two wickets for 2 runs. Resuming yesterday, however, a splendid start was made by F. D. Conry. Dr MacGregor also batted well, and the innings closed for 215. Following on, the visitors were criticised, the bowling of Nepean and Fox being extremely well directed. Sidmouth won by an innings and two runs. Score:—
