OAKHURST v WYMSTAY.—Played on Saturday at Wynnstay. The home team were all absent for ct, O. Orwood being the only one who could make any stand against the bowling of Fred Gough and H. Aston. Oakhurst scored 71. Fred Gough batted well for 25,being the only one putting into double figures for his side. Oakhurst won a good match by 28.

MINSTERFIELD CLUB v SHREWSBURY SCHOOL.—Played at the School on July 29 and 30, and resulted in a draw in favour of the County. This was the third match at the School, the season having been an eminently successful one, only one game having been lost. A word of praise is due to their coaches, Messrs A Watson (Lancashire), and Nicholls and Westcott (Somersetshire), who have no doubtedly worked great improvement in the cricket at the school. Score:—

OSWESTRY v GUILSFIELD.—Played at Oswestry on Saturday, in anything but cricket weather. The visitors batted first, but could do nothing with the bowling of H T Gough and Sabine, and were all disposed of for thirty-two. Captain Hayhurst-Frumps and Moore started the batting for the town, and the score was taken to twenty when the Captain was bowled by a good ball from A Robinson. Sabine, the new comer, was smartly stumped by H F Mytton after making eleven. B T T was bowled by H M Pryce-Jones for five, and Corfield succumbed to the same bowler for a like number. Campbell came in next, and at once seemed to settle down. Moore was bowled by Makepeace for thirty-two. Marshall, who came next, was content with defending his wicket whilst Campbell hit brilliantly all round the wicket, and at the call of time was not out with fifty to his credit. Oswestry were thus left winners by ninety-one runs with five wickets to spare. Score:—

OSWESTRY v BIRKENHEAD VICTORIA.—Played at Oswestry on Monday, before a good company. The visitors were the first to bat, and they started most disastrously, Gough bowling Brocklebank with his first ball. The wicket was favouring the bowlers who made good use of their opportunity, but if one or two chances had not been thrown away the score would not have reached 58. Moore and Clapp opened the Oswestry innings, and both seemed at once to settle down. The score was taken to 52, when rain interfered with the game. On resuming, the necessary runs were made, when rain again stopped play. After waiting some time it was decided to abandon the match. Oswestry were thus left winners by 10 wickets. Score:—
