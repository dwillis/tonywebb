LATER.—Wonford House all out for 153 ; Cathedral School, out for 132.

CRICKET.

Plymouth gained an easy victory over Stonehouse yesterday by 99 runs, scoring 191 to 92.

The return match between Torquay and H.M.S. Britannia was decided at Torquay yesterday, and resulted in a victory for the home team, who scored 100 to the ship's 76. A. Dmoley (32; not out) batted well for the winners, whilst W. J. Troughton (32) did well for the Britannia.

EXMOUTH v. MR. A KINDERSLEY'S XI.

Mr. Kindersley took a team to Exmouth on Wednesday, but were beaten by the first two men of Exmouth's side. The visitors scored 110, and Copleston and Salter put on 132 for the first wicket. At the call of time the home XI. had made 253 for three wickets. Scores:—
