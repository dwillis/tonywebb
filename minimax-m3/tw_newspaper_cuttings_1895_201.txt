MONTGOMERY v. PONTRESBURY.—These teams played off their return match at the latter place on Wednesday, in dull weather. The home team, which comprised a strong eleven, batted first, and were all dismissed for 32 runs, [unclear] taking five wickets for twelve, and Williams four for seventeen. Amos, who compiled a dozen, was the only man to make any stand.
