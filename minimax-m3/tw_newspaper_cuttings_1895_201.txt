MONTGOMERY v. ABERYSTWITH.—Played at Lymore Park, Montgomery, on Saturday, in fine weather. The team captain won the toss and sent the visitors in. Williams and A. Eaton, the Montgomery bowlers, were in fine form, taking six wickets for 12 and four for 17 respectively, and got rid of the Ystwythians for 31, of which E. Davies, a Montgomery enthusiast, made 9. The Montgomerys started at 131, towards which A. Eaton (11), S. Williams (41), R. T. Harris (33), Shaw (12), L Griffiths (11 not out), were the chief contributors. E. Davies, the Montgomery substitute, bowled well for the visitors, who had no rank team. Score:—

WREXHAM v. ST. JAMES'S (WREXHAM).—Played at Wrexham Racecourse on Friday. Rain fell heavily most of the time, and 5th wicket fell for 8 runs, just Williams for thirty-five, Eaton taking five wickets for twelve, and Williams four for seventeen. Amos, who compiled a dozen, was the only man to make any stand. For the visitors the brothers Eaton were in rare form, Pryce Eaton carrying his bat from first wicket down for an admirable score of fifty-nine, while Alf Eaton did yeoman service, and, together with his senior, succeeded in passing the Pontesbury score with only a single wicket down. The other Montgomery men were accounted for by Amos and Cartwright, who secured four wickets each. Scores:—

WHITTINGTON v. TEDSMORE. (RETURN.)—This second fixture between these Clubs was played at Tedsmore on Saturday, and resulted in a rainy week team. This makes two and losers (who only batted nine men) H Humphreys played a spirited but somewhat fortunate innings of 24 before being bowled by W Hurlison, the total for the first wicket being 29. No one else did much except W Jones who made 13, the innings resulting 40. Rowson and Hudleston bowled well. The home team made a fairly good start, the first wicket falling for 10, but all were disposed of for 24, no one reaching double figures.
