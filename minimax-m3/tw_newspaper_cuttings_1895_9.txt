DUNSTABLE BOROUGH GAZETTE: WEDNESDAY 21 AUGUST 1895-3.

DUNSTABLE TOWN FIRST ELEVEN v. ASTON CLINTON.

Played at Aston Clinton on Tuesday, and resulted in an easy win for the visitors by 148 runs. For the winners Manton and Anderson made a splendid stand for the first wicket, putting on no fewer than 104 runs before being parted, when the former left for a grandly played 67, the other principal scores being Giddings 26, Staunton 32, Anderson 28, Pepper 24, and 30 standing to the credit of "Mr Extras." The home wickets fell very quickly before the bowling of W. F. Brown. Langley for the home team batted well for 25, and a stand of nearly an hour's duration was made by Chessall and Pitt, but only 30 runs were added to the score, the play during that time being very slow and monotonous.
