Royal Cornwall Gazette: Thursday 5 September 1895 - 2

CRICKET.

MADRON 2ND v. LELANT.
Played at Madron on Saturday, and won by the home team. Scores: Madron 2nd, 55 (Mollard 15, Nicholls 10); Lelant, 19.

FALMOUTH v. H.M.S. GANGES.
Played at Falmouth on August 29th. Score: H.M.S. Ganges, 1st innings, 49; 2nd innings, 22 for 2 wickets. Falmouth, 146 for 5 wickets.

MORVAL v. ANTONY.
Result a win for Morval by 15. Jeffery bowled well, four wickets for 6 runs. Morval 37 (Poore 15.) Antony 22.

LELANT UNION JACKS v. PENZANCE WANDERERS.
Played at the Vicarage, Lelant, on Friday. Score:—Wanderers 30; second innings 71 (Phillips 10.) Union Jacks 11; second innings 55 (S. Millett 10.)

CORNWALL COUNTY ASYLUM v. ST. BREWARD.
Played at Cornwall County Asylum on 28th August. Scores:—St. Breward 53 (Pote 11, Campbell 19.) The Asylum 125 (Dennison 47, Winstan 12, Adams 27.)

FALMOUTH v. HELSTON.
Played at Helston. An easy win for the visitors on a bowler's wicket. Falmouth 161 (Vincent 29, Tregellas 21, Wainwright 16, Whitley 11.) Helston 39.

ST. COLUMB v. ST. BLAZEY.
This match was played at St. Blazey on Wednesday, the home team winning. Score—St. Columb 47 (Salmon, not out 23.) St. Blazey 45 (Brown 19, Dunstan 11.)

PADSTOW v. POLSETH VISITORS.
Played at Padstow on Wednesday, the home team winning by 65 runs. Score:—Visitors 105 (Hoare 22, E. H. Oliver 45, Holrobine 16.) Polseth Visitors 46 (Martyn 22.)

TROON v. HELSTON.
Played at Helston on Friday. Score:—Troon 125 (Bennetts 15, H. Smith 18, Martin 45, Barnes 10.) Helston 77 (Tyacke 16, Blackwell 13, Toy 22, Rogers not out 14.)

GRAMPOUND v. LADOCK.
Played at Ladock on Saturday, resulting, after a pleasant game, in a victory for the visitors by 39 runs on the first innings. Scores : — Grampound, 61 (C. Penwarden 18, Croggon 14). Ladock, 22.

CAMBORNE v. MADRON.
At Camborne. Score:—Camborne 129 for 3 wickets (Sinnil 18, Rogers 14, H. Smith not out 81, Bennetts junr. 40. Holman not out 10.) Madron 58 (R. Hichens 12); second innings 16.

NEWQUAY VISITORS v. PADSTOW.
Played at Newquay, and won by Padstow by 3 wickets and 67 runs. Padstow 137 for 7 wickets (Hoare, not out 63, W. Kingcote, jun. 14, W. Kinscotte, sen., 21.) Newquay Visitors 70 (Wardle 12, Hosking, not out 21.)

EXILES v. PENZANCE.
Played at Porthleurow, ending in a decisive victory for the Exiles by 209 runs on the first innings. Score:—Exiles 250 (Michell 25, Spencer 85, Straddling 56, Millward 44, Whinfield 17, Ash 25.) Withers 24, J. Llewis not out, 25.) Penzance 51 (Jenkin 16, J. Rogers 12.)

FALMOUTH v. REDRUTH.
Played at Falmouth on Saturday. The late hour at which the visitors arrived induced the Falmouth captain to declare at 90, thus losing by 2 runs. Score:—Falmouth 90 for 6 wickets (Major Ashley 11, Vincent 34, Tregellas, not out 37,) Redruth 92 (Andrew 14, Sclater 23, Tamblyn 15. Sweet 11.)

DEVORAN v. H.M.S. GANGES.
Played at Mylor on Saturday, the home team being easy victors. The visitors went to the wickets first, and scored 54 runs, W. Pascoe making 19, and the Rev. A. Williams 11. H.M.S. Ganges scored 117 for three wickets, and declared the innings closed. In their second innings Devoran were all disposed of for 9 runs.

LAUNCESTON v. BUDE.
The return match between the above clubs was played at Pennygillam on Saturday, and after a good game resulted in a draw greatly in favour of Bude. The gate was small, but above the average, the takings being handed to Gunn, the club's professional. Score:—Bude 148 (Johnson 42, Hornby 25, Dickinson 20, J. R. May 20.) Launceston 95 (Martyn 14, Olse 24, L. H. Arundell 14, Brown 11.)
