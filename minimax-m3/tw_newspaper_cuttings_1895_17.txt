HEATH END v. ST. LAURENCE'S (READING).—At Heath End on Saturday, the result of a highly enjoyable game being in nine win for Heath End, by 38 runs and two wickets. The batting of Maxwell, White and W Hunt was exceedingly good, the latter's innings of 72 runs being marred with two mistakes only. The bowling of Maxwell and Roberts was superb, Roberts' analysis reading seven overs, three maidens, nine runs and five wickets; and Maxwell's thirteen, four maidens, fourteen runs and three wickets. The match was through the hospitality of Mr Hunt, the players partook of tea on the lawn. At the conclusion the meal the captain of the home club (Mr E J Hunt), presented Mr H Roberts with a cap in recognition of his recent performance of the hat trick.
