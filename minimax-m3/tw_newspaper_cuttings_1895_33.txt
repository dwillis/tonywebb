**BAYNERS XI v. PERMANENT STAFF OF THE 2ND BATT. OXFORD LIGHT INFANTRY.**

On Monday last, August 5th, a match was played on Bayners private ground, between the above teams, and, after being hindered a little by the unsettled weather, ended in a decisive victory for the former by an innings and 11 runs. Mr. G. M. Ross captained the home eleven, and, having won the toss, elected to bat. The game was very slow at the commencement, and indeed throughout. The Lighter end of the captain and Mr. Powell became associated, the best stand of the day was made, and some good cricket was seen, both playing with confidence. They put on 64 runs between them, when Mr. Ross was caught at the wicket for an invaluable 37. Powell left soon after with a well-played 39 to his credit. Wheeler made 14, and the remaining batsmen being quickly disposed of, the innings closed for 111. An adjournment was now made for lunch (which had been prepared in a spacious marquee, erected for the occasion) through the generosity of Sir Philip and Lady Ross. After ample justice had been done to the good things provided, the visitors commenced their innings. Captain Ross and Bugler Wakelin were the only ones to show any resistance to the attack of C. M. Ross and Co. who were bowling in their best form, and the innings closed for 51. Captain Ross's 18 was a splendid hit, and his batting display, which included one 6, a clean drive out of the ground. Powell had the best analysis, taking 6 wickets for 23, while Mr. Ross's cost 25 runs. Having to follow on, they failed to reach the number required to save an innings defeat. Dean taking 5 wickets for 18 runs, and the game resulted as before stated. Scores and analysis:—
