                              THE WREXHAM TOUR.

FIRST MATCH.—WREXHAM v. LLANDUDNO.
Wrexham played the first fixture of their tour at Llandudno, on Wednesday. The day was beautifully fine, and, a captainly contested game ended in an even draw. F H Jagger, the brothers Hollings, Lieutenant Throckmorton, and the Rev L. F. Ward batted well for their runs, while with the ball Mr Ward captured five wickets for 40 runs, and Read three for 25. As one thing is looked as if Llandudno were going to make a very poor show with the bat, as their first five wickets fell for 48 runs. Score:—
