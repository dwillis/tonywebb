BURTON LATIMER v. WELLINGBRO' ALL SAINTS' B.—Played at Wellingbro' on Saturday, the visitors winning by 114 runs.

RETTFORD WANDERERS v. COTTONS.—Played at Kestering on Saturday, the visitors winning by 99 runs to 92.

LINCOLN.—The Broadbridge Asylum eleven met the Lincoln team on Saturday. The Asylum went in first, and scored 80 for eight wickets, Bennett contributing his. The Leros then went in, but had only scored 41 when their last wicket fell. The losers would only have required a very poor total had it not been for a wicket 25 by Cox.
        KESTE[unclear]: INDEPENDENT v. KESTERING BRITANNIA.—At Bushwell on Saturday, an interesting game ending in a draw.
