BURTON LATHERS v. WALLINGBRO' ALL SAINTS' R.—Played at Wallingbro' on Saturday, the visitors winning by 114 runs.

BETTISRIGG WANDERERS v. GRAFTON.—Played at Kettering on Saturday, the visitors winning by 99 runs to 86.

LINCOLN.—The Bracebridge Asylum eleven met the Lincoln Inns on Saturday. The Asylum went in first, and scored 50 for eight wickets, Bennett contributing his. The Inns then went in, but had only scored 41 when their last wicket fell. The Inns would only have registered a very poor total had it not been for a well-hit 25 by Cox.

KETTERING.—INDEPENDENT v. KETTERING BRITANNIA.—At Bockwell on Saturday, an interesting game ending in a draw.
