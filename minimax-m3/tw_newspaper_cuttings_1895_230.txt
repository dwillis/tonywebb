BERROW'S WORCESTER JOURNAL: SATURDAY 13 JULY 1895-2

WORCESTER CATHEDRAL SCHOOL v. MR. T. DAVIES' XI.—This match was played on Saturday on the School ground, and resulted in a win for the School by 24 runs. The visitors went in first but save for Messrs. P. P. Williams and R. W. Holland none made any stand, and the innings closed for 47. The School, with the help of a well-played innings of 27 of J. S. Liddell, and 15 by E. A. Bencombe, won against the visitors' score, and eventually the score reached 72. The second innings of the scratch team began disastrously, as seven wickets were down for only 14 runs, but T. F. Hammill raised the score somewhat before he had unfortunately to retire. Of the School bowlers J. K. Deakin did best with an analysis of seven wickets for 17. Score:—

ST. JOHN'S v. BROMSGROVE.—This match was played at St. John's on Saturday, a capital game resulting in favour of St. John's by 37 runs. Bromsgrove won the toss, but Mr. Beech Hicks and T. Straw both bowled so well, and were so ably supported by capital fielding, that the visitors were all disposed of for 33 runs, Straw taking five wickets for 7 runs. St. John's on taking the bat soon lost four wickets, through the excellent bowling of Rolleston, for 15 runs, but on Mr. Young joining Mr. Curtis an invaluable stand was made, and, thanks to their excellent batting, the score was taken to 41 before the next wicket fell, Mr. Curtis playing capital cricket for his 16, and Mr. Young an equally good innings for 17. Mr. Park also hit well for his score, and the innings closed for 70. Rolleston bowled splendidly, and Messrs. J. K. and K. Perkins shared the bowling at the other end, and were well backed up by the excellent fielding. Score:—

WORCESTER GRAMMAR SCHOOL v. HARTLEBURY GRAMMAR SCHOOL.—Played at Flag Meadow on Saturday. Hartlebury having first and [unclear] all sent back for 20. Of these Vale, who was top scorer with nine, played very steadily. Thorp took eight wickets for ten runs. Worcester's prospects did not look very bright when two good wickets went for one run, but on Davis and Thorp being associated runs came more briskly, and their opponents' total was passed with four wickets down. Ultimately the whole side was dismissed for 34 Davis playing very well for his 15. Vale and Dorsett both bowled very well for Hartlebury. Worcester Grammar School have now played nine, won six lost three. Score:—

HANLEY CASTLE GRAMMAR SCHOOL v. WORCESTER CATHEDRAL VOLUNTARY CHOIR.—The return match between these teams was played at Hanley Castle on Saturday, and resulted, as in the first match, in a victory for the Grammar School. The Choir batted first, on a good pitch, but wickets fell rapidly before the excellent bowling of Chadd, who took seven wickets for 7 runs, and Hincks who took three wickets for 4 runs. The innings realised 14 runs. Chadd and Pope started well for the Grammar School, making 22 runs for the first wicket. Hincks and Hingrave also batted in good form against the somewhat weak bowling of the Choir. After tea, which was served on the ground, Chadd continued batting in excellent style, and carried out his bat for 71 runs. Hincks [unclear] were drawn at 6.30, when the Notout bat made 123 runs for six wickets. Score:—
