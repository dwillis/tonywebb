**COLESHILL CC. v. ST. MARGARET'S CC.**
The return match between the above clubs was played at Coleshill on Saturday, August 10th, St. Margaret's proving successful, which was chiefly owing to the batting of Mr. C. C. Powell, who not only made the highest score for his side, but actually took 5 wickets for 6 runs. Mr. J. Deane also bowled wonderfully well, taking the other 6 for 20 runs. Coleshill went to the wickets first, and were soon disposed of, Mr. E. Powley being top scorer with 11, and the innings closed for 51, of which 2 were made commenced their task of making 80 to win, which was soon accomplished, with 7 wickets in hand, and the match which was completed without the slightest chance, and the innings realized 78. Coleshill went to the wickets a second time, and did much better, having made 72 for 3 wickets when time was called.
