**COLESHILL C.C. v. ST. MARGARET'S C.C.**

The return match between the above clubs was played at Coleshill on Saturday, August 10th, St. Margaret's, proving successful, which was chiefly owing to the batting and all-round cricket of Mr. C. C. Powell, who not only made the highest score for his side, but actually took 8 wickets for 8 runs. Mr. J. Dean also bowled wonderfully well, taking the other 5 for 20 runs. Coleshill went to the wickets first, and were soon disposed of, Mr. R. Weatherby being top scorer with 11, and the innings closed for 23. Fountains headed with 11, and the innings closed for 23, but they were not so lucky, and when the task of making 20 to win, which was easily accomplished, Fountains made 11, Mr. Powell taking 4, which was compiled without the slightest chance, and the innings realised 76. Coleshill went to the wickets a second time, and did much better, having made 75 for 5 wickets when time was called. Full scores and analysis :-
