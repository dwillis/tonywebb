GILOCESTER v ROSS.—Played at Ross on Wednesday on a good wicket. The home team were very weak, losing without the services of Munn (captain), Wintle, Woodward, and E. J. Smith. Mr Folle, one of the Ross players, met with an accident whilst fielding and sustained his left leg. Phillips played good cricket for 72, not out. Amongst his hits being three six's which brought loud applause from the spectators. Gloucester won by 81 runs. Score:—
