LIVERPOOL POST: FRIDAY 13 SEPTEMBER 1895: TUESDAY 17 SEPT 1895

**COLUMN 1 (Left):**

LIVERPOOL CRICKET CLUB.
BATTING AND BOWLING AVERAGES.
The first eleven played 37 matches, won 16, lost 6, and 15 were drawn. Won: Western, Werneth (twice), Sefton (twice), Rossall School, Doughton Hall (twice), Brooks, Warrington, Huyton, Birkenhead Park, Preston, 190A Pyrr, not out. Dighton, and Orton. Lost: Huyton, New Brighton, and Orton. Drawn: Ormskirk (twice), Birkenhead Park, Marlborough College, Clifton College, Cheltenham College, Northern (twice), Orton, Rock Ferry, Pottemian - Leamington, Harrow, Wandsworth, Western, Warrington, and Huyton, Parramatta. Runs is the number of 5,901 (including 333 extras) were scored for the loss of 311 wickets, against 5,830 runs (including 322 extras) for the loss of 342 wickets. The "an eleven" played 16 matches, won 5, lost 4 and 7 were drawn, scoring 2,292 runs (including 144 extras) for the loss of 139 wickets, against 2,003 runs (including 177 extras) for the loss of 119 wickets. The second eleven played 27 matches, won 8, lost 7, and drew 12, scoring 3,873 runs (including 311 extras) for the loss of 201 wickets, against 3,597 runs (including 302 extras) for the loss of 220 wickets. The dozen played 6 matches, of which 4 were won and 2 drawn. In all the club played 86 matches, of which 33 were won, 17 lost, and 36 drawn. District matches were played against Yorkshire County and Cambridge University, the former being won and the latter lost. In addition, 2 trial matches were played, giving a grand total of 97 matches during the season. Although Mr. A. T. Kemble has only taken part in 21 matches this season, he has succeeded in stumping and catching 41 opponents.
