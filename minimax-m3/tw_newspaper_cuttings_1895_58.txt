LIVERPOOL POST: FRIDAY 13 SEPTEMBER 1895: TUESDAY 17 SEP 1895

LIVERPOOL CRICKET CLUB.
BATTING AND BOWLING AVERAGES.

The first eleven played 37 matches, won 15, lost 6, and 10 were drawn. Won: Werneth (twice), Sefton (twice), Rossall School, Doush Hall (twice), Bootle, Warrington, Huyton, Birkenhead Park, Preston, 12th A Pyrr, not out, Blighton, and Oxton. Lost: Huyton, College, Manchester, Formby, and Lewton (twice). Drawn: Ormskirk (twice), Birkenhead Park, Marlborough College, Clifton College, Cheltenham College, Northern (twice), Oxton, Rock Ferry, Patricroin: Leytonians, Harrow Wanderers, Western, Warrington, and Hos Poynter, mont. Runs to the number of 5,901 (including 853 extras) were scored for the loss of 311 wickets, against 6,880 runs (including 322 extras) for the loss of 342 wickets. The "an eleven" played 19 matches, won 9, lost 4 and 7 were drawn, scoring 2,282 runs (including 144 extras) for the loss of 126 wickets, against 2,003 runs (including 177 extras) for the loss of 119 wickets. The second eleven played 27 matches, won 5, lost 7, and drew 12, scoring 3,878 runs (including 311 extras) for the loss of 204 wickets, against 3,892 runs (including 352 extras) for the loss of 220 wickets. The an eleven played 6 matches, of which 2 were won, and 2 drawn. In all the club played 86 matches, of which 33 were won, 17 lost, and 36 drawn. District matches were played against Yorkshire County and Cambridge University, the former being won and the latter lost. In addition, 2 extra matches were played, giving a grand total of 97 matches during the season. Although Mr. A. T. Kemble has only taken part in 21 matches this season, he has succeeded in stumping and catching 44 opponents.
