SOUTH NORTHUMBERLAND v. ELSWICK I WORKS.

This match, which is for the benefit of Godband, the South Northumberland professional, will be played at Gosforth on Saturday, and, as two exceedingly strong teams will take the field, the game should ensure a good gate. NORTHERN NOMADS v. THE BORDERERS.

These teams met on Mr Bowick's ground at Close House yesterday. Batting first, the Nomads ran up a total of 228, chiefly by the fine play of P Ashworth (85) and H Nester Furt (57), both of whom scored freely off the bowling, their partnership carrying the score from 114 to 219. On the Borderers going in wickets fell in rapid succession until Fogg-Ellice and C Liddell became associated, when nine men were out for 67. By steady play they increased the total to 102, when the heavy rainfall caused the game to be abandoned, thus robbing the Nomads of a certain victory. SCORE:—
