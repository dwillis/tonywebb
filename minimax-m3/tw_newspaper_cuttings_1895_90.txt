NORTHERN NOMADS v. THE BORDERERS.
These teams met on Mr Berwick's ground on Close House yesterday. Batting first, the Nomads ran up a total of 228, chiefly by the fine play of P Ashworth (85) and H Jeanneret Furst (57), both of whom scored freely off the bowling, their partnership carrying the score from 114 to 219.

On the Borderers going in wickets fell in rapid succession until Fogg-Ellice and C Liddell became associated, when nine men were out for 67. By steady play they increased the total to 102, when the heavy rainfall caused the game to be abandoned, thus rubbing the Nomads of a certain victory. Score:—
