CRICKET.

GENTLEMEN OF BERKSHIRE v. MR C. D. ROSE'S XI.—This match was played on Saturday at Hardwick House, near Pangbourne, the seat of Mr Rose. THAMES BANK IRON WORKS v. BOROUGH POLICE.—This match was on the Reading C.C. ground on Tuesday and resulted in a win for the Iron Works by an innings and 16 runs, making the sixth consecutive victory for them. For the winners E. Gradden batted splendidly for 77, not out. J. Lightfoot took 5 wickets for 5 runs, and E. Underwood 5 wickets for 16 runs. For the losers Grey took 6 wickets. Score:—
