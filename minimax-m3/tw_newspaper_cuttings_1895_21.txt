GUILDFORD C.C. v NEWTOWNS C.C. — These clubs met on Saturday, in their return fixture at Sandleford park. The game, which ended in a win for Guildhall, was remarkable for low scoring, the bowlers taking everything before them, in addition to which the fielding at both sides was good. Humphries and Lucas for Guildhall bowled splendidly, the former capturing 12 wickets for 53 many runs, and the latter 7 for 8. Allsop and Williamson took the bowling honours for Newtown. The teams were entertained on the ground, and a hearty vote of thanks was accorded Lord Arthur and Lady Butler (who were present) for their kindness, also to Mr Allsop. Score:—

PARISH CHURCH INSTITUTE v HIGHCLERE. — The return match between these teams was played, by kind permission, on the cricket ground attached to splendid weather. Highclere winning the toss decided to bat first, but owing to the bowling of Gunning and Gayfer, the first four wickets fell for 21, when Collins came in and made a useful stand, necessitating several changes in the bowling. Still runs came, Collins doing the lion's share, but Gunning getting on at the top end fairly beat him with a beauty, whilst the Thirkills made some useful stands. After the interval the Institute started batting with Cook and Pyke, the former hitting his wicket, whilst Pyke contented himself by keeping up his end. Messrs. and R. Hobbs bowled well for Lambourn, chiefly putting on 18 in quick time. The other notable double figures were Pyke, Young and Gunning. Both teams were entertained to tea, through the kindness of Miss L.
