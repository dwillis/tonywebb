LORD COBHAM'S XI v. MRS. ISAAC'S XI. (UNDER 17).—This match was played on the County Ground at Boughton on August 14th, in very pleasant weather. Lord Cobham's boys batted first, and made a good start, the first five all getting into double figures, of which Seymour's 25 was the highest score. Ninety-five was reached when the fifth wicket fell, but the last five only added 18 runs. E. Lyttelton taking four for 20 runs. The home eleven made an unfortunate start, losing two wickets for 6 runs. Thanks, however, to a good innings by E. Mackintosh, and some free hitting by Hartshorn and P. Lyttelton, the total amounted to 65. Lord Cobham's XI in their second innings made 77 for the loss of four wickets, of which C. Mackintosh scored an excellent 48 not out, including five 4's and five 3's. The innings being closed at this point, Mr. Isaac's boys had to bat for 75 minutes, with a balance of 65 runs to wipe off. Again they had a very poor start, as three wickets fell for a single run. J. Isaac and Hartshorn put, however, a different face upon matters, and raised the score materially. Hartshorn, who left at 30, hit one 6 in his freely made 22. Isaac was still not out for 23 when time was called. The match was decided by the first innings, which gave victory to Lord Cobham's team by 18 runs. Score:—

ST. JOHN'S 2ND XI v. MALVERN 2ND XI.—The return match between these clubs was played at Malvern on Thursday, and resulted in a win for St. John's by 11 runs. St. John's lost seven wickets for 21 runs, and then the last three put on 22. Mr. L. James, who was the only one to reach double figure, hit well for his total. Messrs. Park, Isaac, and Curtis also batted well. For Malvern, Messrs. W. H. Marsland and Romer were top scorers; but, owing to some smart catches being taken, the total only reached 32. The bowling of Messrs. Bannister and Robinson for St. John's and Messrs. Clogg and Jenkins for Malvern was very good. Score:—
