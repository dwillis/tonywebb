IS E. TOMLIN TO QUALIFY FOR SOMERSET-
SHIRE?
We hear glowing accounts from Oxford of the
abilities of E. Tomlin, brother of the county profes-
sional of that name. He is bowling on the 'Varsity
ground to such well-known players as G. J. Mor-
daunt, P. F. Warner, H. K. Foster, and all the best
cricketers up there, and his services are evidently
appreciated, as we hear that he has already been
approached with a view to his playing for Somerset-
shire. This would prove a great loss to Leicestershire,
and our county committee would do well to take the
matter in hand.
