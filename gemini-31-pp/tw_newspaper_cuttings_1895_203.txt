THE WREXHAM TOUR.

FIRST MATCH.--WREXHAM v. LLANDUDNO.
Wrexham played the first fixture of their tour at Llandudno, on Wednesday. The day was beautifully fine, and a capitally contested game ended in a draw. J. G. Jagger, the brothers Hollings, Lieutenant Throckmorton, and the Rev L. F. Ward batted well for their runs, while with the ball Mr Ward captured five wickets for 40 runs, and Read three for 25. At one time it looked as if Llandudno were going to make a very poor show with the bat, as their first six wickets fell for 48 runs. Score:-

THIRD MATCH.--WREXHAM v. BRYNYNEUADD.
On Friday the Wrexham team appeared at Llanfairfechan, and opposed Mr Platt's eleven on his beautifully situated ground.
