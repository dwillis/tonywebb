Poole Park had a narrow squeak on Wednesday against Blandford, and only the intervention of time saved them from defeat. Their downfall in the second innings was remarkable, as on a first attempt a respectable total had been compiled.

The opening day's play in the Bournemouth v. M.C.C. and ground fixture was completely spoilt by the rain. The M.C.C. won the toss, and elected to bat, Hearne and Hulton opening to the bowling of Head and Andrew. Hearne scored a three from Head's first over, and was then bowled by Andrew. Head got the new comer caught at short leg for the addition of another three, at which play ceased for the day, several adjournments having been made whilst these runs were being scored. A fair number of spectators witnessed the proceedings.

The Poole match, v. Mr. Spencer Smith's XI., was also a failure. When the game was abandoned Poole had scored 50 for six wickets, George Gill contributing more than half the total.

With the match against Civil Service, played at Parkstone on Saturday last, the Arnold College Cricket Club brought the season to a close. Altogether 19 matches have been played, of which 15 have been won, 3 lost, and one drawn. Mr. Philbrick has batted well and consistently, and his scored over 1,100 runs, whilst Mr. Langhorne has met with great success in the bowling department, having taken 135 wickets at an average of 6.51 per wicket. W. Reece and L. Reece have both met with considerable success in both batting and bowling. J. H. Philbrick was of much assistance to the team during the latter part of the season.
