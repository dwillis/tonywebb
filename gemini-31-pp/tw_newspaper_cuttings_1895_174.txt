NORTHAMPTON HERALD SATURDAY 10 AUGUST 1895-2.

BEDFORDSHIRE v. M.C.C. AND GROUND.
Twelve aside played in this two days' match, which was commenced at Bedford on Wednesday in fine weather. The visitors went first to the wickets, and were dismissed for 122, Barnes heading the score with 29. W. F. Brown took seven wickets for 44. Bedfordshire commenced batting at a quarter-past four, L. C. R. Thring and Manton facing the bowling of Needham and Richardson. The latter was soon dismissed for eight, but the former made a stand of an hour and a half, in which time he had compiled 78 in excellent style, when he unfortunately played on. Pope, his partner, had made 43 (not out), when stumps were drawn. The match resulted in a creditable win for the home county by an innings and 28 runs. Score:—
