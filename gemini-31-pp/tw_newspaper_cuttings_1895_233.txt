WELLAND AND DISTRICT V. LEDBURY.-Played at
Welland on 25th July. The wicket was soft, and the
runs came very slowly. Ledbury went in first and
were all dismissed for 40, owing to the bowling of
Parr and Hill. Welland started well by making 45
for three wickets, chiefly owing to Gunn (25) and
Coventry (22). Farley bowled well for Ledbury.

HANLEY CASTLE GRAMMAR SCHOOL V. MALVERN
LINK.-Played at Malvern Link on Thursday, on a
wet wicket, time alone preventing the boys gaining a
