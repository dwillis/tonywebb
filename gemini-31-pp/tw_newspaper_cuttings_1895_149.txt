CATHEDRAL CHOIR v. ST. PETER PERMOUNTER-
GATE.—Played at Lower Close on August 20th, and
resulted in a win for the Cathedral Choir by 23 runs.
Corbyn, Smith I, and Hill played well for the
Cathedral, and Lee and Alcock for the losing side.
Cathedral Choir—Hill 2, Smith I 6, England 0,
Corbyn 11, Beeston 1, Self 0, Darken 0, Smith II 0,
Day 0, Steward 0, Dayton 1, extras 13; total, 35.
St. Peter Permountergate—Lee 5, Alcock 1, Hubbard
2, Baldry 2, Betts I 0, Parks 0, Blowers 0, Betts II 2,
Dix 1, Harvey 0, Page 0, extra 1; total, 12.

ST PAUL'S v SILVER ROAD.—Played at Mousehold
on August 17th, and resulted in a win for St. Paul's
by 27 runs. The bowling of the Rev J Calvert and
B M Jackson was good. The former captured four
wickets for 5 runs, and the latter five for 8, and B E
Jackson also batted well for the winners; while
Lubbock bowled well for the losers. St Paul's—B M
Jackson 5-1, Rev J Calvert 5-31, B E Jackson not out
18-11, G F Hill 3-10 not out, Gilbert Hill 0, H Hill
1-0 not out, Mann 0-8, Adcock 2, Breed 0, Ellis 1,
Twiddy 0, extras 8-8; total 44-61. Silver Road—
Boyce 0, Browne 0, Ramsbottom 3, Lubbock 5, Payne
2, Burton 2, Gibbs 0, High 1, Simpson 0, Kiddell not
out 3, extras 1; total 17.

THORPE VILLAGE v. NORWICH UNION.—Played at
Thorpe on Thursday, and resulted in a win for the
Village by 42 runs. Thorpe batted first, and thanks
to Black (21) and Newstead (13), they were able to
make 64 runs. On the N.U.F.C. going in to bat only
Smith could make any stand against the bowling of
Black and Newstead, who took five for 8 and four for
11 respectively. Thorpe Village—Pitis 1, Parker 5,
Wright 5, Black 21, Newstead 13, Desborough 1, Sopp
0, Harper 4, Woodcock 2, Fox 5, Fenn not out 0,
extras 3; total 64. Norwich Union—W. Walton
2, W. C. Walton 3, Smith 9, Cross 0, Smith not out 11,
Browne 1, Corsbie 0, Coleman 2, Drane 0, Rodd 0,
Exshaw 0, extras 3; total 22.

ACLE v. CARROW SECOND.—Played at Acle, and
resulted in an easy win for the home team by an
innings and 27 runs. For the winners H. Holmes
batted well, and W. Holmes and J. Hawes did good
service with the ball, each securing four wickets at a
cost of only 3 runs. For the losers Turner batted freely.
Acle—H Holmes 41, Rev E Young 1, Rix 7, J Holmes
1, W Holmes 1, Hawes 5, F Holmes 1, Walley 1,
Riches 1, Young 4, Jeary 8, extras 7; total 91.
Carrow Second—Murray 11-0, Palgrave 0-7, Goreham
3-0, Shaw 0-4, Turner 6-17, Parslyn 0-1, Hinks 0-0,
Cowles 3-3, Smith 4-0, Cornwall 1-0, Drake 0-1;
totals 31-33.

P. LEEDER'S ELEVEN v. BROOKE.—Played at Brooke
Park on Saturday and resulted in a win for Brooke by
an innings and 31 runs. A. Bunbury, who gave an
easy chance early in the innings, settled down and
played sterling cricket. For the losers Barnby played
steadily. A. Brinn also bowled well. Brooke—Bun-
bury 58, Holmes 5, F Vincent 8, Middleton 0, Hupton
2, H Vincent not out 6, Brinn 0, Minns 17, Nurse 0,
Holmes 2, Frost 0, extras 5; total 110. P Leeder's

Leeder 1-2, Barnby not out 11-10, F Leeder 0-7, W
Brayne 2-2, Lloyd 0-1, J Leeder 0-2 not out, Rix 0-6,
H Leeder 0-0, extras 1-8; totals 19-60.
