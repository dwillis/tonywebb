GREENFIELD.
CRICKET. - GREENFIELD v PULLOXHILL. - This
match was played at Greenfield on Monday, and ended
in a win for Pulloxhill by eight runs on the first
innings. For the winners, Tookey and Parker both
batted well. Scores:-Pulloxhill: G. Pearce b S.
Weston, 4; R. Goodman c Osborn b S. Weston, 3; E.
Parker c S. Weston b Wilsher, 5; W. Tookey c W.
Weston b S. Weston, 17; D. Pearce b S. Weston, 0;
T. Read b Wilsher, 4; F. Abott run out, 3; A. Vass
b Wilsher, 0; A. Clarke b Wilsher, 0; L. Woodland b
S. Weston, 0; S. Marsh not out, 0; extra, 1: total,
37. Greenfield: S. Weston c Read b G. Pearce, 0; S.
Wilsher b G. Pearce, 1; P. Osborn b G. Pearce, 9;
W. Weston c G. Pearce b Tookey, 4; C. Osborn c G.
Pearce b Parker, 6; B. Abbott b Parker, 3; G. Clarke
run out, 0; R. Line b Parker, 2; A. Upton c Parker
b G. Pearce, 0; L. Jones not out 1; J. Cherry b G.
Pearce, 2; extras, 1; total, 29. In the second innings
Pulloxhill scored 24, and there being only ten minutes
left for play Greenfield did not bat.

VOLUNTEERS AT CRICKET.
A match was played at Churn Camp on Wednesday
last week, between the Luton Detachment and the
Remainder of the 3rd Volunteer Battalion, resulting
in a win for Luton by 28 runs. Scores:-Luton
Detachment, 75; Remainder of Battalion, 47.

HOCKLIFFE.
CRICKET.-A cricket match was played at Hockliffe
on Saturday, between the local cricket eleven and a
Woburn team, resulting in a victory for the home team,
the scores being :-Hockliffe, 95; Woburn, 75.
