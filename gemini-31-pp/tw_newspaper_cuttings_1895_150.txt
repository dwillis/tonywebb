BURTON LATIMER v. WELLINGBORO' ALL SAINTS' B.---Played at Wellingboro' on Saturday, the visitors winning by 114 runs.
KETTERING WANDERERS v. GRETTON.---Played at Kettering on Saturday, the visitors winning by 39 runs to 86.
LINCOLN.---The Bracebridge Asylum eleven met the Lincoln Imps on Saturday. The Asylum went in first, and scored 50 for eight wickets, Bennett contributing 16. The Imps then went in, but had only scored 41 when their last wicket fell. The losers would only have registered a very poor total had it not been for a well-hit 25 by Cox.
ROTHWELL INDEPENDENT v. KETTERING BRITANNIA.-At Rothwell on Saturday, an interesting game ending in a draw.
