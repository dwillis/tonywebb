MAIDEN ERLEGH CRICKET WEEK.
THIRD MATCH v. FREE FORESTERS.—This match,
played on the Maiden Erlegh ground, terminated on
Friday in last week, when again there was a large
and fashionable assemblage to witness the game. The
result was an easy victory for the home team, who were
superior to their opponents both in batting and bowling,
and also in fielding. Score:—

Erlegh cricket week was concluded on Saturday, when,
in showery weather, the home club beat the Aldershot
Division somewhat easily. Score:—

SUTTON'S JUNIORS V. SULHAMPSTEAD JUNIORS.— This
match was played at Sulhampstead, on Wednesday, and
resulted in an easy victory for the former, by an innings
and nine runs. Score: Sutton's Juniors, 72; Sulhamp-
stead, 20 and 43.
MR T. W. GIRDLESTONE'S XI v GIRDLESTONEITES
(CHARTERHOUSE).—This match was played at Sunning-
dale School on July 31 and won by 107 runs by the home
side, A. Daffen performing the ' hat trick." The school
thus concluded a very successful season's cricket, the
Boys' Eleven having only lost five matches out of nine-
teen, the 2nd Eleven one out of three, whilst the Masters
have only lost one out of seven played. Score:—
