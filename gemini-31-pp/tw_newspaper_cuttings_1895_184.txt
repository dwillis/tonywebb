THAME 2ND XI. v. OXFORD HIGH SCHOOL.—
Played at Oxford on Wednesday July 10th, ending in
a victory for the School by 33 runs on the first innings.
The School went in first, and scored 61 before they
were got rid of. On Thame going in, they were all
dismissed for the paltry total of 28, B. O. Corbett and
Andrews sharing the bowling honours with five
wickets each. The second venture of the School
realised 46. Score :—Oxford High School, 61 and 48 ;
Thame 2nd, 28.

ATHLETIC CLUB v. IPSDEN C.C.—The return match
between these teams was played at Ipsden on
Saturday last, and resulted in a victory for the visi-
tors by 29 runs on the first innings. It was a good
evenly-contested game throughout, both sides acquit-
ting themselves in gallant style. For the W.A.C.
Daymond, Peyman, and Pounsett did yeoman service,
the trio compiling no less than 62 runs between them,
the latter two also distinguishing themselves with
the ball as well as the bat, taking three wickets for
nine runs, and four for 32 respectively. Arding and
Hillier proved capital mainstays of the home team,
both making serviceable scores. After the match the
players were hospitably entertained by Lieutenant
Arding, and an enjoyable evening was spent. Score :—
