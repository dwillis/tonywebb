ROYAL CORNWALL GAZETTE : THURSDAY 22 AUGUST 1895 - 2.

Cricket.

ST. AUSTELL v. ST. COLUMB
At St. Columb. St. Austell 97 (Bray 19). St. Columb, 22.

ST. MAWES v. GERRANS.
At St. Mawes. St. Mawes 71 (Fookes 15, Jenkin 11). Gerrans 22.

CAMBORNE v. H.M.S. GANGES.
Played at Mylor. Ganges, 34. Camborne, 152 (W. J. Bennetts 33, J. V. Gilbert 29, C. W. Rule 21, R. Holman 15, W. H. Rogers 15, M. Trevartha 12).

POOL v. REDRUTH.
At Redruth on Saturday. Redruth 51 (Tamblyn 12, Winn not out 14.) Pool 81 (Phillips 25, Thomas 15, H. Teague 15, J. Teague 15).

FALMOUTH v. VISITORS.
Played at Falmouth, the visitors being beaten easy. Score:—Falmouth, 133 (Captain Fanshawe not out 59, F. Fanshawe 24, H. Vincent 19, R. Fanshawe 12. Visitors, 90 (J. W. Symons 25, Rev. W. Withnell 20, C. S. Horley not out 19, Rev. R. Robinson 14).

LAUNCESTON v. 1st ST. MARY'S
(LAUNCESTON).
This match, which was originally fixed for Thurs-day week, but was postponed in consequence of the rain, was played at Pennygillam, and resulted in a win for the Towners by four wickets and 108 runs. Score:—St. Marys, 33. Launceston, 141 for 6 wkts. (Redstone not out 60, Lyne 26, Martyn 19, Rev. J. H. Arundell 12).

TRURO v. PENZANCE.
The match at the close was very exciting. Truro played really against time and though at one time there appeared to be no hope to defeat a draw, wickets were so disposed of as to dismiss the last of the visitors just as the clock struck the hour for drawing stumps; thus leaving Truro the winners.
