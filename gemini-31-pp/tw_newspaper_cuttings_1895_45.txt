CAMBRIDGE BOROUGH POLICE v. CAMBS.
COUNTY POLICE.
On Wednesday these Clubs met in a return
match on Trinity College Ground. The County
went to the wicket first, but were dismissed for
24. For the Borough Williamson secured five
wickets for nine runs, and Rackham five for 10
runs. In the second innings Williamson got six
wickets for 22, and Rackham three for 23.
Brooks and Taylor divided the wickets for the
County. An excellent lunch was provided for the
players and friends. The following are the scores:
