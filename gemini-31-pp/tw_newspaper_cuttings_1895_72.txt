RAGLAN BARRACKS v. NORTH DEVON.
This match was played at Mount Wise yesterday afternoon.
The Raglan Barracks team was composed of members of the
Somerset Light Infantry and Berkshire Regiment. The Barracks
went in first on a comparatively good wicket, and knocked up
107. In reply the visitors knocked up 115 for six wickets, thus
securing a substantial win. Score:—

TAVISTOCK v. PLYMOUTH.
Played at Tavistock on Tuesday. Plymouth began well with
Conry and Collins, putting on 58 for the first wicket, but against
the bowling of Wood and Milford the others only added 58, and
the innings closed for 116. Tavistock started badly, losing Penny
without scoring. Serle-(pro), although he had a little bit of
luck, played a sound game, further proving him to be one of
Devon's best bats at present. The innings realised 136, the
home team winning a good game by 20 runs. Score:—

SIDMOUTH v. INCOGNITI.
This match commenced at Sidmouth on Wednesday and con-
cluded yesterday, the Incogs winning by an innings and 23 runs.
Rain interfered with the match the first day, and when stumps
were drawn the visitors had made 155 for the loss of seven wickets.
Yesterday the wicket was altogether in favour of the bowlers.
The innings of the Incogs was soon finished, and Sidmouth
were disposed of the first time for 61 runs before lunch. Going
in in the afternoon they only raised 78. Hixson and Fox bowled
splendidly, the former taking twelve wickets during the match
for 73 runs, and the latter eight for 53. Score:—
