COLESHILL C.C. v. ST. MARGARET'S C.C.
The return match between the above clubs was played at
Coleshill on Saturday, August 10th, St. Margaret's again
proving successful, which was chiefly owing to the brilliant
all-round cricket of Mr. C. C. Powell, who not only made the
highest score for his side, but actually took 5 wickets for 6
runs. Mr. J. Dean also bowled wonderfully well, taking the
other 5 for 20 runs. Coleshill went to the wickets first, and
were soon disposed of, Mr. E. M. Weatherby being top scorer
with 11, and the innings closed for 28. St. Margaret's now
commenced their task of making 29 to win, which was soon
accomplished. Fountain made 11; Mr. Powell made 21,
which was compiled without the slightest chance, and the
innings realised 78. Coleshill went to the wickets a second
time, and did much better, having made 73 for 3 wickets
when time was called. Full score and analysis:-
