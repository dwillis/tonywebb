COLESHILL C.C. v. ST. MARGARET'S C.C.
The return match between the above clubs was played at Coleshill on Saturday, August 10th, St. Margaret's again proving successful, which was chiefly owing to the brilliant all-round cricket of Mr. C. C. Powell, who not only made the highest score for his side, but actually took 6 wickets for 6 runs. Mr. J. Dean also bowled wonderfully well, taking the other 3 for 20 runs. Coleshill went to the wickets first, and were soon disposed of, Mr. R. M. Weatherby being top scorer with 11, and the innings closed for 29. St. Margaret's now commenced their task of making 30 to win, which was soon accomplished. Fountain made 11; Mr. Powell made 21, which was compiled without the slightest chance, and the innings realised 78. Coleshill went to the wickets a second time, and did much better, having made 73 for 3 wickets when time was called. Full score and analysis:—
