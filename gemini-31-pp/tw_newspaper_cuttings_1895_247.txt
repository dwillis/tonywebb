ROYAL MUNSTER FUSILIERS V ARMY SERVICE
CORPS.
BATTING EXTRAORDINARY—THE RECORD BEATEN
Captain W. Coape Oates, Royal Munster Fusiliers,
the popular Captain of the Curragh District C.C., and
Private Fitzgerald, of the same battalion, yesterday
had the distinction of topping the batting record by
compiling the immense score of 623 runs during their
partnership, and as both batsmen were not out at the
call of "time," when they seemed good for another 100,
it is impossible to estimate what might have happened
had the game been prolonged. The building up of this
huge score occupied exactly 4 1/2 hours, yielding an aver-
age of 131 runs per hour. 603 is supposed to have been
the previous record, that being the score credited to
Messrs A. H. Trevor and G. F. Vernon in a match,
Orleans Club v Rickley Green, some years ago, but it is
understood both batsmen were "out." Captain Oates,
who has done so much for cricket, especially for the
ground on which the immense score has been compiled,
richly merits the distinction of having contributed the
lion's share towards this remarkable performance, and
it bears eloquent testimony to the value of the improve-
ments to the ground inaugurated by him eight months
ago, that this new record score should have followed so
closely the score of over 400 runs, recently made by the
Curragh District against Dublin University. Score:—

IRISH CRICKET UNION.
A meeting of the committee of the Leinster branch of
the Irish Union was held last evening in the Wicklow
Hotel, when there were present—Dr. E. W. Frith (in the
chair), Mr F. Stokes (Dundrum), Mr H. Thornton (Pem-
broke), Mr C. Mitchell (Pembroke), Mr. J. Cotter (Dun-
drum), Mr F. M. Browning (Dublin University), hon.
sec. ; Mr. J. Foxall (Leinster), hon. treasurer. Mr
Foxall announced that the receipts at the recent Gentle-
men of Ireland v M.C.C. match amounted to £62, and
after the discharge of all liabilities a balance of close on
£30 remained to the credit of the union—a very satis-
factory state of affairs. The Junior Cup competition
was discussed, and it was decided that clubs, in order
to be eligible, should be affiliated with the Leinster
Branch by July 1st. Entries for the cup close on July
14th.
