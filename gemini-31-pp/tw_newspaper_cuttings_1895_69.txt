R.M.L.I. v. PLYMOUTH COLLEGE.
The return match between these teams took place at Mount
Wise yesterday in splendid weather, the Marines winning by 111
runs. Batting first, the visitors were all disposed of for 31, R. A.
Lake contributing 15. The Marines, in reply, scored 141, Captain
Curtels, Lieutenant Morse, and Corporal McKean being the
principal scorers. Following on, the visitors scored 159, Shack-
lock 52, Lake 32, Mere 22, and Shanks 20, batting well.

PLYMOUTH v. STONEHOUSE.
This match was played at South Devon-place yesterday, and
resulted in an easy win for the home team. Batting first, the
home team put together 121, P. P. Tyacke, H. B. Spencer, and H.
R. E. Harrison scoring most of the runs, and played very fine
cricket against the good bowling, coupled with accurate fielding,
of the visitors. J. G. Davey bowled thoroughly well, sent down
23 overs 14 maidens four wickets for 24 runs. The innings of the
visitors was a poor display, owing to the brilliant trundling of H.
R. E. Harrison. The Plymouth man bowled at a tremendous rate,
and took eight wickets for but 2 runs. Following on the visitors
fared a trifle better, and scored 10 for eight wickets. The wicket-
keeping of S. Spinney was very fine, only 2 leg byes being regis-
tered. Score :—
