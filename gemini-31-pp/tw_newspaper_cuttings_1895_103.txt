CHELTENHAM v HAILEYBURY.
Frequent heavy showers caused stoppages in this match
at Lord's on Saturday, and at six o'clock the game was
abandoned altogether. The draw, if anything, was slightly
in favour of Haileybury, who on the first innings had an
advantage of 50. On both sides cricketers of more than
ordinary promise took part, whilst Woof, the old Glouces-
tershire bowler and coach at Cheltenham, speaks very
highly of one or two young cricketers that have yet to get
into the eleven. Champain is to be accorded a place in the
Gloucestershire team against Middlesex, whilst it is quite
possible that Barrett will be tried for Hampshire. Score:-
