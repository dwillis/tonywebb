G.W.R. V. BATH ASSOCIATION.
The G.W.R. team placed another victory to their
credit in above match at Bath, on Saturday last,
although they were minus the services of Pearman,
Beachcroft and Major. The home captain won the toss
and decided to bat first, but it is questionable whether
this was of any advantage, as the wicket was somewhat
soft, and Bown and Overton again bowled with great
effect, the former having the remarkably good result of
eight wickets for ten runs. Bath were all dismissed for
39 runs in about forty-five minutes, only Twining reach-
ing double figures. The first four wickets of the visitors
fell for 37 runs, but H. Hartley and C. Smith increased
the score to 56 before the latter was bowled. H.
Hartley played another good innings and was top
scorer, Beanland and A. Hartley rendering useful
assistance, the score eventually reaching 102, or 63 to the
good. Bath did much better in their second attempt,
Twining again batting well, his 56 being the result of
some fine hitting, and included four drives out of the
ground. It is only fair to state, however, that the
G.W.R. were without the bowling of Bown in the second
innings, that player having to leave early. Kirby
bowled well, taking three wickets for two runs. With a
few minutes more to spare, the visitors would probably
have won the match outright by eight wickets, as only
seven runs were required to win at the call of time.
