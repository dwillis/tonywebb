"HEREFORD TIMES" CRICKET CHALLENGE TROPHY.
SEMI-FINAL.
PRESTEIGN V MALVERN.
This match was played at Presteign on Thursday in last
week, the home side winning by 33 runs after a good game.
For the winners the Rev. H. G. Green-Price, F. L. Evelyn,
W. T. Williams, and W. Green-Price batted finely, whilst
P. Davies met with most success with the ball. The feature
of Malvern's display was the all round play of W. H.
Hartland, who secured four of the Presteign wickets, besides
carrying his bat through the innings for 24. Score:-

SHROPSHIRE V LUDLOW AND SOUTH SHROP-
SHIRE.
This match was played on Saturday on the Shrewsbury
ground. Ludlow went in first, and scored the good total of
of 201 for nine wickets, although F. L. Evelyn was absent.
F. H. Sitwell (52), Clerke (36), Leake (21), Harding (21), and
Willis (16), all batted well. On the home team going in,
only Sprott and Wood made any stand, and eight wickets
fell for 77 runs, Hinds and Leake bowling exceedingly well.
