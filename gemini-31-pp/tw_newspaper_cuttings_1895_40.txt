CAMBS. CUP COMPETITION.
On Saturday the second round of the Cambs.
Cup Competition was played on Parker's Piece,
between the Old Higher Grade and Sawston.
The match ended in an easy win for the
Old Higher Grade by 190 runs. For Sawston, J.
Bailey was the only scorer, with five runs to his
credit, extras being credited with six. For the
Old Higher Grade, Cornell took six wickets for one
run, and Luff four wickets for four runs. The

MARRIED V. SINGLE.
On Tuesday a second match was played upon
the same ground between Eleven Married and
Eleven Single, Mr Hoare himself taking the bat
with the Married Eleven. It was a well contested
game, the Single team being the victors by 5 runs
only. F Baker was successful with both ball and
bat. Refreshments were dispensed to players and
spectators, and in the evening a substantial supper
was provided for all that had taken part in the
game. About 30 sat down, and Mr Hoare joined
the company. The tables being cleared, Mr Blake,
in appropriate words, proposed the health of the
host, which was drunk with musical honours. A
social and harmonious evening was spent, such as
Sutton cricketers have not often the opportunity
of spending. Score :—
