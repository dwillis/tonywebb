BERROW'S WORCESTER JOURNAL: SATURDAY 27 JULY 1895-3.

WORCESTERSHIRE CLUB AND GROUND v.
HALESOWEN.
At Worcester. Although Club and Ground had
much the best of matters, time nearly saved the
visitors, their last wicket going down to the last ball
of the match. E. C. Hobson and E. G. M. Car-
michael played good cricket for their respective
totals, and Burrows and J. Lightwood bowled well.

WICHENFORD v. ST. JOHN'S 2ND XI.—This return
match was played at Wichenford on July 18, and
resulted in a win for Wichenford by 10 runs. St.
John's batted first, the bowling of Messrs. Hobson
and Williams proving a great deal too effectual for
the opponents. Wichenford found a good point in
the water bailiff. Messrs. Buckland, Hobson, and
Jones Williams batted well for Wichenford ; and
Messrs. H. Appleyard, Parke, and Williams batted
well for St. John's. Score:—

KIDDERMINSTER GRAMMAR SCHOOL v. WORCESTER
GRAMMAR SCHOOL.—Played at Worcester, on July
22. The bowling of the visitors was very deadly, and
four of the home team's wickets fell for no runs.
Shortly afterwards Woodward took three wickets
with three successive balls. Score:—
