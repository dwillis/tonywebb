STANTONBURY, ST. JAMES' v. STONY STRATFORD
MUTUAL.-This match was played at Stantonbury on
Friday, August 2nd, and resulted in a win for the
visitors by 9 runs. This was brought about chiefly by
Allen's 15 runs in the 1st innings, and the bowling of
Allen, H. Martin and Peddar the latter also
performing the hat trick. Bown and Cockerill for
the Mutual bowled well.
