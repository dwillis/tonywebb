OXFORD TIMES SATURDAY 29 JUNE 1895-3.

CHERWELL VALLEY v. HOLYWELL.—On Saturday
the Holywell A.C. went over to Heyford and met the
new Cherwell Valley Club. The latter went in first,
and were all got rid of for 65. Holywell commenced
their innings very disastrously, losing three wickets
for four runs, but the partnership of Craddock and
Churms turned the tables. Later on with eleven runs
to win and two wickets to fall it was a very doubtful
whether the required number would be obtained, but
Fortescue and Hewlett put on seven and six each,
and the score finally reached 69. With three-quarters
of an hour more to play the Valley went to the wicket
for a second time and scored 36 for the loss of six
wickets. Score:—

PRINTERS C.C.—A pleasant and close game was the
result of the above fixture on Saturday last, which

20th instant, at Burford, in beautiful weather. The
visitors went in first and were dismissed for 64 runs.
