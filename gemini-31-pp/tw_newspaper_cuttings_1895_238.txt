BERROW'S WORCESTER JOURNAL - SATURDAY 17 AUGUST 1895 - 3.

WICHENFORD V. T. C. RICHENS' XI.—This match was played at Laughern Hill on Monday, and ended in a win for Wichenford by 18 runs after a most exciting match. Mr. Richens won the toss and went in, and put together a very smart 53, Messrs. Harris and Humphreys playing well for their runs. Wichenford then went in and began disastrously, as six wickets were down for 5, but when B. Preece joined J. Page an excellent stand was made, the former hitting out with perfect ease. G. A. Fidoe was his next partner but one, and when these two got together they hit the bowlers to all parts of the field and eventually won the match. B. Preece's fine innings included two 4's, two 3's, nine 2's, and nine singles. Mr. E. L. Harkness kept wicket for the losers in first-rate style. Williams and Caswell again bowled very effectively for Wichenford. Score:—

WICHENFORD V. CATHEDRAL GUILD.—This match was played at Laughern Hill, and ended in an easy win for Wichenford by 54 runs and 5 wickets. The Guild won the toss and elected to bat on a good wicket, but were soon dismissed, thanks to the excellent bowling of W. Williams and H. Caswell. Both bowled perfectly and with an excellent length. Wichenford got 74 for six wickets and declared, Messrs Hastings, T. C. Williams, and G. F. Jones Williams getting most runs. The Guild then went in again and did slightly better against the same two bowlers, who were bowling capitally, and got for eight wickets. Score:—

ST. JOHN'S 2ND XI. V. COLWALL.—Played at Colwall and resulted in an easy victory for St. John's, Messrs. Beaumont, Dodson, Park, and South all batted well for their runs, Mr. Beaumont being top scorer with a well played innings of 45. Mr. Evans was the only double-figure scorer for Colwall. Mr. Robinson bowled splendidly for St. John's, his analysis reading 6 overs 4 maidens 5 runs and 5 wickets. Mr. Beaumont also bowled well, capturing three wickets for 15 runs. BROMSGROVE V. BRONDESBURY.—At Bromsgrove on Wednesday. Score: Bromsgrove, 46; Brondesbury, 94. In their second innings, Bromsgrove made 114 for the loss of six wickets.
