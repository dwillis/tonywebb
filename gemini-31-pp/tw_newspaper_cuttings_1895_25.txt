NEWBURY WEEKLY NEWS: THURSDAY 12 SEPTEMBER 1895 - 1.

NEWBURY CRICKET CLUB, 1895.

Thirty matches were arranged, of which 22
only were played; eight were scratched (six by
opponents two by Newbury). The General
Election caused Newbury to scratch their two.
Of the matches played 15 were won, 4 were lost,
and 3 drawn. A record was established by
Newbury winning their last six matches
consecutively, which included every match
(five in number) in the cricket week. Mr. C. E.
Slocock's 56 wickets for 8.23 runs per wicket
was a very fine performance. In the six last
matches (including the cricket week ones) the
players were all bona-fide members of the
Newbury Cricket Club, and with very few
exceptions were local men. The captain (Mr.
J. L. Flint) is entitled to a word of praise. It
is no doubt greatly due to his tact and know-
ledge of the members and of their capabilities,
that the club did so well. The teams have, so
far as one can judge, never done otherwise
than played together in a thoroughly good and
sportsmanlike spirit, the result showing that
"unity is strength."
