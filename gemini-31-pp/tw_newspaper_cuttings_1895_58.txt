LIVERPOOL POST: FRIDAY 13 SEPTEMBER 1895: TUESDAY 17 SEPT 1895

LIVERPOOL CRICKET CLUB.

BATTING AND BOWLING AVERAGES.
The first eleven played 37 matches, won 16,
lost 6, and 15 were drawn. Won: Western,
Werneth (twice), Sefton (twice), Rossall School,
Boughton Hall (twice), Bootle, Warrington, Huy-
ton, Birkenhead Park, Preston, Rock Ferry, New
Brighton, and Oxton. Lost: Huyton, [unclear]
College, Manchester, Formby, and Lathom
(twice). Drawn: Ormskirk (twice), Birkenhead
Park, Marlborough College, Clifton College, Chel-
tenham College, Northern (twice), Oxton, Rock
Ferry, Fettesian-Lorettonians, Harrow Wan-
derers, Western, Warrington, and Hoi Papara-
menoi. Runs to the number of 5,951 (including
353 extras) were scored for the loss of 311 wickets,
against 5,380 runs (including 322 extras) for the
loss of 342 wickets. The "A" eleven played 16
matches, won 5, lost 4, and 7 were drawn, scoring
2,282 runs (including 144 extras) for the loss of 125
wickets, against 2,053 runs (including 177 extras)
for the loss of 119 wickets. The second eleven
played 27 matches, won 8, lost 7, and drew 12,
scoring 3,878 runs (including 311 extras) for the
loss of 201 wickets, against 3,897 runs (including
352 extras) for the loss of 326 wickets. The extra
eleven played 6 matches, of which 4 were won
and 2 drawn. In all the club played 86 matches,
of which 33 were won, 17 lost, and 36 drawn. Dis-
trict matches were played against Yorkshire
County and Cambridge University, the former
being won and the latter lost. In addition, 9
extra matches were played, giving a grand total of
97 matches during the season. Although Mr. A.
T. Kemble has only taken part in 21 matches this
season, he has succeeded in stumping and catching
41 opponents.
