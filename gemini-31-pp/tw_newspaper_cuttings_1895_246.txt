KILDARE MILITIA V. BOG OF ALLEN.--The above match was played on the Harrison ground, Curragh Camp, on Saturday, resulting in a brilliant victory for the home team. The visitors won the toss, and sent "Kerry" and Thornhill to the wicket, but the former, before he was well set, was run out by his partner. Thornhill was then joined by de Robeck, who commenced hitting out at once, and quickly ran up a fine innings of 35. Thornhill left shortly afterwards, having contributed a very sound 25. Lech now went to the wicket and played good cricket for his 52 (not out). Sergeant-Mj. Colnett's excellent bowling accounted for the remainder of the team, and the innings closed for 145. Keogh and Dennis started the batting for the Kildares, and the former's acquaintance with the sticks was abruptly brought to a close, and his place was taken by Gason. Dennis was smartly caught by Thornhill at point for a useful 20, and Sergeant-Mj. Colnett joining Gason, the pair hitting out freely brought the score to 100 before the latter was dismissed for 28. The next stand was made by Gresson, who, by the aid of scientific cricket, contributed 35. Wickets began to fall now with great rapidity, Colnett being the first to go after an excellent 67, without giving a single chance. Wornham was the only remaining member of the team who succeeded in getting double figures, being not out for 53. Mr Butler-Brooke, who is better known in the hunting than in the cricket field, made 7, and shortly after the innings closed for 235. Score:--
