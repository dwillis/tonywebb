NORTHAMPTON HERALD : SATURDAY 10 AUGUST 1895-2

NORTHAMPTON TOWN LEAGUE
FIRST SECTION.
Excelsior v. Star.—This match was played on the
County Ground on Saturday, and ended in a draw,
rain on this occasion spoiling what would otherwise
have been a most interesting contest. Batting first,
the Excelsior compiled 145 for eight wickets, when
they declared their innings closed. C. M. Loeve scored
71, included in which were one 5, six 4's, and five 3's,
he being, however, missed three times; A. Quigley
33, and H. Tull 12. G. Squires obtained four wickets
for 37 runs, W. Kirby two for 23, H. Davis one for 10,
and C. Roberts one for 16. Upon the Star batting they
made a rather poor start, F. Kendall, who was in good
trundling form, obtaining three wickets in the first
three overs. F. Cory, who scored 22 and A. Tite, 21
not out, however, made a good stand, and when stumps
were drawn the score was 55 for four wickets. Kendall
had three wickets for 16 and S. Kirkham one for three.
Clarence v. Colts.—Rain also interfered in this
match on the County Ground on Saturday. Batting
first, the Clarence, who only played ten men, scored
152, J. A. Bedford compiling 40, F. Twist 30, and F.
Rolley 15. For the Colts A. Mackness took four
wickets for 31 runs, A. Ingham two for 36, and J.
Brown two for 51. The Colts obtained 86 for two
wickets, T. Lightwood not out 39, A. Mackness 17, and
A. Barlow 15. Venn took both wickets at a cost of
19 runs.
SECOND SECTION.
Trade v. Regent-square.—This match was played on
the Racecourse on Saturday, when the Trade won by
nine wickets and nine runs. Scores: Regent-square,
101 (W. Downing 54); Trade, 110 for one wicket
(Thomas not out 65, Law not out 32). On behalf of
the winners J. Potter took five wickets for 36. The
Trade made their runs in an hour.
Excelsior Albion v. Artisan Star.—This game on
the Racecourse on Saturday ended in a draw in favour
of the latter. Scores: Excelsior Albions, 57 for six
wickets (Swannell 31); Artisan Star, 134 for six
wickets (A. Underwood 40), innings declared closed.
A. Underwood (two for seven) for the Artisan Star,
and Kendall (four wickets) for their opponents, were the
most effective bowlers.
Primitives v. St. Katherine's.—On the Racecourse
the "Prims" won by four wickets. Scores: Primi-
tives, 129 for six (W. Parkin not out 36); St. Kathe-
rine's, 117 (W. Taylor not out 30, Alstow 31, F. Cuthis
28). W. Taylor took three wickets for the winners,
and Thurlby three wickets for the losers.
THIRD SECTION.
Y.M.C.A. v. St. Sepulchre's B.C.—On the Racecourse
on Saturday, the Y.M.C.A. won by seven runs. Scores:
Y.M.C.A., 69; St. Sepulchre's B.C., 62.
G.P.O. 1st Wesleyans v. Wesleyan Star.—The former
won this match on Saturday on the Borough Ground
by 18 runs, scoring 37 runs to their opponents' 19.
Catholic Alma v. St. Giles'.—Played on the Borough
Ground on Saturday, and won by the former. Scores:
Catholic Alma, 35; St. Giles', 33.

BEDFORDSHIRE v. HERTFORDSHIRE.
This match was concluded at Bedford on Tuesday,
and ended in a draw. Overnight Bedfordshire had
been dismissed for 182, and their opponents had scored
16 for one. The play on Tuesday, owing to rain, did
not commence until nearly three o'clock, when C.
and H. Pigg, the not outs, resumed their innings, the
former being bowled by Manton when he had 29 to
his credit, and the latter 31. The innings of 165 did
not close until six o'clock. In the remaining half-hour
Bedfordshire lost four wickets for 47, and the game
ended in an even draw.

Wellingborough Grammar School (Past 2nd XI. v.
Present 2nd XI.)—This match was played at Welling-

Present, 186.
St. Paul's v. Pioneer 2nd XI.—Played on the Race-
course on Saturday, and won by St. Paul's by six
wickets. Scores: St. Paul's, 52 for four wickets;
Pioneer 2nd XI., 51.
Moulton v. Overstone Park.—Played on Saturday at
Overstone, and resulted in a win for the former by 26
runs. Moulton batted first, scoring 55, to which Over-
stone replied with 29. The wicket was very dead, and
favoured the bowlers. For the visitors Mabbutt, Main,
and Manning batted well, while Barnes was the highest
scorer for the homesters. The Overstone wickets fell
evenly to Cox and Glasspool, while Barnes accounted
for the majority of those of Moulton.
Pioneer v. Church and Co. C.C.—Played on the
Borough Ground on Saturday, when the Pioneer won
easily by six wickets and 53 runs. Scores: Church's,
44; Pioneer, 97 for four wickets.
