WEST HERTS AND WATFORD OBSERVER: FRIDAY 18 AUGUST 1939-3.

LORD'S STAFF PLAYER HITS
101 AFTER SHOCKS

STANMORE (8 wkts.) 192 .................. LORD'S GROUND STAFF 254

IN half-an-hour Lord's Ground Staff lost three wickets for 35 runs; all fell to K. H. Chapman, who hit the stumps with his first ball. Hayman offered sterner resistance, but two more wickets fell before time was taken with the score at 110 for five wickets. Hayman went on to make his century, well supported by the later batsmen. They batted briskly, but showed great respect for the bowling of Wendham Griffiths, who in 15 overs conceded only 47 runs and bowled out two of the most successful batsmen. Stanmore were set to score 255 runs in three hours, normally an easy task, but here the bowling proved to be too accurate to allow quick scoring with safety, and the game finished in a draw. Score:

ANOTHER DON YATES CENTURY

Bill End Hospital 23 ........ Odhams (0 wkts., dec.) 156

Don Yates was at it again on Saturday with a fine innings of 108 not out. He and D. Edis (41) scored 156 runs without being separated. When Bill End Hospital went in to bat, they could do nothing against the spin bowling of H. Beach (four for 5) and J. Prescott (four for 4), for they were all out for 23. Score:
