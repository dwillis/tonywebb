ONLY THREE BATSMEN
REACH DOUBLE FIGURES

READING'S COLLAPSE AGAINST
THE DUCKS

READING v. THE DUCKS

Played at Earley on Sunday, and won by The
Ducks by 69 runs. Taking first knock on a wicket
that was very easy before lunch, The Ducks' early
batsmen scored very slowly and carefully, and gave
the side a good start with 106 for 3. Then 6 wickets
fell for 18 runs, but a last wicket stand added 31,
the total being 155. C. L. Grieshaber (3 for 20) and
A. V. Percival (3 for 32) were the most successful
bowlers. Only three Reading batsmen reached double
figures, Grieshaber (23) being top scorer, and the
whole side was out for 86.

WICKET FAVOURS THE BOWLERS

READING v. OLD REDINGENSIANS

Played at Earley on Monday, and won by Reading
by 39 runs. There had been heavy rain in the
morning, and play did not begin until just before 2
p.m., Reading taking first knock. In two hours the
whole side was out for 82, H. W. Neilan (24), who
was well caught by C. N. West, making the most
runs. A. W. Flatman (6 for 25) had bowled well for
the O.R.s. Then C. J. Woollhead (3 for 16) and
H. W. Neilan (7 for 26), bowling unchanged, dismissed the O.R.s in 84 minutes for 43 runs. To play
out time, each side batted a second time for 45
minutes, Reading again proving the better side with
88 for 5 (Thirkill 38, and Neilan 25 not out), against
42 for 7 (Percival taking 3 wickets for 9 runs in one
over).

O.R.s' CRICKET WEEK

BERKSHIRE GENTLEMEN BEATEN
BY 20 RUNS

OLD REDINGENSIANS v. BERKSHIRE
GENTLEMEN

Played at Reading School on Friday last week.
In a low-scoring match the O.R.s won by 20 runs.
For the Gentlemen, S. Bradfield took 4 wickets for
11 and W. G. Bott 4 wickets for 10. For the O.R.s,
J. Caudle took 5 for 19 and K. G. Fidler 3 for 13.
