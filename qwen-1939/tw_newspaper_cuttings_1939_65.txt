WEST HERTS AND WATFORD OBSERVER: FRIDAY 18 AUGUST 1939-4.

CAPTAIN'S BOWLING
TURNED THE SCALE

RICKMANSWORTH 116 ............ CHORLEYWOOD (9 wkts. dec.) 135
A DEADLY second spell by Cooper, the Chorleywood captain, put the brake on Rickmansworth's gallop on Saturday, and turned the match in the visitors' favour. Rickmansworth after a good start soon lost wickets to Cooper, whose analysis was five for 33.
Chorleywood, winning by 19, had revenge for their defeat earlier in the season.
Ashwell and M. Whitteridge, putting on 61 for the first wicket, were the only Chorleywood batsmen to withstand the attack of Bradley (five for 24) and D. H. Shepherd (three for 32). Roger Evans (28) was an exception. Score:

PINNER HAD FIVE WICKETS
TO SPARE

Pinner
Old Merchant Taylors 112........(8 wkts.) 141
Old Merchant Taylors were below strength and batted poorly on a slow wicket, on which the ball kept low. H. Harold, this year's School captain, was an exception. The pitch became easier later, though never at any time difficult, and Pinner had no difficulty after losing a few early wickets. Pinner passed the Taylors' score for the loss of five wickets.
