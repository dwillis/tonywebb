"SHIRES" CONVINCING WIN
In their Hospital Cup match in Bedford Park on Tuesday evening, the Shire Hall XI proved altogether too strong for their Ickwell visitors. For the winners, W. Furlong took seven wickets for nine runs. Scores:-
Ickwell.-D. Humphries 11; G. Reeks 4; G. Brunt 1; G. Dilley 0; A. Elliman 0; N. Dunger 5; E. Edwards 7; G. Turnbull 2; H. Newell 0; G. Parrish 0 (not out); E. Thompson 0; extras 1; total 31.

KEMPSTON METHODISTS' REVENGE

COLMWORTH BOWLER'S FEAT
In their Bedford League (Div. II., Eastern) match at home with Woodbury on Saturday, Colmworth have to thank R. Chamberlain for their easy win. He and V. T. Pell soon dismissed the visitors for 18 runs, and Chamberlain took 5 wickets for 6 runs. Scores:-
Colmworth.-A. Wilson 4; V. Pell 3; J. Darrington 10; S. Matthews 8; J. Chamberlain 3; A. Thompson 5; A. Chamberlain 7; R. Chamberlain 4; Jack Swales 0; V. T. Pell 1 (not out); Jim Swales 0; extras 4; total 50.

SCHOOLBOY'S BRILLIANT BATTING
On Tuesday evening, the Goldington Road School claimed a victory when they met the Silver Jubilee School in the Elementary Schools' League. K. Hullatt and D. Harlow were the opening pair for Goldington Road, and Harlow was dismissed for three, but Hullatt went on to make 51. With the rest of the side making 18 runs between them, the total reached was 74. Silver Jubilee were rather unfortunate in losing their first four wickets for eight runs, but with the help of Haynes (22) and Wiffin (7) the score of 48 was reached. Scores:-
Goldington Road School.-Hullatt 52; Harlow 3; Berwick 5; Webster 2; Stacey 0; Wadner 0; Saint 0; Burton 5; Wilson 0; Colbert 3; Abbott 0; extras 4; total 74.
Silver Jubilee School.-Bayliss 5; Sims 0; Huckle 0; Bennett 3; Haynes 22; Whiffin 7; Johnson 2; Cook 1; Lane 2; Leo 0; Hoole 6; extras 0; total 48.

ONE BATSMAN OUTSTANDING
CARRIES HIS BAT THROUGHOUT
The resistance of A. Woodward for Caldecote II. was responsible for his side's victory over R.B.S. at Bedford on Saturday in the Third Division of the Bedford League. Going in first, he carried his bat throughout the innings for 55 runs. Scores:-
