The Bucks Free Press: Friday 25 August 1939

BEESON WELL ON THE MARK

AFTER Wycombe had declared at 222 for 3, eight Beaconsfield wickets fell in a determined attempt to get the runs and an exciting finish found the visitors only 8 runs short of the Wycombe total.
A brilliant century by Frank Ryan was the outstanding feature of the Wycombe innings, which had started promisingly before Baker was bowled by Worley. Tovey drove powerfully before being caught and bowled and then Ryan and Keen settled down to an attractive partnership of nearly 100.
Keen was eventually bowled by Boddy for 42 and in an attempt to get runs quickly before declaring, Beeson was sent in to hit and he and Ryan carried the score to 222 without further loss. C. G. Boddy bowled well for Beaconsfield without much luck, but R. W. V. Robins, the Middlesex amateur, could not find a length, and failed to take a wicket.
Beaconsfield fielded a fair scoring side, but opened fairly quietly. Montague hit four fours in scoring his 22, and then R. W. V. Robins showed what a useful forcing bat he can be. With straight drives and impudent pulls he caught up the clock and, with C. G. Boddy at the other end, Beaconsfield looked like winning.
Beeson, however, was bowling extremely well and encouraged the batsmen into taking too many liberties. After scoring 91 runs, the former England captain was well caught by Janes in the long field. The visitors still had a chance of winning, but chose to play out time and Wycombe could not manage to take the last two wickets.

EASY WIN FOR BEACONSFIELD

Robins' "Spinners" Create Havoc

LOW SCORING

BEACONSFIELD easily defeated South Oxfordshire Amateurs at Wilton Park on Sunday by 78 runs.
The home side were all out for the rather poor total of 130, V. C. Baylis and D. G. Jeffery being top scorers, but this proved ample for the Amateurs, who were all skittled out for 52.
R. W. V. Robins was again assisting Beaconsfield, and the visitors were all at sea with his spinners, the Middlesex player having the fine analysis of 6 for 28, while D. G. Jeffery also bowled well to take 4 for 24.
