E. T. KILLICK'S GOOD INNINGS
AGAINST ST. ALBANS.
The Rev. E. T. Killick, the England Middlesex and Cambridge University player, batted splendidly for Letchworth at Clarence Park, on Saturday. He had made 72 not out when Letchworth, who batted first, declared with 153 for 4. A S. Hacker (28) and G. F. Beaumont (27), St. Albans' opening pair, put on 57 before they were parted, but after that wickets fell quickly and at the close of the game, which was left drawn, St. Albans had made 92 for 5.

ST. ALBANS BEAT OLD ALBANIANS.
St. Albans beat the Old Albanians by three wickets at Clarence Park, on Sunday. With J. T. Marriott, who took 6 wickets for 22 runs in eight overs, bowling well, St. Albans dismissed the Old Boys for 85, a collapse ensuing after the sixth wicket fell at 74. When St. Albans had seven wickets down for 50, a close finish seemed likely, but W. Lismore (26 not out) and G. Weatherhead (12) knocked off the runs without further loss.

HERTS WITHOUT KINGSLEY.
Herts will be without P. G. T. Kingsley, the skipper, who is in camp with the Territorials, for their matches with Norfolk, at Norwich, on Monday and Tuesday, and with Bedfordshire, at Bedford, on Friday and Saturday. Arthur Grimsdell will captain the team, which will be chosen from: A. Grimsdell, G. E. Fordham, F. Appleyard, G. W. Ford, F. K. Butler, M. A. Wiggs, G. V. Ledger, G. J. Adams, M. H. Kingsley, A. E. Tee, E. L. Lane and Roberts (E.).

L. O. KING'S "HAT TRICK."
L. O. King, of Radlett, performed the "hat trick" for the Herts County Club on Friday, when they beat St. Albans by 184 runs. Taking seven wickets for 33, he was mainly responsible for St. Albans' cheap dismissal. King also batted well, his 67 being the top score of the game. The Country Club lost two wickets for 14, but J. A. R. Oliver (56) and King, whose partnership realised 104, won the game without further loss.

BIG STAND FOLLOWS CHEAP WICKETS.
The County Club's match at Halleybury ended in a draw. The College batted first and the game opened sensationally, for Oliver got Chanter bowled off the first ball and had Souter caught at the wicket after three runs had been scored, but the third-wicket partnership realised 118, and Halleybury declared with 194 for 6. Northmet (St. Albans) beat Frogmore by eleven runs at Park Street, on Sunday. They made 86, of which R. Levin (26 not out) and J. Smith (4) contributed 50 in a last-wicket stand. O. Butler (21), B. Smith (15), D. P. Hollingsworth (10) made most of the runs previously. Wright (4-18) and J. England (4-34) did best with the ball for Frogmore, while L. Lee (25) batted well, but the rest of the side did badly until W. Lee (18) and A. Rimmonds (5 not out) put on 27 for the last wicket, which lifted the total to 75. F. Runchiman (5-17), L. Morley (3-18) and D. Hollingsworth (3-19) bowled steadily for Northmet.

K. A. GRIFFITHS BATS WELL FOR OLD BOYS.
The Old Albanians' match with Abbot's Langley, on Saturday, was left drawn. P. J. Glenister, R. G. Simons, A. W. Briers and F. Carlisle all batted well for Abbot's Langley, while K. A. Griffiths (61 not out) was in capital form for the Old Boys. His opening partnership with R. F. George (52) yielded 61. Two more wickets fell cheaply, but with G. F. Jarry (12 not out) he had added 49 in an unbroken fourth-wicket stand when the end came.
