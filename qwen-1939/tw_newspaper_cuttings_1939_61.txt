WEDNESDAY'S WIN ON SUNDAY!
Sandridge again disappointed their supporters on Sunday by losing their home game with Hadlett Wednesday by 44 runs.
Dismissed in ten overs for 32, no member of the Sandridge team reached double figures, Cautsin (9) and Roan (8) being top scorers. Hadlett scored 26 of the necessary runs before the first wicket fell, Sampson going on first change then secured 3 wickets for 6 runs, finishing up with 4 for 12. W. Lamb subsequently hit out, and he and Hemslcy added 21 for the last wicket. The fact that two Sandridge players one their principal run-getter and the other who was holding up his end nicely were run out had disastrous effect on the side, but the bowling of Brennan was exceptionally good, as his figures, 5—13, show. A SETBACK FOR ELSTREE.
Elstree lost unexpectedly to the Old Johnians on Saturday. E. Evans (6—32) was in great form with the ball and the Old Boys, who batted first, had a disastrous time. The first wicket fell at 11 and seven men were out for 14. P. Lumley (10) helped E. Keeton (36) to stop the rot, but the eighth wicket fell at 24. J. Stieb (15) and Keeton put on the total of 48 for 9, and J. Leach, who made one not out, kept his end up while Keeton lifted the total to 74 before being bowled by J. Milner.
W. H. Ridout (22) and J. Milner (10) put on 30 in an opening partnership for Elstree, but things went badly after that and five men were out for 36. A. Willshire (15) lifted the score to 54 for 6, but D. Kerans (6—37) and C. Leigh (4—17) got on top again and the innings closed for 62. Scores:-
