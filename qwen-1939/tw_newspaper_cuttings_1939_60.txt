ST. MICHAEL'S AND LONDON COLNEY DRAW.
St. Michael's, owing to holidays were weakly represented on Saturday for their match with London Colney at Verulamium.
Batting first, London Colney, who arrived late, declared with 105 for 4, S. Scales (62s) and G. Eames (48) contributing 69 in a second-wicket stand. St. Michael's without adequate time to set the runs, had no difficulty in saving the game, being 40 behind with eight wickets in hand when stumps were drawn.

RADLETT'S LAST PAIR FOIL HARPENDEN.
The return match between Radlett and Harpenden, at Cobden Hill, Radlett, on Saturday, was left drawn with Radlett failing hard to avert defeat, being 31 behind with one wicket to fall when time came.
Harpenden made 119 against good-length bowling by T. Down (3-53) and L. G. King (4-54). They were chiefly indebted to R. A. Day, who made 59 by patient batting, and D. C. L. Mole, who contributed 33.
Radlett had five wickets down for 21 and seven men out for 38, while the total had reached only 65 when the last pair—F. G. Taylor (11 not out) and F. Green (12 not out) came together. They defied all attempts to dislodge them and added 23. J. W. Bewell, who made 18, showed great promise for Radlett. R. A. Day, who took 5 wickets for 22 in the course of fourteen overs, of which six were maiden, did best with the ball for Harpenden, although W. H. Marsh (4-51) again bowled very steadily. Scores:

A TENSE FINISH AT
HARPENDEN.
In the second-team fixture Harpenden beat Radlett at Harpenden on Saturday by 16 runs.
Having dismissed Radlett for the small total of 77, Harpenden were given a good start by Hassell (18) and Pearce (16), but it appeared to be set for a comfortable win. The later batsmen failed, however, and it was left to Newman and Brock to save the match amid great excitement. With eight wickets down for 60 and nine for 72, the issue was in doubt when the last pair came together. Vociferous applause accompanied every run scored until the Radlett total was passed. All the Harpenden bowling was good, particularly West's, who had the excellent figures of 5 for 16. Scores:-
