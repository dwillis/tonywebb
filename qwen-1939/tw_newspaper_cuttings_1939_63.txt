SANDRIDGE BEAT REFEREES.
Two veterans figured prominently in a low-scoring game at Sandridge, where the Mid Herts Referees lost by 24 runs on Saturday.
Batting first the Referees were dismissed for 33, A. C. Sampson obtaining 4 for 8, Winch 2 for 6, Atkins 1 for 0 and Caustin 1 for 9. This score should have been easy for Sandridge, but they found Eames bowling his breaks with his old-time accuracy and seven wickets were down for 21. Caustin (57 not out) and Sampson, however, added 19 for the eighth wicket and Caustin afterwards hit out, obtaining 16 in one over, and the innings closed for 59. Clay (17) played a sound defensive innings for the Referees. Scores:-
