MONKS RISBORO’
IN FORM

Stokenchurch Find
Fielding Keen

AT WHITELEAF

AT Whiteleaf on Saturday Monks Risborough defeated Stokenchurch by 68 runs. Monks Risborough batted first and Hinton and Constable added 55 for their fourth wicket partnership. The pitch became more difficult and the other six wickets fell for 21 runs. A keen attack and some good catches proved too much for the Stokenchurch batsmen. Scores:
