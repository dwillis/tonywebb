STARRED SELECTIONS

WASHED out completely on Saturday and late starts almost everywhere on Monday—such is the sorry tale to record of the cricket weather for the August holiday week-end festival. Even some of the clubs with Sunday fixtures found their wickets unplayable after the previous day's soaking.
Cricket without sunshine is like Hamlet without the prince, or Australia without Bradman—it breaks down badly.
Of the few matches that were concluded, Hemel Hempstead brought off an innings victory over Wimbledon Crusaders, and Abbots Langley recorded a thumping success over a team of Wiltshire visitors.
West Herts completed the double over Harpenden, Leavesden, playing on matting, easily overcame Richmond Town, as did Kings Langley over Tunnel Sports Club.
Some of the fixtures arranged at holiday times against teams of unknown quality produce one-sided results, and nobody cares for that. It is obvious that Kings Langley, Hemel Hempstead and Abbots Langley were streets ahead of their visitors.
Once again this column urges holiday battles between the Langleys! While nobody reached the century mark, several went mighty close. The best individual efforts were:

"Observer" Awards for July

Bowling claims for "Observer" awards for July were better than ever, and it was an extremely difficult job to pick the best. Batting, perhaps due to the bad weather, was below par.
Competition for the awards is so keen that it is futile to send in claims reporting performances of second-rate merit. Only the very best get the awards.

YATES GETS 1,000 RUNS WITH "OBSERVER" BAT

On Sunday Don Yates, the Odhams and North Watford Conservatives prolific batsman, reached his thousand runs for the season. He has accomplished this feat in 23 innings, and has used an "Observer" prize-winning bat, which he won last year. "I have never had a better bat," he said yesterday. "The one I won last year drives beautifully, and I am certainly anxious to get another."
Perhaps that is why Yates put his best foot forward and won a bat for July. Results in another column.
