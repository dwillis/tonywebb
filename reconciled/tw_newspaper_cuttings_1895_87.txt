HOWICK v WARKWORTH.
At Howick, in beautiful weather, these teams met for
the third time this season. A good wicket had been pre-
pared and a pleasant game ensued, the only regrettable
feature of the match being an accident to W Shaw, one of
the visiting eleven, who, in running to take a catch, un-
fortunately slipped and received the ball full on his face,
the injury preventing him from taking any further part
in the game. Douglas was in fine form with the willow,
as were also Hope and Ainslie for the home team; while
G Green showed that he had not lost any of his hitting
power, and Wardle also did well for the visitors. Score:—

ELSWICK WORKS v. ROYAL GRAMMAR
SCHOOL.
These teams met on the ground of the Works Club at
Benwell on Saturday, in beautiful weather, before a good
attendance of interested spectators. The visitors batted
first, but, owing to the excellent bowling of Elsey and
Swinburne, were unable to put together more than 36
before being all dismissed, Bartram being the only one
to make anything like a stand. Elsey took seven wickets
for 15 runs, and Swinburne three for 14.
Cochrane and Spencer were the first pair of batsmen
for the home team, but the latter was clean bowled by
Spurgin when he had made 4, and the home captain
shared the same fate after having scored 9. Elsey,
Newitt, and George all batted well, and the innings closed
for the total of 113 runs. For the visitors Spurgin bowled
splendidly, taking eight wickets for 46 runs, which, when
the batting strength of the opposing team is considered,
is a highly creditable performance for this young bowler.

ROCKLIFF v. BENWELL.
Played at Whitley, on a perfect cricket. The home
team won the toss and started the innings with Murray
and Farmer, to the bowling of Hodgson and French.
Runs came slowly but surely until with the score stand-
ing at 41, Murray was bowled by Suttie, who had gone
on in place of Hodgson. F Latimer joined Farmer and
another good stand was made, Farmer eventually being
out to a catch at the wicket, having played a good
innings of 31. With E W Taylor in the score mounted
rapidly, the Rockliff captain batting freely from the start;
but he fell to a fine one-handed catch by Miller at third
man, having contributed 21. Shaw did not stay long,
his wicket falling with the score at 123; but with Dale and
Latimer together the score mounted steadily in spite of
several changes of bowling, and when it had reached 191
Taylor declared the innings closed, Latimer carrying out
his bat for a fine innings of 74 made in his best style.
With an hour and a half to play, Benwell started their
innings to the bowling of E W Taylor and Dale, but
made a bad beginning, Miller being bowled by the first
ball he received from Taylor, and May being shortly after
well caught in the long field off the same bowler, the
score standing at 8. At this point Milne and Ashmead
got together, and, starting steadily, carried the score on
until it looked as though they would play out time; but
at 68 Ashmead played a ball into his wicket. Milne con-
tinued to play fine cricket on being joined by Suttie, and
about 30 more runs had been added when "last over"
was called. Suttie received his last over, the third ball
of which he lifted clean out of the the field into the sea,
and at this point the match terminated in a draw, Milne
carrying out his bat for a faultless 75. Score:—
