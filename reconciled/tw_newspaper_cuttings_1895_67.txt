ROYAL CORNWALL GAZETTE: THURSDAY 5 SEPTEMBER 1895-2

CRICKET.
MADRON 2ND v. LELANT.
Played at Madron on Saturday, and won by the
home team. Scores: Madron 2nd, 35 (Mollard 13,
Nicholls 10); Lelant, 19.

FALMOUTH v. H.M.S. GANGES.
Played at Falmouth on August 29th. Score:
H.M.S. Ganges, 1st innings, 49; 2nd innings, 22 for
2 wickets. Falmouth, 142 for 5 wickets.

MORVAL v. ANTONY.
Result a win for Morval by 15. Jeffery bowled well, four wickets for 6 runs. Morval 37 (Poore 15.) Antony 22.

LELANT UNION JACKS v. PENZANCE WANDERERS.
Played at the Vicarage, Lelant, on Friday. Score:—Wanderers 30; second innings 71 (Phillips 10.) Union Jacks 11; second innings 55 (S. Millett 10.)

CORNWALL COUNTY ASYLUM v. ST. BREWARD.
Played at Cornwall County Asylum on 24th
August. Scores:—St. Breward 53 (Pote 11, Camp-
bell 19.) The Asylum 125 (Dennison 47, Winnan 12,
Adams 27.)

FALMOUTH v. HELSTON.
Played at Helston. An easy win for the visitors
on a bowler's wicket. Falmouth 101 (Vincent 29,
Tregellas 21, Wainwright 18, Whitley 11.) Helston
39.

ST. COLUMB v. ST. BLAZEY.
This match was played at St. Blazey on Wednes-
day, the home team winning. Score:—St. Columb
41 (Salmon, not out 23.) St. Blazey 45 (Brown 19,
Dunstan 11.)

PADSTOW v. POLSEATH VISITORS.
Played at Padstow on Wednesday, the home team
winning by 65 runs. Score:—Padstow 105 (Hoare 22,
E. H. Olver 45, Hollobone 16.) Polseath Visitors
40 (Martyn 25.)

TROON v. HELSTON.
Played at Helston on Friday. Score:—Troon 125
(Bennetts 15, R. Smith 18, Martin 45, Barnes 10.)
Helston 77 (Tyacke 16, Blackwell 13, Toy 23, Rogers
not out 14.)

GRAMPOUND v. LADOCK.
Played at Ladock on Saturday, resulting, after a pleasant game, in a victory for the visitors by 39 runs on the first innings. Scores : - Grampound, 61 (C. Penwarden 18, Croggon 14). Ladock, 22.

CAMBORNE v. MADRON.
At Camborne. Score:—Camborne 129 for 3 wickets
(Shand 18, Rogers 14, R. Smith not out 81, Bennetts
junr., 40, Holman not out 10.) Madron 38 (R.
Hichens 12); second innings 16.

NEWQUAY VISITORS v. PADSTOW.
Played at Newquay, and won by Padstow by 3
wickets and 67 runs. Padstow 137 for 7 wickets
(Hoare, not out 63, W. Kinscote, jun., 14, W. Kins-
cotte, sen., 21.) Newquay Visitors 70 (Wardle 12,
Hosking, not out 21.)

EXILES v. PENZANCE.
Played at Porthcurnow, ending in a decisive
victory for the Exiles by 209 runs on the first
innings. Score:—Exiles 280 (Michell 26, Spencer 35,
Stradling 42, Milward 44, Winfield 17, H. Ash 25,
Withers 24, J. Davis, not out, 23.) Penzance 51
(Jenkin 16, J. Rogers 12.)

FALMOUTH v. REDRUTH.
Played at Falmouth on Saturday. The late hour
at which the visitors arrived induced the Falmouth
captain to declare at 90, thus losing by 2 runs.
Score:—Falmouth 90 for 6 wickets (Major Ashley 11,
Vincent 34, Tregellas, not out 37.) Redruth 92
(Andrew 14, Setcher 23, Tamblyn 15, Sweet 11.)

DEVORAN v. H.M.S. GANGES.
Played at Mylor on Saturday, the home team being easy victors. The visitors went to the wickets first, and scored 54 runs, W. Pascoe making 19, and the Rev. A. Williams 11. H.M.S. Ganges scored 117 for three wickets, and declared the innings closed. In their second innings Devoran were all disposed of for 9 runs.

LAUNCESTON v. BUDE.
The return match between the above clubs was
played at Pennygillam on Saturday, and after a good
game resulted in a draw greatly in favour of Bude.
The gate was small, but above the average, the
takings being handed to Gunn, the club's pro-
fessional. Score:—Bude 149 (Johnson 42, Hornby
25, Dickinson 20, J. R. Maw 20.) Launceston 95
(Martyn 14, Oke 24, L. H. Arundell 14, Brown 11.)

TRURO v. ST. AUSTELL.
