NORTHAMPTON HERALD: SATURDAY 3 AUGUST 1895-3.

NORTHAMPTON TOWN LEAGUE.
FIRST SECTION.

Temperance v. St. Michael's.—This match was de-
cided on the Borough Ground on Saturday, the Tem-
perance winning very easily. Their score numbered
157, and St. Andrew's 62. The following batted very
well for the victors: W. Planer (50), W. J. Baker (28),
E. Mitchell (14), W. Crack (12), P. Jones (not out
11), B. C. Smith (11), and W. J. Ricks (10). In the
bowling section W. Planer took six for 19, P. Jones
one for 17, and G. Clarke three for 4. For the losers
C. Hazzard with 21 was the only double-figure scorer.
The chief trundlers were F. Cox (two for 55), W. East
(six for 54), and J. Rickard (two for 19).

Colts v. Clarence.—These teams met on the Borough
Ground on Saturday, and the Clarence had to suffer a
severe defeat at the hands of their opponents. The
winners' score totalled 130, the chief contributors being
A. Darlow (36), C. Wood (22), A. Mackness (27), and
F. Yeoman (15). The best bowling exhibitions were
given by J. Bridges (four for seven), J. Brown (four
for 16), and J. Clarke (one for 19). The Clarence, for
whom only nine men batted, were only able to put
together 44, towards which F. Ridley was the chief
scorer with 24. The trundlers were G. Eales (four
for 40), F. Ridley (two for 42), W. Vann (one for 19),
A. Tompkins (one for 15), and E. Moseley (one for 15).
Star v. St. Michael's.—This match was played on
the County Ground on Saturday afternoon. St. Michael's
batted first, and compiled 125, but the Star only re-
sponded with 65, therefore suffering a rather severe
defeat. For the winners S. Goodall played good
cricket for 53, W. H. Kingston 24, T. G. Beale 23, and
F. T. Tebbutt 11. In the bowling section T. G. Beale
took seven wickets for 35, W. Chapman one for 19, and
F. T. Tebbutt one for 10. For the Star W. T. Moring
played very well for 23 and G. Roberts for 20. The
bowlers were O. Squires (four for 45), W. T. Moring
(two for 15), G. Roberts (one for 15), and Cory (one
for 30).
Excelsior v. Enterprise.—Played on the County
Ground on Saturday. The Enterprise won the toss,
but the Excelsior batted first, and made 132 for six
wickets. Their best batsmen were J. Brown (42), S.
Wrigley (not out 42), J. M. Lucas (20), P. Cox (16 not
out), and H. Tall (10). For the same team G. J.
Thompson bowled exceedingly well, and took six for
28, and P. Kendall secured three for 27. The Enterprise
were all dismissed for 56, A. E. Coe (15) and W. Gregg
and Bailey (10) batting best. The bowlers were A. E.
Bradford (two for 37), Olney (one for 16), Gregg (one
for 17), Squires (one for 18), and Field (one for 42).

SECOND SECTION.

Teachers v. Doddridge.—Played on the Borough
Ground on Saturday afternoon, the result being an easy
win for the Teachers. The scores were—Teachers 87,
Doddridge 67. For the winners A. Brown scored 38,
J. Rose 37, W. Grant 23, and Nightingale 13. In the
bowling department G. M. Turner secured four wickets
for 30 runs, E. Dudley two for six, and F. Bull three
for five. The chief contributors to the Doddridge com-
pilation were W. Thurlby 16, J. Smith, C. Jones, and
A. Scotton 11 each. The bowlers were J. Smith (one
for 38), Howlett (one for 38), and C. Sutton, (two for

34).
Regent-square v. Victoria-road.—This match was
played on the Borough Ground on Saturday, and ended
in a win for Regent-square. Regent-square made 83,
towards which Alf. Nind was responsible for 25, not
out, and Arthur Nind 23, and A. Garratt 10. The
bowling for the winners was performed by W. Garratt
(six for 25) and W. Downing (four for 37). Victoria-
road compiled 64, W. Garratt being chief scorer with
17. F. Cooke (six for 35) and W. Neal (four for nine)
were the best bowlers.

Excelsior Albions v. Pioneer.—Played on the Borough
Ground on Saturday. The Pioneer made 78, and the
Excelsior 44. For the winners S. Wilkinson was re-
sponsible for a well-played 24, C. Richardson scored 20,
and H. Branson 14 not out. Stanford (five for 23) and
E. Battle (four for 21) did well in the bowling section.
The Excelsior's score totalled 44, towards which
Swannell was chief batsman with 13. The bowlers for
the defeated team were J. Copson (seven for 18), O.
Robinson (two for 16), and Tate (one for 14).

LONG BUCKBY v. NORTHAMPTON COUNTY CLUB AND GROUND.

Played at Long Buckby on Saturday last, and resulted
in an easy victory for the home team by 88 runs. The
principal scorers for the winners were T. Horton (52),
the Rev. W. P. Kingston (35), E. Scriven (20), the
Rev. C. H. Kingston (16), and W. Kennell (11, not
out), the total reaching 156. The County bowlers were
H. E. and C. A. Kingston, Bedford, and Smith. For
the County Club and Ground the chief scorers were O.
Hickson, P. Smith, Bedford, and C. A. and H. E.
Kingston, the total reaching only 68. For the winners
the bowlers were J. G. Bentley (five for 12), W. Ken-
nell (three for 18), and T. Horton (one for 13).

Kislingbury v. West End Rovers.—Played at Kisling-
bury, on Saturday, and won by Kislingbury.

Northampton Licensed Trades' C.C. v. Weedon Gar-
rison.—This match was played at Weedon on Satur-
day, and resulted in a victory for the visitors, whose
total reached 126 to the 61 made by their opponents.
The Weedon eleven, thanks to the fine bowling of the
veteran Joseph Potter, were dismissed for 61, Lieut.
Strong contributing 29 and Lieut. Haynes 10. On
the winning side the chief scorers were J. Lucas (48),
J. Potter (18), F. Goodyer (16), and M. Butterfield
(26). Lieutenants Strong and Harper each obtained
four wickets.

Dallington v. Star Reserve.—Played on the ground of
the former on Saturday, and resulted in a win for the
home team by 36 runs. Scores:—Dallington, 72; Star
Reserve, 36.

Woodford v. Grafton Underwood.—Played at Wood-
ford on Monday, and won by the visitors by 32 runs to
22.
