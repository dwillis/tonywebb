DUNSTABLE ADVERTISER: SATURDAY 17 AUGUST 1895-4.

LUTON VOLUNTEERS v. REST OF BATTALION.—A
match was played at Churn Camp last week between
teams representing the luton Detachment and the
remainder of 3rd Vol. Battalion, the Lutonians winning
by 28 runs. Scores :—Luton Detachment : Lieut. Green,
b Captain Eve, 10; Lieut. Lees, c Locker, b Supt.
Grey, 1; Pte R. Fox, b Pte Locker, 10; Pte J. Smith, b
Locker, 5; Captain Tibbs, b Private Locker, 4; Lieut.
G. P. Mills, h w, b Captain Eve, 2; Cyclist Waller,
st, b Private Locker, 0; Private Battams, c Clarke, b
Private Locker, 12; Private H. Cook, b Private Locker,
0; Supt. Morrison, b Private Locker, 3; Private
Draper, c Harlby, b Private Locker, 3; Private T.
Wren, not out, 3; extras, 8; total, 75.—
Remainder of Battalion : Colour-Sergeant Clarke, c
and b Cook 6; Sergeant Grey, b Supt. Morrison 2;
Private Sharrars, b Cook 13; Captain Eve, b Green
0; Private Lowe, c Wren b Green 6; Lieut. Du
Sautoy, b Green 1; Sergeant Thurley, b Green 1;
Private Jones, b Green 0; Private Gilbert, not out 8;
Lieut. Eve, stumped 5; Private Munn, b Green 0;
Private Allen, b Green 0; extras, 5; total, 47.

HOUGHTON v. WESTONING.—This match was played
at Houghton Green on Monday and ended in a
victory for the home team. Some excellent batting
was done by F. Love who carried his bat through for
40, and by A. Cook who was finally bowled for 37
runs. Score :—Westoning : F. Haylen, b Giddings
4; G. Crawley, c Holt b Robinson 7; A. Knutt, c sub
b Giddings 4; A. Muckleston, b Giddings 24; A. Neil,
l-b-w b Giddings 0; G. H. Treppe, b Giddings 1; M.
Judge, b Giddings 0; T. Summus, c Carter b Holt 7;
A. Stanley, not out 0; F. Summus, b Giddings 1; F.
Hobbs, b Robinson 0; extras, 4; total, 52. Houghton :
F. Love, not out 40; H. Thompson, b Muckleston 1;
J. Robinson, b Muckleston 4; G. Holt, c and b
Muckleston 4; T. Giddings, c Judge b Muckleston 0;
G. Cogswell, c and b Nutt 0; W. Carter, b Nutt 6;
F. Pratt, c Crawley b Nutt 1; G. Fowler, b Nutt 11;
H. Tompkins, b Nutt 1; A. Cook, b Treppe 37; extras,
15; total, 132.
