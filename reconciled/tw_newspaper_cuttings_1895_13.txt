READING OBSERVER: SATURDAY 25 MAY 1895-4.

CRICKET.

IMPORTANT TO SECRETARIES.—As in former years, we
readily grant a portion of our space for cricket match
reports, but we must impress upon secretaries the
importance of sending in the scores as soon as possible
after matches are played. We further desire to intimate
that no score can be inserted unless it is written legibly
and the innings set out in the form in which the matches
are published. Sheets for drawing up reports can be
obtained gratis on applying at the Observer Office.
Notices of forthcoming matches will be inserted at the
top of our cricket column at the rate of 1s. for each
insertion. Match cards, posters and bills of all kinds are
printed with the utmost despatch and on strictly reason-
able terms at the Observer Office.
