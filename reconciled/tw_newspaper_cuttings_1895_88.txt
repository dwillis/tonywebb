Winlaton v Greenside.—At Winlaton, and resulted in
a win for the home team. For the winners Blenkinsop
and A and H Tebb hit hard. Blenkinsop also bowled well
and took five wickets for nine runs. For Greenside
Armstrong batted well. Score:—Winlaton: J H Reed, b
Armstrong, 13; A Brownless, b Armstrong, 7; J Blenkin-
