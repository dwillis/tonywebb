HEATH END v. ST. LAURENCE'S (READING).—At Heath End
on Saturday, the result of a highly enjoyable game being an
easy win for Heath End by 85 runs. The batting of Maxwell,
White and W Hunt was exceedingly commendable, Maxwell's
innings of 72 runs being marred with two mistakes only. The
bowling of Maxwell and Roberts was superb, Roberts' analysis
reading seven overs, three maidens, nine runs and five wickets ;
and Maxwell's thirteen, four maidens, fourteen runs and three
wickets. After the match, through the hospitality of Mr Hunt,
the players partook of tea on the lawn. At the conclusion of the
meal the captain of the home club (Mr E J Hunt), presented Mr
H Roberts with a cap in recognition of his recent performance of
the hat trick. Mr Hunt referred in eulogistic terms to the excellent
services rendered by Mr Roberts, who, in reply, thanked the
club for their kindness. Score:—
